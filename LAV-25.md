LAV = "Light Armored Vehicle"
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LAV-25
In Service: 1983-Present

An eight-wheeled amphibious armored reconnaissance vehicle built by [[General Dynamics]] and used by both [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] and [[United States Army|Army]].

![[Pasted image 20260704100732.png]]

Comparison with the [[ACV-30]]: They're both 8x8 wheeled combat platforms, but the LAV-25 is a light, agile reconnaissance vehicle, while the ACV-30 is a massive, heavily armored Infantry Fighting Vehicle.
