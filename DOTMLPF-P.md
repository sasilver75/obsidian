Pron. "Dot-Mill-P-F"

A comprehensive analysis framework used by the US [[Department of Defense|DoD]] to identify capability gaps and determine the best solutions to resolve them.

It stands for:
- ==Doctrine==: The fundamental principles and established guidance that govern how military forces are employed.
- ==Organization==: The structural design and authorized staffing of units and forces.
- ==Training==: The methods and programs used to prepare individual and units for combat and operations.
- ==Materiel==: The physical equipment/weapon systems/supplies needed to effectively equip forces.
- ==Leadership and Education== : The professional development, education, and training required for leaders at all levels.
- ==Personnel==: The availability of trained ,military, civilian, and contractor personnel to fill capability requirements.
- ==Facilities==: The overarching departmental or national regulations, laws, and directives that dictate operations.
- ==Policy==: The overarching departmental rules, directives, legislative constraints that govern operations and acquisitions.

The framework is a critical step in the [[Joint Capabilities Integration and Development System]] (JCIDS), and helps military planners decide whether a capability gap requires a non-material change, a materiel change, or a combination of both.

If an analysis reveals that a gap can be fixed by altering how the military trains/leads/organized without needing new equipment, a DOTMLPF-P Change Recommendation (DCR) is submitted. If the analysis proves that new equipment or technology is required to achieve the mission, a materiel solution is recommended, formally launching an acquisition program.
