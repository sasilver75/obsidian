Specialized Combatant Command Directorates (e.g. USSOUTHCOM or USSOCOM use additional codes)
- J9 Officer: Military Operations, Interagency Cooperation
- J10 Officer: Finance, Business Operations
- J35 Officer: Future Operations


==Aside==: When you see a two-digit or three-digit staff code, the first number tells you the overarching directorate, and the second number identifies the precise department or branch.
- Under J3 Operations
	- J33 handles Current Operations
	- J35 handles Future Operations
- Under J2 Intelligence
	- J21 is often the Targeting or Watch branch
