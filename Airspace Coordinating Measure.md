ACMs are divided into specific functional categories based on their design and operational intent. Each category ("type") contains several specific sub-measures ("usages"):
1. Air Defense Area (ADAREA)
	- [[Weapon Engagement Zone]]s (WEZ)
	- [[Fighter Engagement Zone]]s (FEZ)
	- [[Missile Engagement Zone]]s (MEZ)
2. Air Defense Measure (ADM)
	- Air Defense Identification Zones (ADIZ)
	- Localized tactical weapon firing lanes designed to intercept incoming threats.
3. Airspace Control Area
	- Airspace Control Sectors
	- [[High-Density Airspace Control Zone]] (HIDACZ)
4. Air Corridor/Route (AIRCOR)
	- Minimum-Risk Routes (MRR)
	- Transit Corridors (TC)
	- Standard Use Army Aircraft Flight Routes (SAAFR)
5. Restricted Operating Zone (ROZ)
	- [[Air-to-Air Refueling Area]] (AAR)
	- [[Combat Air Patrol]] (CAP) anchors
	- Unmanned Aircraft Areas (UA)
	- Drop Zones (DZ)
	- [[Close Air Support]] (CAS) wheels
6. Reference Cover/Points (Ref Point)
	- Air alignment point, [[Bullseye]] markers, and common reference coordinates designed to achieve maximum spatial awareness.
7. Stand-Alone Measures
	- [[Coordinating Altitude]] (CA)
	- [[Coordination Level]] (CL)
	- [[No Fly Area]] (NOFLY)
	- [[Airspace Coordination Area]] (ACA)


Key Airspace Coordinating measures in an [[Airspace Control Order]]:
- [[Airspace Coordination Area]] (ACA): A 3D bock of target-area airspace protecting friendly aircraft from surface-to-surface fires.
- [[Restricted Operating Zone]] (ROZ): Reserved airspace for specific operations or restricted due to high threat/hazard levels.
- [[High-Density Airspace Control Zone]] (HIDACZ): Designated volume with concentrated, highly-varied weapons and aircraft use.
- [[Coordinating Altitude]] (CA): An altitude separating different types of users (e.g. high-flying fixed-wing versus low-flying rotary-wing assets.
- [[Coordination Level]] (CL): The floor altitude below which fixed-wing aircraft typically don't operate without clearance.
