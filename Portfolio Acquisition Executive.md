The [[United States Army]] in 2025 ((?)) did a sweeping transformation of its acquisition process to accelerate delivery of critical warfighting capabilities to soldiers.
- ((What was it like before?))

The most significant change is the creation of six Portfolio Acquisition Executives, each of which owns a comprehensive capability area:
- ==Fires== (Redstone Arsenal, AL)
	- Manages missiles, space, and howitzer systems
- ==Maneuver Ground== (Fort Benning, GA)
	- Manages soldier equipment and ground combat systems
- ==Maneuver Air== (Fort Rucker, AL)
	- Manages aviation platforms, survivability, and autonomy
- ==Command and Control and Counter Command and Control== (Aberdeen Proving Ground, MD)
	- Manages networks, intelligence, sensors, and electronic warfare
- ==Agile Sustainment and Ammo== (Picatinny, NJ)
	- Manages armament, ammunition, and support equipment
- ==Layered Protection and CBRND== (Fort Leonard Wood, MO)
	- Chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear defense, along with terrestrial sensors

Each PAE places a single two-star general or civilian equivalent in charge of every major function for their assigned capability.


> "When you start aligning by capability rather than siloes, you will get a better outcome. With PAEs, we can think by capability (meaning *outcome*)..."
