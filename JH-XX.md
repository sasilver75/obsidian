https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JH-XX

A designation applied to American intelligence agencies to a regional bomber/tactical bomber/fighter bomber.

The second stealth bomber of China confirmed in existence by US intel community after the [[H-20]]; the Pentagon speculates the fighter-bomber is capable of long-range strikes and nuclear weapon delivery.

In December 2024, social media photos suggested that Chengdu Aircraft Corporation (CAC) has publicly flown a prototype aircraft in Chengdu, tentatively designated as the "J-36" by military analysts.
