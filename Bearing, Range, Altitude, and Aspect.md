BRAA calls are specific to one aircraft. To pass the location of a single target to three planes on a [[Combat Air Patrol|CAP]], three separate BRAA calls would be needed; this is why [[Bullseye]] is the primary method; only one radio call has to be made to get everyone on the same page.

Still, a BRAA call is mandatory in some situations:
- If the information is only for one aircraft
- Or is a direct response to a BRAA request
	- ==BOGEY DOPE== is a request for information on indicated or closest GROUP in BRAA format.
- When a controller is reporting a threat. This happens when an untargeted hostile/bandit/bogey is within a briefed range of a friendly aircraft.
	- "Showtime 11, LEad Group, Threat BRAA 270/55, Thirty-Nine Thousand, Flank Northeast, Hostile"
	- Now Showtime 11 knows that there's a threat directly West, at 39,000 feet, 55 miles away, flying Northeast and showing its flank, and confirmed hostile.
		- Since this information is relevant to Showtime, the BRAA format is used, instead of Bullseye.
