https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loitering_munition

A type of self-destructive [[Unmanned Aerial System|UAV]] equipped with a warhead that is designed to remotely loiter by a *human operator* until a target is designated, then crash into it and detonate.

Emerged in the 1980s for the [[Suppression of Enemy Air Defenses|SEAD]] role (e.g. using anti-radar/radiation sensing), but by the 2000s they were developed for additional roles, from long-range strike and fire support to short-range tactical systems that fit in a backpack.
