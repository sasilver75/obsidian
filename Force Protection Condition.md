A progressive, defensive alert system used by the [[Department of Defense|DoD]] to standardize security measures and responses to potential terrorist threats against military *personnel, facilities, and assets*.

The system consists of five ascending levels of security measures. Higher levels trigger strict access controls, including mandatory 100% ID card checks, vehicle inspections, lane closures, or restrictions limiting base access strictly to mission-essential personnel.

FPCON Levels:
- ==FPCON Normal==: Routine security posture applied only when a general, global threat of possible terrorist activity exists.
- ==FPCON Alpha==: Increased general threat of possible terrorist activity whose nature and extent are unpredictable.
- ==FPCON Bravo==: Increased and more predictable threat of terrorist activity directed against Department of Defense elements.
- ==FPCON Charlie==: Intelligence indicates that targeting or some form of terrorist action against personnel or facilities is likely.
- ==FPCON Delta==: A terrorist attack has occurred in the immediate area, or intelligence confirms an imminent attack on a specific location.

> Since September 11, 2001, the baseline FPCON level across most military installations has generally remained at Bravo or higher.
