The critical area of performance for aircraft detection systems, defined by the distance at which the radar beam rises enough above the Earth's surface to make detection of a target at the lowest level possible.

It's associated with the low elevation region of performance, and its geometry depends on terrain, radar heart, and signal processing.

This concept is associated with the notions of:
- [[Radar Shadow]]
- [[Clutter Zone]]
- [[Clear Zone]]

The Radar Horizon and Clutter Zone to avoid radar detection by using a technique called [[Nap-of-the-Earth]] navigation.
