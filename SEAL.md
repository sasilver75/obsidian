SEAL = "Sea, Air, and Land"

Part of [[US Special Operations Command|SOCOM]]'s [[Naval Special Warfare Command]]

[[US Special Operations Command|SOCOM]]'s force of choice for small-unit maritime military operations which originate from, and return to a river, ocean, swamp, delta, or coastline.

This littoral capability is considered more important now than ever, as half the world's infrastructure and population is located within one mile of an ocean or river.

There are 8 "Vanilla" Seal Teams:
- Naval Special Warfare Group 1 (Coronado, CA), focusing on Pacific/Indo-Pacific region

	- Seal Team 7
- Naval Special Warfare Group 2 (Little Creek, VA), focusing on European and African areas
