---
A doctrinal measure established by land or amphibious commanders to delineate coordination requirements for joint  surface attacks.
It expedites the engagement of any [[Time Sensitive Target|Target of Opportunity]] beyond the line without prior coordination with the ground commander.

==It allows commanders to freely attack targets behind the line while protecting friendly forces, close-in operations, and operations short of the line==.

Attacks *beyond* the FSCL can occur without coordination with the ground commander.
Attacks *short* of the FSCL require coordination with the appropriate ground commander.
