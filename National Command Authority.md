Refers to the ultimate lawful source of military orders, most notably defined as the [[President of the United States]] and the [[Secretary of Defense|SECDEF]], or their duly deputized alternates and successors.

> "The term has no statutory or constitutional basis and was replaced in 2002 in favor of explicitly referring to the president and/or the secretary of defense."

> "The term also refers to communications with the commanding officers of the unified combatant commands to put U.S. forces into action."
