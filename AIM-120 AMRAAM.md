The AIM-120D (also known as the AIM-120D-3)  is the latest operational variant of the US military's Beyond-Visual-Range (BVR) AMRAAM missile, featuring [[GPS INS]] guidance, electronic warfare resilience, and an estimated range of over 100 miles, it is deployed across US stealth and multirole fighters.
