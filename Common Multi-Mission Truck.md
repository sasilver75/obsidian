==An experimental program/demonstrator as of 2025==

The Common Multi-Mission Truck (CMMT, "Comet") is a family of compact, low-cost air vehicles for a wide range of missions, compliant with the [[Weapon Open System Architecture]] requirements, enabling rapid upgrades while avoiding vendor lock.

CMMT comes with two configurations:
1. CMMT-D (Demonstrator): An air-launched variant that deploys via US Air Force airlifters, fighters, and bombers to put affordable mass on target.
2. CMMT-X (Experimental): A smaller long-range launched effect that deploys from rotary-wing platforms.

> "We are flying CMMT air vehicles with two configurations this year: A variant that deploys via airlifters like the C-130 and C-17 to put affordable mass on target, and a shorter long-range launched effect that deploys from helicopters. The former variant, which can also deploy from fighters and bombers, has a range exceeding 500 nautical miles. The CMMT is 96 inches long and can fit inside the F-35’s internal weapons bay. Extra fuel modules can be added to increase the CMMT’s range, but will also increase its length."

> "Unit price depends greatly on performance and production requirements that our customers have not yet shared. We understand that the U.S. Air Force is pricing its Franklin missile at $150,000 for the basic air vehicle, and we plan to be competitive on that front. Should the Pentagon procure the system, Lockheed anticipates it could scale production to 2,500 units per year or more."

Above all else, CMMT is a manufacturing program at Lockheed; it must be 100 percent producible, low-cost, and scalable first, and then integrate performance-enhancing and mission-specific capabilities as use cases and threats emerge.
