The specific engagement envelope around a target where an air-to-air missile retains enough kinetic energy and speed that the targeted aircraft cannot evade the weapon, even if the pilot is aware of the threat and takes immediate, evasive maneuvers.
- Note that the target can still use countermeasures, etc.

Outside the NEZ, a fighter can potentially outrun a missile or drain its energy through high-G turns so that it falls short.

The NEZ size/shape is defined by the missile's propulsion, aerodynamic performance, and seeker capabilities, and dynamically changes based on the target's altitude, the aircraft's launch speed, and atmospheric conditions.
