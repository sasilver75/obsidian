Pron. "Decide" ((surely? unconfirmed))

The program of record for [[Collateral Damage Estimation]].
- CDE is the holistic process of determining the potential for collateral damage resulting from target engagement
- While it can be calculated manually, automated tools help identify undesired consequences and hazards posed by weapons effects and aid in making recommendations to mitigate those effects in compliance with [[Rules of Engagement|ROE]] and [[Law of War]].
