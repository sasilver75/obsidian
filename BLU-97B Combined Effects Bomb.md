The submunition used in several cluster bomb type weapon systems, mainly the CBU-87 and its precision-guided vresion the CBU-103.

Bomblets separate from the main bomb and independently free-fall to the ground; an inflatable bag slows them down and spreads them out. Once bomblets reach a force of 6 Gs, they arm themselves. They have a combined [[Shaped Charge]], fragmentation, and incendiary effect on the target.

==5% (but as high as 17%) dud rate; fitted with an extremely sensitive fuze, and clearing this [[Unexploded Ordnance|UXO]] was very dangerous; seven combat engineers died on one occasion.==
