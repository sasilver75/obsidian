A 2,000 lb hardened-penetrator weapon designed to destroy fortified underground bunkers and reinforced concrete facilities. Features a heavy steel casing allowing it to penetrate up to 6 feet of concrete before a delayed-action fuse triggers internal explosive payload.

Similar to the [[Mk 84]] in the sense that the BLU-series are partially relevant for the sake that they're equipped with [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|JDAM]] guidance kits.
