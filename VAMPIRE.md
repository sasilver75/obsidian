VAMPIRE = "Vehicle Agnostic Modular Palletized ISR Rocket Equipment"

From [[L3Harris]], vampire is a self-contained, low-cost, multi-mission precision-guided weapons platform... a compact [[Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance|ISR]] and [[Counter-Unmanned Aerial System|C-UAS]] weapons system (typically [[Group 3 UAS|Group 3]]) designed to deploy on nearly any platform, vehicle, or vessel.

Has been used in combat operations since 2023.
- Washington ordered 14 VAMPIRE systems for Ukraine in 2023, and followed up with additional orders in 2025.
- [[United States Army|Army]] has tapped L3Harris for a $106M contract in 2026.

The WESCAM MX-10D RSTA sensor system provides ISR overmatch; can be equipped with the [[Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System|APKWS]] or other laser-guided munitions.
- The WESCAM MX-10D RSTA is an [[Optical|EO]] [[Infrared|IR]] stabilized targeting system mounted on a telescopic mast, providing ISR overmatch with fully digital, high-definition extended-range ISR and laser target designation.

VAMPIRE uses the Widow Mission Management System, which is Forward Area Air Defense Command and Control (FAADC2) compliant, and features a modular plug-in design for quick addition of sensors, effectors, radio management, navigation, and moving maps.
