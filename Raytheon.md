Now called RTX, after the merger of United Technologies and Raytheon.


RTX has the following subsidiary/business units:
- Collins Aerospace: Makes aviation electronics, communications systems, engine nacelles, seating
- Raytheon: Precision weapon systems like the [[MIM-104 Patriot|Patriot]] and [[AIM-120 AMRAAM]], along with advanced [[ANSPY-6|AN/SPY-6]] naval radars.
- Pratt & Whitney: Celebrated for manufacturing the propulsion system for F-35 and fuel-efficient engine families for commercial airliners.
