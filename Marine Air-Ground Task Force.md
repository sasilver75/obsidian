Pronounced"Magtaff"

The [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] standard task-organized combined-arms force, integrating command, ground combat, aviation, and logistics under one Marine commander, so that it can fight or respond as an integrated team.

Typically has four elements:
- Command Element
- Ground Combat Element
- Aviation Combat Element
- Logistics Combat Element

MAGTFs are built around specific missions, and exact units change depending on what the commander needs. A humanitarian assistance MAGTF might emphasize helicopters, engineers, medical support, and logistics, while a combat MAGTF might emphasize infantry, fires, air support, reconnaissance, and air defense.

MAGTF come in three sizes, increasing in size:
- [[Marine Expeditionary Unit]] (MEU)
- [[Marine Expeditionary Brigade]] (MEB)
- [[Marine Expeditionary Force]] (MEF)
