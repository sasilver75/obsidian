https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobility_kill

Damaging a platform to a sufficient degree such that it cannot move.

In anti-tank warfare, a vehicle that is immobilized due to damage or anti-tank obstacles, or the act of immobilizing such a target through damage or anti-tank obstacles.

Can be caused by anti-tank mines, RPGs, ATGMs, and other direct and indirect fire rounds, or through anti-tank obstacles like dragon's teeth, anti-tank ditches, Czech hedgehogs.

In rare cases, tanks that have suffered mobility kills have continued to engage enemy targets, even though they are immobile. Immobile tanks are vulnerable for ground-attack planes, drones, etc.
