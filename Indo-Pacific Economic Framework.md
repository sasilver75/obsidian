https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Pacific_Economic_Framework

An economic initiative launched by Joe Biden in 2022, launching with fourteen founding member nations in the Indo-Pacific region with an open invitation for other countries to join.

The earlier [[Trans-Pacific Partnership]] (US withdrew under Trump) was pitched by Obama as a means to promote American economic interests in the region, in the face of a rising china.
