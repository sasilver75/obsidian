Refers to high-risk, high-reward government-funded research agencies focusing on breakthrough technologies.

Programs are led by empowered project managers who serve limited terms (typically 3-5 years) to maintain fresh perspectives. Projects aim for game-changing capabilities rather than safe, incremental improvements.

Core agencies:
- [[Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency]] (DARPA)
- [[Intelligence Advanced Research Projects Activity]] (IARPA)
- [[Advanced Research Projects Agency - Energy]] (ARPA-E)
- [[Homeland Security Advanced Research Projects Agency]] (HSARPA)
- [[Advanced Research Projects AGency for Health]] (ARPA-H)
