# Aside: [[Beyond Visual Range]] vs [[Beyond Visual Line of Sight]]
- Distinct terms used in air combat and uncrewed aviation, respectively.
- BVR refers to engaging targets out of human sight, using long-range sensors and weapons.
- BVLOS/BLOS refers to operating a remote vehicle entirely out of the pilot's direct vision.
