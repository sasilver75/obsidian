QUICKSINK is a low-cost method for neutralizing surface vessels using a modified, air-delivered munition.

A modified [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|JDAM]] from the [[United States Air Force|USAF]], used as a means of bolstering its capacity to launch strikes on enemy ships.
- ((2000 lb bomb, I believe?))

In addition to the typical [[GPS INS]] navigation of a JDAM, New imaging infrared seeker attached to the nose, which gives the bomb the ability to engage moving targets, even in adverse conditions.

Though unpowered, standard JDAMs can glide out to targets around 15 miles away, depending on the weapon’s exact configuration and release envelope.
