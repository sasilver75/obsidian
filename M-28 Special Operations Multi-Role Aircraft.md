A top-tier platform for [[Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance|ISR]], [[CASEVAC]], cargo carry/drop, and troop transport. Has a [[Short Takeoff and Landing|STOL]] capability and a rugged/versatile airframe for austere operations in extreme environments.

Developed jointly by PZL Mielec (a [[Lockheed Martin]] company) and [[Sierra Nevada Corporation]] (SNC), it is marketed in the US as the MC-145B Wily Coyote.
