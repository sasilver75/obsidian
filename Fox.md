Short for "foxtrot," Fox is used as a brevity code used by NATO pilots to signal the simulated or actual release of an air-to-air munition or other combat function.

Announcing release of a weapon is intended to help avoid friendly fire, alerting other pilots to avoid maneuvering into the path of the munition.

==Three variations:==
- Fox One: Indicates launch of a [[Missile Guidance|Semi-Active Radar Homing]] missile (e.g. [[AIM-7 Sparrow]])
- Fox Two: Indicates launch of an infrared homing missile (e.g. [[AIM-9 Sidewinder]])
- Fox Three: Indicates launch of an [[Missile Guidance|Active Radar Homing]] missile (e.g. [[AIM-120 AMRAAM]] )

"Fox four" was used in the past to refer to the use of guns or cannon (which was previously Fox three, prior to the advent of active radar homing missiles), but that was obsoleted in favor of the callout phrase "guns-guns-guns".
