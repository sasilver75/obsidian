- Tasked Unit
- ICAO: KLSV for the air field they're on
- Mission data line: Name of mission (AA01) with those letters indicating day of operation. AA=1, AB=2, etc. 01 means first mission of the day.
- EAGLE21 is the flight's call sign.
- "-" is a field left blank
- OCA = Offensive counter air
- Deploy and Arrives back at Nellis AFB
- Mission Aircraft is 2x [[F-15E Strike Eagle]], and the numbers are letter/code for what each will be carrying, weapons wise.
	- This listing is called the SCL, the [[Standard Conventional Load]].
- Includes time information
- DMPIID = DMPI ID, [[Designated Mean Point of Impact]], meaning a geographic spot in the *middle* of your targets. This gives the aircrew to look at one location, but the freedom to target things as they see fit. Has a northing (N) and westing coordinate (W) and an elevation above sea level.
- Also some contact information for the controller for the mission, radio frequencies and a call sign, as well as a telephone number if the air crew wants to speak with them before the mission starts.
- In this fragment, we have just one target, but it's not uncommon for a secondary target to be added in case the primary can't be serviced for some reason. This is sometimes called a [[Dump Target]].

Q: Why so cryptic?
A: Because ATOs originated in an era when the order had to be transmitted over a low-bandwidth secure connection, or printed out and hand-carried to the wings (if they didn't have the infrastructure for secure communications). Minimizing the amount of space it took up made it easier for couriers to take, and eased the load on the limited secure connections at the time.
