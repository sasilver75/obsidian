https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stationary_target_indication

A mode of operation of [[Radar]] that enables the operator to discriminate between the target and [[Clutter Zone|Clutter]].

In contrast to [[Moving Target Indication]] (MTI), it cannot take advantage of the fact that the target moves with respect to clutter, so it must exploit some intrinsic characteristics of the target which are different from those of clutter.
