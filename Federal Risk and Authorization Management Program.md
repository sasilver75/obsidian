A US government program providing a standardized approach to security assessment, certification, and continuous monitoring for cloud products and services. It eliminates duplicate agency efforts so that we have a "do once, use many times" framework based on NIST standards.

The goal is to accelerate the adoption of secure, modern cloud solutions across federal agencies.

Replaces repetitive agency security reviews with a single process managed by the General Services Administration.
