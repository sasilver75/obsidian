https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_assessment

Evaluates the effectiveness of military force during operations to determine whether objectives have been achieved, and what additional actions may be required.

The final phase in the [[Joint Targeting Cycle]], providing feedback to inform subsequent planning/targeting.

Doctrine typically says that it has the following components to inform reattack recommendations:
1. [[Battle Damage Assessment]] (BDA): An evaluation of the physical and functional damage inflicted on a target and its related systems by lethal or nonlethal military action.
2. [[Munitions Effectiveness Assessment]] (MEA): An analysis conducted alongside battle damage assessment that evaluates the performance of weapon systems and munitions and may inform adjustments to tactics, methods, or weapon employment.
3. [[Collateral Damage Assessment]] (CDA): A review of unintended damage, typically outside the target boundary, or to collateral concerns within it.
