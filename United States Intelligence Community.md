The [[Office of the Director of National Intelligence]] (ODNI) is an independent US government agency responsible for overseeing and integrating the 18 agencies of the intelligence community:
- [[Central Intelligence Agency]] (CIA) (Independent Agency)
- [[Bureau of Intelligence and Research]] (INR) (Department of State)
- [[Office of Naval Intelligence]] (ONI) (US Navy)
- [[Sixteenth Air Force]] (16AF) (US Air Force)
- [[Military Intelligence Corps]] (MIC) (US Army)
- [[Marine Corps Intelligence]] (MCI) (US Marine Corps)
- [[National Space Intelligence Center]] (NSIC) (US Space Force)
- [[National Security Agency]] (NSA) (Department of Defense) and [[Central Security Service]] (CSS) (Department of Defense)
- [[National Reconnaissance Office]] (NRO) (Department of Defense)
- [[Defense Intelligence Agency]] (DIA) (Department of Defense)
- [[National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency]] (NGA) (Department of Defense)
- [[Office of Intelligence and Counterintelligence]] (OICI) (Department of Energy)
- [[Office of Intelligence and Analysis (Treasury)]] (OIA) (Department of the Treasury)
- [[Intelligence Branch]] (IB) (Federal Bureau of Investigation)
- [[National Security Branch]] (NSB) (Federal Bureau of Investigation)
- [[Office of National Security Intelligence]] (ONSI) (Drug Enforcement Administration)
- [[Coast Guard Intelligence]] (CGI) (US Coast Guard)
- [[Office of Intelligence and Analysis (Homeland Security)]] (I&A) (Department of Homeland Security)
