The [[United States Navy|Navy]]'s leading provider of maritime intelligence to both the Navy and to joint warfighting forces, as well as national decision-makers and other consumers in the [[United States Intelligence Community]]. 

ONI has five Centers of Excellence, each with a distinct command.

It also has the Farragut Technical Analysis Center, which conducts strategic scientific and technical intelligence analysis of foreign technologies, sensors, weapons, platforms, combat systems, [[Command, Control, Communications, Computers, Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance|C4ISR]], and cyber capabilities.
