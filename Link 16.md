https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Link_16

A secure, jam-resistant high-capacity military tactical data network used by the US, [[North Atlantic Treaty Organization|NATO]], and allied forces to share real-time tactical pictures, targeting data, and digital voice messages.
- Specified in [[Link 16|STANAG 5516]]: Tactical Data Exchange, specifying the protocols, conventions, and J-series message formats used by allied aircraft, ships, and ground forces to share near-real-time tactical data.
- A J-series message are standardized, bit-packed binary data formats used by Link 16 and TADIL-J systems to share real-time tactical information, such as air tracks, surveillance, and weapons coordination.
	- ==[TADIL-J](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TADIL-J)==: Refers to the standardized ==J-series message== formats used in the Link 16 tactical data link network, defined by [[Link 16|MIL-STD-6016]] and STANAG 5516.
