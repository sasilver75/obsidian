Part of [[US Special Operations Command|SOCOM]]'s [[Naval Special Warfare Command]] 

The exclusive mission of SWCC operators is to expertly drive and provide large-caliber gunfire support on specialized, high-tech, high-speed, and low-profile Surface Combatant Craft to secretly infiltrate and exfiltrate [[SEAL]]s on special operations worldwide.
