A new [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] structure as of 2024-2026 or so.

The A-Staff structure is organized into six core directorates:
- [[A1 Officer]]: Manpower, Personnel, and Services
- [[A2 Officer]]: Intelligence
- [[A3 Officer]]: Operations
- [[A4 Officer]]: Logistics and Engineering
- [[A5 Officer]]: Plans and Integration
- [[A6 Officer]]: Communications (and Cyber)
