
A [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] prototyping initiative designed to replace the aging [[Medium Tactical Vehicle Replacement|MTVR]] fleet.
Currently spearheaded by Mack Defense, focuses on modular 4x4 and 6x6 hybrid-electric trucks.

==Not in service.== The Corps is currently evaluating MTT prototypes at the Nevada Automotive Test Center to inform final requirements. Scheduled to complete prototype testing in early FY2027, with production decisions to follow.
