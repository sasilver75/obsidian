The Joint AI Center (JAIC) was an American [[Department of Defense|DoD]] organization exploring use of AI in combat. In February 2022, JAIC was integrated into the Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office (CDAO).

The goal is to use AI to solve large and complex problem sets that span multiple combat systems.
