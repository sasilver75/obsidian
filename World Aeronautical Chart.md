Small-scale aviation maps (drawn at a 1:1,000,000 scale; 1 inch equals ~16 miles) covering large land areas for visual navigation.

Discontinued by the FAA in 2015 as pilots shifted to digital maps.



==WAC Locator==: A regional index map or grid system used to identify, index, and locate specific sheets within the standard World Aeronautical Chart series. A locator helps pilots quickly find the exact chart number needed for a specific geographic region.
