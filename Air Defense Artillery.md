The [[United States Army]] Air Defense Artillery (ADA) is the branch responsible for protecting friendly forces and critical infrastructure from aerial attacks, missiles, and unmanned aircraft. They operate advanced systems like the [[MIM-104 Patriot|Patriot]], [[Terminal High Altitude Area Defense|THAAD]], [[ANTWQ-1 Avenger|AN/TWQ-1 Avenger]], [[Maneuver Short-Range Air Defense|Sgt Stout]] and [[FIM-92 Stinger]], [[Phalanx CIWS]], and [[ANMPQ-64 Sentinel|AN/MPQ-64 Sentinel]] radar.
