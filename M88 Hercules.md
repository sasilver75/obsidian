Hercules = "Heavy Equipment Recovery Combat Utility Lift Evacuation System"

One of the largest armored recovery vehicles in use by the US Armed Forces. Can be used to tow/lift/rescue heavy 70-ton combat vehicles like the [[M1A2 Abrams]], even.

Designed by [[BAE Systems]]
