A long-range active radar homing air-to-air missile developed by the [[People's Liberation Army|PLA]] in service of the [[PLA Air Force|PLAAF]] and [[PLA Navy|PLAN]]

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PL-15

In service since 2016; The impetus behind the American's [[AIM-260 JATM]] program in the first place, since it exceeded the range of the [[AIM-120 AMRAAM|AIM-120D]]. Since then, the PL-15 has been eclipsed by the much larger [[PL-17]], which stretches larger even than the [[AIM-174]] (or Russian R-37M).
