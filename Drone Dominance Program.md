# Gauntlet Two
- Gauntlet 2 is meant to involve substantially more complex scenarios than the first Gauntlet competition held in February 2026. and will result in twice as many drones orders, and this time features separate competitions for long-range strike drones and compact "close-quarter" UAVs optimized for performance in more confined terrain, including indoors or underground.
- The qualifying event involved warhead-less drones operated by vendor pilots performing both long-range and close-quarters strike missions, ramming dummy personnel targets inside compounds, impacting a moving [[HMMWV|Humvee]], flying in day and night and inclement weather conditions, and crashing attempting to enter buildings.
	- One more ordeal could potentially wean down the 19 qualifiers before the event.  120 of each finalist UAS will be operated by US military personnel who receive one week of training before trialing the quadcopters in complex scenarios under conditions of adversary [[Electronic Warfare]].
- Based on score derived from operator evaluations and observed outcomes, DDP plans to select three top-performing close-quarters UASs and five long-range UASs to receive production contracts totaling 60,000 drones between them, each costing no more than $4,500 (short range) or $5,500 (long range) while adhering to even stricter standards barring Chinese components (than in Gauntlet 1)
- Of the 19 qualifiers, seven were winners of Gauntlet Phase 1:
	- Ascent Aerosystems
	- Auterion
	- Griffon Aerospace
	- Modal AI
	- [[Neros Technologies]]
	- Skycutter
	- Ukrainian Defense Drones Tech Corp


> Qualifying Event for Gauntlet II: 49 companies offered 79 drone designs; DDP qualified only 19 companies for the final Gauntlet 2 trial taking place August 2026 at Fort Carson, Colorado
