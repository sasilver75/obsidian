These are the people on loan from service chiefs that are deployed at a deployed location. It's the wing commander staff at each of these bases that receive [[Aviation Technology Office|ATO]]s, and do a "shredding" process to turn a large ATO into [[ATO Fragment]]s, which just contain the details that a single individual (e.g. pilot) needs to know.
