A deployed, operational wing, usually formed or activated for a particular mission, contingency, exercise, or geographic area. While a permanent Air Force wing belongs to a home station over the long term, an AEW is the organization created to employ forces *away from their normal home stations.* The aircraft and personnel don't necessarily come from one permanent wing. An Air Expeditionary Wing may combine units and individuals contributed by several bases (e.g. a fighter squadron from one baes, maintainers from another, support personnel from others might all operate together under one AEW commander).


These are the people on loan from service chiefs that are deployed at a deployed location. It's the wing commander staff at each of these bases that receive [[Aviation Technology Office|ATO]]s, and do a "shredding" process to turn a large ATO into [[ATO Fragment]]s, which just contain the details that a single individual (e.g. pilot) needs to know.
