The program is the JLTV, but it's also known and marketed under [[Oshkosh Defense]] development as the L-ATV (Light Combat Tactical all-Terrain Vehicle).


In May 2025, after acquiring 20,000 units, the [[United States Army|Army]] cancelled procurement of the JLTV as part of its transformation initiative, freeing up the funds to invest into lighter, autonomous, and modernized military systems.
- The [[United States Marine Corps|Marine]]s are sticking with the JLTV, but will buy fewer than originally planned, because the Army's exit drives up costs.


Lightweight, but has some [[Mine Resistant Ambush Protected|MRAP]] capabilities.
