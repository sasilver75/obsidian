https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATACMS
Pron. "Uh-Tack-Ums"

In Service 1991-Present

A short-range supersonic, [[Solid Rocket Motor]] [[Short-Range Ballistic Missile]] designed and manufactured by [[Ling-Temco-Vought]] (LTV), and, later, through acquisitions, [[Lockheed Martin]].

Can fly up to 190 miles, fired from either the tracked [[M270 Multiple Launch Rocket System|M270 MLRS]] or the wheeled [[M142 High Mobility Artillery Rocket System|M142 HIMARS]].

During the Ukrainian War, they've been very important to destroy air defense sights, grounded aircraft, airbases etc.

Slowly being replaced by the [[Precision Strike Missile]] (PrSM)
