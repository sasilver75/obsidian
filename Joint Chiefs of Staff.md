The body of the most senior uniformed leaders in the [[Department of Defense|DoD]], advising the [[President of the United States|POTUS]], [[Secretary of Defense|SECDEF]], the [[Homeland Security Council]], and the [[National Security Council]] on military matters.

Consists of:
- A [[Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff]]
- A Vice Chairman
- Chiefs of the [[United States Army|Army]], [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]], [[United States Navy|Navy]], [[United States Air Force|Air Force]], and [[United States Space Force|Space Force]], and chief of the [[National Guard]] Bureau

Each of the individual service chiefs works directly under the secretaries of their respective military departments (e.g. the secretary of the Army, secretary of the Navy, etc.)
