If the [[Joint Force Commander]] (JFC) does *not* delegate the authority for joint target planning, coordination, and deconfliction to the [[Joint Force Air Component Commander]] (JFACC), and approves the formation of a JFE, the air component needs to ensure linkages to this organization.

The JFE will integrate and synchronize fires planning and coordination and is composed of representatives from the J-3, and components (land, maritime, air, special ops), elements of the JFC's staff and directorates, and representatives from space and cyberspace operations, interagency partners and the intelligence community, and closely tied to the JFC [[J2 Officer|J-2]] for execution of joint targeting process.

The JFE is key for [[Joint Integrated Prioritized Target List]] (JIPTL) management and [[Joint Targeting Coordination Board]] (JTCB) collaboration with the J-2.
