A decades-old [[United States Army|Army]] foundational [[Targeting]] methodology used to shape the battlefield and integrate fires with maneuver operations.

The systematic process coordinates lethal and non-lethal engagements to achieve the commander's objectives.

- ==Decide==: This is the initial and most critical phase. Commanders and staff identify which targets must be acquired and engaged to achieve the mission, determine the priority of targets, and allocate specific resources. 
- ==Detect==: The intel/reconnaissance elements take the commander's guidance and utilize sensors and intelligence networks to locate the targets in real time.
- ==Deliver==: The tactical execution phase where the chosen lethal or non-lethal engagement means are applied against the targeted entity (e.g. artillery strikes, cyber effects, electronic warfare)
- ==Assess==: The final step evaluates the success of the operation. Staff measure whether the desired physical damage or behavioral change occurred, and determine if re-engagement is necessary.

See also [[Frame, Orient, Decide, Assess]] (FODA) framework, which is designed to be a modern replacement for this decades-old targeting methodology.
