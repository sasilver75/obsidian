An experimental stealth [[Unmanned Combat Aerial Vehicle]] (UCAV) designed and built by [[Kratos Defense]].
Originally designated the XQ-222.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kratos_XQ-58_Valkyrie


Variants:
- XQ-58A: Original prototype variant being tested with branches of the US military
- MQ-58B: An [[Electronic Warfare|Electronic Attack]] version intended for the Marine Corps' use in a [[Suppression of Enemy Air Defenses|SEAD]] role as a [[Collaborative Combat Aircraft|CCA]], to be operated alongside USMC [[F-35 Lightning II]]s.


Capable of being launched from a single point using [[Jet-Assisted Take-Off|JATO]], or launchable via a runway using a wheeled chassis that the drone leaves behind.
