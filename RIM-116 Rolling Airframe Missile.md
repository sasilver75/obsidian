SeaRAM variant: A launcher variant equipped with independent sensors derived from the [[Phalanx CIWS|CIWS]], being installed on [[Independence-Class Littoral Combat Ship]]s and certain [[Arleigh Burke-Class Destroyer]]s.

The [[RIM-116 Rolling Airframe Missile|Mk 49 GMLS]] is the primary deck-mounted launcher for the RIM-116 Rolling Airframe Missile (RAM).
- In 2025, the [[United States Navy|Navy]] received its 250th MK49 RAM air defense system.



> "See this [[Phalanx CIWS|CIWS]]? Let's take off the 20mm and put some tiny missiles on it; instead of 3000 rounds of 20mm HE a minute, you have 11 missiles that can be fired at short-range targets."
