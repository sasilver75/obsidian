An [[Orbital Elements|Orbital Element]], part of a set of parameters describing the orbit of an object around a central body.

Once you know the orbital plane, you still need to know how the ellipse is rotated *inside that plane.*


[[Argument of Periapsis|Argument of Perigee]] is the specific version of an argument of periapsis that is used exclusively for orbits around earth. Both measure the angular distance from the ascending node to the point of closest approach.
