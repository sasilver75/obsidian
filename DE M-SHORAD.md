A 50kW [[Directed Energy]] air defense system based on a [[Stryker]] that hasn't gained traction.

The U.S. Army received four prototype systems for assessment, but following user assessments, the Army ==decided against further broad procurement== or fielding the directed energy variant to operational combat units, moving instead toward alternative programs like the Enduring High Energy Laser (E-HEL).

> “That 50-kilowatt power level is proving challenging to incorporate into a vehicle that has to move around constantly — the heat dissipation, the amount of electronics, kind of the wear and tear of a vehicle in a tactical environment versus a fixed site.”

> “You may have a 50-kilowatt laser, but at 10 kilometers can you put at least four kilowatts in a centimeter square because … that’s what you need to burn through a quarter inch steel plate?” the four-star general asked. “But that’s really hard to get … from a big beam to get the small portion of it on the exact spot to be able to burn at that high intensity and any kind of dust particle or that starts to disrupt that.”
