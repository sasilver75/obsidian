> "The USAF’s service intelligence center and a field operating agency that reports directly to the Deputy Chief of Staff for Intelligence (HAF A2). NASIC produces integrated and predictive air, space, and specialized intelligence supporting military operations while also enabling forces to avoid technological surprise by countering existing and evolving air and space threats."
