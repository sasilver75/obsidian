> The [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|JDAM]] introduced all-weather satellite-assisted inertial navigation kits ([[GPS INS]]) that could be installed on the US's pre-existing cold war stockpile of dumb bombs, turning legacy weapons into a 21st century arsenal capable of eliminating ground targets with volume and accuracy. 
> In 2015, the JDAM saw a significant development with the introduction of the [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|JDAM-ER]], which saw the deployment of wing kits to allow the same bombs to glide under guidance, increasing range from 17 miles to 46 miles, making it possible for aircraft to drop bombs from outside the reach of some types of air defenses. Similar glide bombs have since been responsible for devastation on the front lines of Ukraine. As capable as these proved to be in combat, technology marches on. 
> Over the last few years, American tactical aircraft have been integrating a new type of glide bomb that makes the JDAM-ER look like a dumb bomb, with a tri-mode seeker, onboard AI, and the ability to locate/identify/close with moving targets... [[GBU-53B StormBreaker]] pushes the limits of what bombs are thought to be capable of doing. Glide bombs are difficult to detect and target using many common AD assets, and because they don't have propulsion, they present little-to-no infrared signatures, making them hard to intercept with [[Man-Portable Air Defense System|MANPADS]]. StormBreaker introduces on-board AI, an on-board data-link, and an advanced tri-mode seeker that makes it possible to prosecute more targets than ever before.  StormBreaker: Rather than relying solely on its GPS/INS to close with targets, it carries an advanced tri-mode seeker (active radar seeker, uncooled IR imaging sensor, sunlight-active laser seeker to be guided into targets via an offboard laser designator that can be operated by aircraft or ground troops in the area). These seekers don't operate independently; they share information across modalities, which in conjunction with computing power, lets the weapon identify and prioritize targets in flight. Thanks to its two-way data link, it can receive updated targeting information to *change those priorities* even after the weapon has been dropped. "The beauty of StormBreaker is that it will seek out and prosecute a target for you, instead of just prosecuting a coordinate." Four functional modes: In Coordinate Attack mode, the weapon functions like older glide bombs, using its on-board guidance system to close in on a set point in space. In Normal Attack mode, the weapon fuses data from on-board sensors to close with stationary or moving targets in any weather conditions, using GPS/INS to fly into the general vicinity, then relying on its infrared imaging sensor to identify the target, and the millimeter-wave radar seeker to close in for terminal guidance. In Immediate Attack mode, the weapon flies along a predictable flight path, and engages targets as they present themselves, using onboard sensors to locate/identify/close with targets that weren't even visible when the weapon was dropped. In Laser Illuminated Attack mode, it can function like any laser guided bomb, using its onboard computer to determine the trajectory to a target being laser-designated by air or ground troops. Its multi-effects warhead is designed to maximize its impact across a variety of targets. This 105lb [[Shaped Charge]] is designed to produce both blast and fragmentation effects, making it good for both hard and soft targets. Measures at 69 inches long, with a 6.5 inch diameter with wings folded, weighing in at 204lbs, more than half of which is its warhead. the [[F-15E Strike Eagle]] can carry a mind-boggling 28 StormBreakers into the fight, capable of engaging targets up to 70 miles out. So it's possible for a 4-ship formation of strike eagles to fly in and wipe out more than 100 individual targets in just a single sortie. For situations where stealth is a more important consideration, the [[F-35 Lightning II]] can carry 8 StormBreaker int he weapons bay alongside a pair of [[AIM-120 AMRAAM]]. The F-35B might be limited to 6, with a slightly smaller internal weapons bay.
