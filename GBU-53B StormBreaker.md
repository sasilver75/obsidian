> The [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|JDAM]] introduced all-weather satellite-assisted inertial navigation kits ([[GPS INS]]) that could be installed on the US's pre-existing cold war stockpile of dumb bombs, turning legacy weapons into a 21st century arsenal capable of eliminating ground targets with volume and accuracy. In 2015, the JDAM saw a significant development with the introduction of the [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|JDAM-ER]], which saw the deployment of wing kits to allow the same bombs to glide under guidance, increasing range from 17 miles to 46 miles, making it possible for aircraft to drop bombs from outside the reach of some types of air defenses. Similar glide bombs have since been responsible for devastation on the front lines of Ukraine. As capable as these proved to be in combat, technology marches on. Over the last few years, American tactical aircraft have been integrating a new type of glide bomb that makes the JDAM-ER look like a dumb bomb, with a tri-mode seeker, onboard AI, and the ability to locate/identify/close with moving targets... [[GBU-53B StormBreaker]] pushes the limits of what bombs are thought to be capable of doing.
