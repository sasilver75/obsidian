From [[Perennial Autonomy]]

A three-foot fixed-wing propeller-driven ==interceptor== from a truck-portable launcher at speeds up to 175 miles per hour, with an operational range of 3-12 miles.

Has downed more than 4,000 Russian drones over Ukraine.

In April 2026, Secretary Dan Driscoll told lawmakers that the [[United States Army|Army]] had purchased 13,000 Merops interceptors in the early days of the [[Operation Epic Fury]] conflict, at roughly $15,000 per unit.


Perennial has partnered with Munich-based Twentyfour Industries to produce the MErops in Germany, adding European capacity as allied demand grows.
