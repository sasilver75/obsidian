# Inventory

Ground Maneuver/Mobility
- [[ACV-P]]: Amphibious Combat Vehicle personnel carrier variant used to move Marines from ship to shore and then maneuver inland as an armored troop transport.
- [[ACV-C]]: Amphibious Combat Vehicle command-and-control variant that provides a mobile command post for commanders and staffs during amphibious and ground operations.
- [[ACV-30]]: Amphibious Combat Vehicle infantry fighting vehicle variant armed with a stabilized remote 30mm cannon for direct-fire support.
- [[ACV-R]]: Amphibious Combat Vehicle recovery variant equipped to repair, tow, lift, and recover damaged or immobilized vehicles in the field.
- [[LAV-25]]: Legacy eight-wheeled light armored reconnaissance vehicle armed with a 25mm cannon and used by Marine light armored reconnaissance units.
- [[Advanced Reconnaissance Vehicle]] (ARV): Next-generation reconnaissance vehicle family intended to replace or supplement the LAV family with improved sensing, communications, mobility, and payload capacity.
- [[Joint Light Tactical Vehicle]] (JLTV): Protected light tactical vehicle replacing part of the HMMWV fleet for troop movement, command-and-control, weapons carriage, and general tactical mobility.
- [[HMMWV]]: Legacy High Mobility Multipurpose Wheeled Vehicle used as a light tactical truck, weapons carrier, ambulance, shelter carrier, and utility vehicle.
- [[Ultra-Light Tactical Vehicle]] (ULTV): Lightweight tactical vehicle designed for small-unit mobility, internal transportability, and expeditionary movement by infantry and reconnaissance forces.
- [[Medium Tactical Vehicle Replacement]] (MTVR): Marine Corps medium tactical truck family used for cargo, troop movement, towing, fuel, water, and general logistics support.
- [[Logistics Vehicle System Replacement]] (LVSR): Heavy tactical logistics truck family used for long-haul cargo, container movement, wrecker operations, and bulk sustainment.
- [[Medium Tactical Truck]] (MTT): Developmental Marine Corps truck program intended to replace the MTVR with modular medium-payload tactical vehicles.
- [[McClung-class Landing Ship Medium]] (LSM): Planned Navy landing ship class intended to move Marine Littoral Regiment forces, equipment, and supplies between islands and austere shore locations.
- [[Multi-Mission Reconnaissance Craft]] (MMRC): Planned small craft for Marine reconnaissance and littoral mobility missions in contested coastal environments.

Fires / Missiles
- [[M777|M777A2]]: Lightweight 155mm towed howitzer used for cannon artillery fire support.
- [[M142 High Mobility Artillery Rocket System|M142 HIMARS]]: Wheeled rocket-artillery launcher used to fire precision rockets and missiles such as GMLRS, ATACMS, and PrSM.
- [[Guided Multiple Launch Rocket System|GMLRS]] + [[Guided Multiple Launch Rocket System|ER GMLRS]]: Precision-guided rocket family for HIMARS and MLRS launchers, with ER GMLRS extending the range of the baseline rocket.
- [[MGM-140 Army Tactical Missile System]] (ATACMS): Long-range surface-to-surface ballistic missile fired from HIMARS or MLRS launchers for deep precision strike.
- [[Precision Strike Missile]] (PrSM): Next-generation long-range surface-to-surface missile replacing ATACMS for precision deep-strike missions.
- [[Navy-Marine Expeditionary Ship Interdiction System]] (NMESIS): Marine Corps ground-based anti-ship missile system pairing Naval Strike Missiles with the unmanned ROGUE-Fires launcher.
- [[Naval Strike Missile]]: Sea-skimming precision anti-ship missile used by NMESIS and naval platforms to attack surface vessels.
- [[ROGUE-Fires]]: Remotely operated JLTV-based launcher vehicle that carries and launches Naval Strike Missiles as part of NMESIS.
- [[M270 Multiple Launch Rocket System]] (MLRS): Tracked rocket-artillery launcher that fires GMLRS, ATACMS, and PrSM-family munitions.
- [[M224 Mortar]]: Lightweight 60mm mortar used by infantry units for close indirect fire support.
- [[M252 Mortar]]: 81mm medium mortar used by infantry units for longer-range indirect fire support than 60mm mortars.
- [[FGM-148 Javelin]]: Man-portable fire-and-forget anti-tank guided missile used against armored vehicles, bunkers, and point targets.
- [[M3E1 MAAWS]] (Carl Gustaf): Recoilless rifle used by small units for anti-armor, anti-structure, illumination, and area-effect fires.

Air Defense / Counter-UAS
- [[FIM-92 Stinger]]: Man-portable infrared-guided surface-to-air missile used for short-range air defense against aircraft, helicopters, and some unmanned aircraft.
- [[Marine Air Defense Integrated System]] (MADIS): Mobile Marine Corps short-range air defense and counter-unmanned aircraft system integrating sensors, command-and-control, electronic warfare, and kinetic effectors.
- [[Marine Air Defense Integrated System|Light Marine Air Defense Integrated System]] (L-MADIS): Lighter counter-unmanned aircraft variant of MADIS optimized for expeditionary employment by smaller Marine units.
- [[Medium Range Intercept Capability]] (MRIC): Marine Corps medium-range air-defense system derived from Iron Dome technology and integrated with Marine sensors and command-and-control.
- [[ANTPS-80 Ground Air Task-Oriented Radar|AN/TPS-80 Ground/Air Task-Oriented Radar]] ([[ANTPS-80 Ground Air Task-Oriented Radar|G/ATOR]]): Expeditionary active electronically scanned array radar used for air surveillance, air defense, weapons cueing, and counterfire support.

Aviation
- [[F-35 Lightning II|F-35B]]: Short takeoff and vertical landing Marine Corps F-35 variant used for stealth strike, close air support, sensing, and air-defense missions from amphibious ships and expeditionary sites.
- [[F-35 Lightning II|F-35C]]: Carrier-capable Marine Corps F-35 variant used for stealth strike, sensing, and air-defense missions from aircraft carriers.
- [[FA-18 Hornet|F/A-18 Hornet]] (Legacy, Transitioning): Legacy Marine Corps multirole fighter and strike aircraft being replaced by F-35 variants.
- [[AV-8B Harrier II]] (Legacy, Transitioning): Legacy short takeoff and vertical landing attack aircraft being replaced by the F-35B.
- [[AH-1Z Viper]]: Marine Corps attack helicopter used for close air support, armed escort, anti-armor fires, and aerial reconnaissance.
- [[UH-1Y Venom]] (Super Huey): Marine Corps utility and armed support helicopter used for command-and-control, escort, casualty evacuation, reconnaissance, and light attack.
- [[MV-22 Osprey|MV-22B]] Osprey: Marine Corps tiltrotor aircraft used for assault support, troop transport, logistics, and long-range expeditionary mobility.
- [[CH-53E Super Stallion]]: Legacy heavy-lift helicopter used to move troops, vehicles, artillery, and cargo for Marine Air-Ground Task Force operations.
- [[CH-53K King Stallion]]: Modern heavy-lift helicopter replacing the CH-53E with greater lift capacity, range, reliability, and digital flight systems.
- [[KC-130J Super Hercules]]: Marine Corps tanker and transport aircraft used for aerial refueling, assault support, logistics, battlefield illumination, and multi-mission support.
- [[MQ-9 Reaper]]: Medium-altitude long-endurance remotely piloted aircraft used for intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance, targeting, and strike.
- [[XQ-58 Valkyrie]]: Experimental uncrewed aircraft used to explore low-cost autonomous teaming, sensing, and strike support for crewed aircraft and distributed forces.

Aviation Weapons / Effects
- [[AIM-9 Sidewinder|AIM-9X Sidewinder]]: Short-range infrared-guided air-to-air missile used for within-visual-range air combat and some surface-attack modes.
- [[AIM-120 AMRAAM]]: Active radar-guided beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile used by fighters for air-defense and counter-air missions.
- [[AGM-114 Hellfire]]: Precision air-to-ground missile used against vehicles, small boats, personnel, and point targets.
- [[AGM-179 JAGM]]: Joint Air-to-Ground Missile replacing or supplementing Hellfire-family weapons with multi-mode guidance for moving and stationary targets.
- [[Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System]] (APKWS): Laser-guidance kit that turns Hydra 70 rockets into low-cost precision-guided rockets.
- [[Hydra 70]]: Unguided 70mm rocket family used for air-to-ground fires and as the rocket basis for APKWS.
- [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|JDAM]]: GPS/inertial guidance kit family that converts unguided bombs into precision-guided air-to-ground munitions.
- [[GBU-54 Laser Joint Direct Attack Munition]]: Laser JDAM variant adding laser guidance to a GPS/inertial-guided bomb for moving or relocatable targets.
- [[GBU-10 Paveway II]]: 2,000-pound-class laser-guided bomb used for precision attack against larger point targets.
- [[GBU-12 Paveway II]]: 500-pound-class laser-guided bomb used for precision attack with lower yield than 2,000-pound weapons.
- [[GBU-16 Paveway II]]: 1,000-pound-class laser-guided bomb used for precision attack between the GBU-12 and GBU-10 size classes.
- [[GBU-24 Paveway III]]: 2,000-pound-class laser-guided bomb with improved guidance and glide performance over earlier Paveway II weapons.
- [[GBU-39B Small Diameter Bomb]]: 250-pound GPS-guided glide bomb that lets aircraft carry more precision weapons per sortie.
- [[GBU-53B StormBreaker]]: Small precision glide weapon with multi-mode guidance for attacking moving or stationary targets in adverse weather.
- [[AGM-158 JASSM|AGM-158C LRASM]]: Stealthy long-range anti-ship missile derived from the JASSM family and used for maritime strike.

Sensors / C2 / Networks
- [[Common Aviation Command and Control System]] (CAC2S): Marine Corps aviation command-and-control system that integrates air tracks, sensors, communications, and weapons coordination for the Marine Air Command and Control System.
- [[Common Aviation Command and Control System|CAC2S Small Form Factor]]: Smaller and more expeditionary CAC2S configuration intended to provide aviation command-and-control capability to distributed Marine units.
- [[Composite Tracking Network]] (CTN): Network that fuses and shares sensor tracks across Marine and naval systems to improve air-defense, situational awareness, and fire-control coordination.
- [[Marine Air Command and Control System]] (MACCS): Marine Corps system of agencies, sensors, communications, and command posts used to control aviation, airspace, air defense, and aviation fires.
- [[ANTPS-80 Ground Air Task-Oriented Radar|AN/TPS-80 Ground/Air Task-Oriented Radar]]: Multi-role Marine Corps radar used for air surveillance, air-defense cueing, and counterfire target acquisition.
- [[Advanced Field Artillery Tactical Data System]] (AFATDS): Digital fires command-and-control system used to plan, coordinate, approve, and execute artillery, rocket, mortar, naval, and air-delivered fires.
- [[Joint Battle Command-Platform]] (JBC-P) and [[Blue Force Tracking]]: Tactical command-and-control and friendly-force tracking systems that share unit positions, messages, and situational awareness.
- [[Project Dynamis]]: Marine Corps command-and-control modernization effort aimed at improving data integration, decision support, and distributed operations.

Unmanned / Logistics / Future Programs
- [[Tactical Resupply Unmanned Aircraft System]] (TRUAS): Small unmanned aircraft system intended to deliver supplies to forward Marine units without requiring crewed ground convoys or aircraft.
- [[Medium Aerial Resupply Vehicle - Expeditionary Logistics]] (MARV-EL): Medium unmanned logistics aircraft concept for moving heavier resupply loads across distributed expeditionary forces.
- [[MQ-72C Lakota Connector]]: Proposed uncrewed or optionally crewed logistics aircraft based on the UH-72 Lakota family for Marine Corps distributed resupply.
- [[Autonomous Low-Profile Vessel]] (ALPV): Uncrewed low-profile maritime resupply vessel intended to move supplies covertly through contested littoral waters.
