==Five Line== Request for CAS:
1. ==Observer/Warning Order==: Standard opening statement identifying the caller.
2. ==Friendly Location/Mark==: Grid coordinates and how friendly positions are marked (e.g. IR strobe, smoke).
3. ==Target Location==: relative direction (cardinal or clock) and distance from the friendly position to the target.
4. ==Target Description==: What the threat is (e.g. enemy squad, vehicle).
5. ==Remarks/Restrictions==: Critical safety measures, hazards, or final attack restrictions.


A ==Wheel== in the context of CAS (or "wheel orbit/wagon wheel") is the circular/elliptical holding pattern flown by support aircraft around a target area. It provides a constant, unbroken visual and sensor observation of the battlefield, while waiting to execute strikes.
