https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catastrophic_kill

Damage inflicted on an armored vehicle that renders it permanently non-functional/irreparable, most commonly by an explosion.

Typically results in the ignition of any fuel the vehicle may be carrying as well as the detonation/cook off/sympathetic detonation of its ammunition.

Most historical casualities in armored warfare were the result of K-kills, often associated with a tank's turret being blown skyward due to the overpressure of an ammunition explosion.
