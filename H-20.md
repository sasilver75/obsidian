A projected subsonic stealth bomber design referred to as a strategic project by the PLA; the first dedicated strategic bomber developed by china.

The *development* of a strategic bomber was revealed in September 2016. 
In 2025, some images on Chinese social media indicated that some test flights of the new bomber had potentially taken place.
==Not yet in service.==
