A strategy pairing crewed vehicles (like [[AH-64E Apache]]s or [[F-35 Lightning II]]s) with uncrewed systems ([[Unmanned Autonomous System|UAS]], [[Unmanned Ground Vehicle|UGV]], etc.). It allows human operators to control, share data with, and receive real-time intelligence from autonomous units, maximizing lethality and minimizing pilot casualties.
