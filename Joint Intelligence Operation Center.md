JIOCs centralize theater intelligence tasking, collection, analysis, and production.

Their targeting offices create target folders based on deliberate planning and taskings. 

JIOCs are located within all [[Unified Combatant Command]]s (CCMDs), and coordinate with theater [[J2 Officer|J-2]]s and maintain the [[Joint Target List|JTL]], [[No-Strike List|NSL]], and [[Restricted Target List|RTL]] for specific [[Operational Plan|OPLAN]]s or contingency plans ([[Contingency Plan|CONPLAN]]s).

JIOCs have liaisons from the major national intelligence agencies to facilitate effective national intelligence support to the theaters.

The functional commands [[US Strategic Command|USSTRATCOM]], [[US Transportation Command|USTRANSCOM]], [[US Special Operations Command|USSOCOM]] also have a JIOC.
