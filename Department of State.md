The lead foreign affairs agency and enabler of US diplomacy with a unique perspective on the nations of the world. The point of contact with the DOS for intelligence, analysis, and research is the Bureau of Intelligence, and Research (INR).

Information concerning political and military leaders, cultural trends, and economics can be valuable for a truly effects-based approach to conflict.
