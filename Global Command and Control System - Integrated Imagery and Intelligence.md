Often called "I-Cubed"

A specialized software suite integrated into the broader [[Global Command and Control System|GCCS]]... fusing raw imagery, target tracking, and intelligence data directly onto operational maps, providing a [[Common Operating Picture]] and delivering a unified, near-real-time tactical imagery and intelligence picture to military commanders and analysts.
