Not classified, but still withheld from public release.

Covers 
- Law enforcement records
- Export controlled technical data
- Critical infrastructure details
- Personal data on federal employees

Before 2010, agencies used dozens of inconsistent labels for this material, but a 2010 Executive Order replaced all of these with a single category.

No security clearance is required, but access is limited to those with a legitimate need.
