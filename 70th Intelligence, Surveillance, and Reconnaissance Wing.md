Serves as the [[United States Air Force|USAF]]'s cryptologic wing. The 70 ISRW executes global ISR missions to enable effects for the [[National Command Authority]], the joint warfighting team, air component operations, and USAF mission partners. Additionally, the 70 ISRW directs ISR support to US Cyber Command, with a focus on digital network analysis and computer network exploitation to enable cyberspace effects
