https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_countermeasure

An electrical or electronic device designed to trick or deceive [[Radar]], [[Sonar]], or other detection systems, like infrared (IR) or lasers.
- Can be decoys like the [[ANALE-55 Fiber-Optic Towed Decoy|AN/ALE-55 Fiber-Optic Towed Decoy]], the [[BriteCloud]], or even Laser-based [[Directional Infrared Countermeasure]] (DIRCM) platforms.

Most air forces use ECM to protect their aircraft from attack.

Compare these with kinetic air-interceptor-interceptor like the [[Miniature Self-Defense Missile]].
