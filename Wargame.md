
An adjudicated conflict simulation where players make decisions and a model/ruleset/umpire or computer determines outcomes.
The goal is analysis, education, or planning insight.
Players make decisions, opposing players react, adjudication determines consequences, the scenario state changes, re-loop till finish.

See also [[Tabletop Exercise]]

Wargames are not about predicting what will happen. They do not do that. They are a tol for discovering assumptions, tradeoffs, weak points, decision powers, and possible failure modes.
