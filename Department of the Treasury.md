Can support the [[Joint Targeting Cycle]] for counter-threat finance issues.

The Department of the Treasury’s Office of Foreign Assets Control and Office of Terrorism and Financial Intelligence administer and enforce economic and trade sanctions, based on US foreign policy and national security goals, against certain national security threats (e.g., rogue nations, terrorist facilitators, [[Weapons of Mass Destruction|WMD]] proliferators, money launderers, transnational criminal organizations).
