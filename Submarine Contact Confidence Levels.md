Naval operations and [[Anti-Submarine Warfare]] grades contacts using a standardized scale for verification:
1. ==NONSUB==: Contact evaluated and classified as ==not a submarine==.
2. ==POSSUB==: Contact classified as a ==possible submarine== with leak or limited indicators.
3. ==PROBSUB==: Contact classified as a ==probable submarine== holding strong, reliable sensor or acoustic evidence.
4. ==CERTSUB==: Contact sighted, tracked, and ==positively identified== as a submarine.


> Note that while sometimes you'll hear Smack people saying things like "Probsurf" (surface ship), this doesn't seem to really be a thing. Surface ships aren't graded on a "probability" scale like submarines because they can usually be verified visually or via radar. Instead, they use standard environmental and identity tags.
