aliases:
  - OTA
  - Other Transaction Agreement
---
A flexible, streamlined procurement tool used by the [[United States Department of Defense|DoD]] to fund research, prototypes, and follow-on production without being bound by strict, traditional Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) requirements.

OTAs were initially crated to help the government quickly acquire cutting-edge technology and engage with "non-traditional" defense contractors, such as startups and small tech companies that don't typically navigate complex government contracting laws.
