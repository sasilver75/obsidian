Training event using real units, staffs, platforms, ranges, command posts, simulations, or some combination, with the goal of improving or proving readiness and interoperability. Broad term encompassing:
- [[Field Training Exercise]] (FTX): A military exercise in which actual personnel and units operate in the field to practice tactical tasks under realistic terrain, movement, communications, sustainment, and friction.
- [[Command Post Exercise]] (CPX): A military exercise in which headquarters staffs practice planning, command-and-control, reporting, coordination, and decision-making, usually while subordinate forces are simulated rather than physically maneuvering.
- [[Live-Fire Exercise]] (LFX): A military exercise in which units employ real weapons and ammunition under controlled range conditions to train fires, maneuver, safety procedures, and tactical coordination.
- [[Tabletop Exercise]] (TTX): A discussion-based scenario exercise in which participants walk through decisions, authorities, procedures, and coordination requirements without deploying forces or executing field maneuvers.




# Well-Known Military Exercises with US Involvement

Many of these are umbrella exercises, not a single exercise type; many contain a mix of FPX/CPX/LFX/computer-assisted simulation, staff planning, logistics rehearsal, and even sometimes humanitarian/disaster-response training. Some are exercise campaigns with multiple sub-events, while others are more CPX-like, and others are more FTX/LFX-like.

Indo-Pacific
- [[Exercise Rim of the Pacific]]: Biennial U.S. Pacific Fleet-hosted multinational maritime exercise around Hawaii for coalition naval interoperability across surface, subsurface, air, amphibious, mine, and maritime-security operations.
- [[Exercise Valiant Shield]]: Biennial U.S.-led Indo-Pacific joint field exercise focused on integrating air, maritime, land, space, cyber, command-and-control, and long-range fires in a large-force scenario.
- [[Exercise Talisman Saber]]: Biennial U.S.-Australia combined joint exercise for large-scale crisis-response, amphibious, maritime, air, land, and logistics operations.
- [[Exercise Cobra Gold]]: Annual Thailand-U.S.-cohosted multinational exercise combining field training, command-post training, humanitarian assistance, disaster response, and regional security cooperation.
- [[Exercise Balikatan]]: Annual Philippines-U.S. combined exercise focused on territorial defense, interoperability, amphibious operations, maritime security, air-ground integration, and humanitarian assistance.
- [[Exercise Salaknib]]: Annual Philippines-U.S. Army exercise focused on land-force interoperability, combined arms training, command-and-control, and territorial-defense readiness.
- [[Exercise Kamandag]]: Annual Philippines-U.S. Marine Corps exercise focused on coastal defense, amphibious operations, humanitarian assistance, disaster response, and combined expeditionary operations.
- [[Exercise Super Garuda Shield]]: Annual Indonesia-U.S.-led multinational exercise focused on combined arms training, staff planning, airborne operations, amphibious operations, and Indo-Pacific interoperability.
- [[Exercise Yama Sakura]]: Annual Japan-U.S. command-post exercise focused on bilateral staff coordination, planning, and command-and-control for the defense of Japan.
- [[Exercise Keen Sword]]: Biennial Japan-U.S. field-training exercise focused on large-scale bilateral defense operations across maritime, air, land, space, and cyber domains.
- [[Exercise Keen Edge]]: Biennial Japan-U.S. command-post exercise that alternates with Keen Sword and focuses on staff-level planning, command-and-control, and bilateral crisis response.
- [[Exercise Resolute Dragon]]: Annual Japan-U.S. Marine Corps exercise focused on expeditionary advanced base operations, littoral maneuver, sensing, fires, and joint command-and-control.
- [[Exercise Iron Fist]]: Recurring Japan-U.S. amphibious exercise focused on amphibious assault, island defense, ship-to-shore movement, and Marine-ground force interoperability.
- [[Exercise Malabar]]: Annual naval exercise among India, Japan, Australia, and the United States focused on maritime interoperability, anti-submarine warfare, air defense, and surface warfare.
- [[Exercise Cope North]]: Annual Guam-centered multinational air exercise focused on air combat, agile combat employment, humanitarian assistance, disaster response, and air-force interoperability.
- [[Exercise Cope Tiger]]: Recurring Thailand-Singapore-U.S. air exercise focused on tactical aviation interoperability, air combat training, and regional security cooperation.
- [[Exercise CARAT]]: Annual Southeast Asian maritime exercise series focused on bilateral naval cooperation, maritime security, amphibious operations, and professional exchanges.
- [[Exercise SEACAT]]: Annual Southeast Asia maritime-security exercise focused on information sharing, coordination, and operational responses to illicit maritime activity.

Korean Peninsula
- [[Exercise Freedom Shield]]: Annual Republic of Korea-U.S. spring exercise focused on combined defense planning, command-post operations, field training, and readiness against North Korean threats.
- [[Exercise Ulchi Freedom Shield]]: Annual Republic of Korea-U.S. late-summer exercise combining government crisis-management procedures, command-post training, and military field training.
- [[Exercise Ssang Yong]]: Recurring Republic of Korea-U.S. amphibious exercise focused on combined landing operations, ship-to-shore movement, and Marine Corps interoperability.

Europe/NATO
- [[Exercise DEFENDER-Europe]]: Recurring U.S. Army Europe and Africa-led exercise focused on strategic deployment, reception, onward movement, sustainment, and NATO interoperability.
- [[Exercise Saber Strike]]: Recurring Baltic-region exercise focused on multinational land operations, command-and-control, maneuver, and deterrence on NATO's northeastern flank.
- [[Exercise Saber Guardian]]: Recurring southeastern Europe exercise focused on multinational land operations, river crossings, air and missile defense, medical evacuation, and Black Sea-region readiness.
- [[Exercise Swift Response]]: Recurring airborne exercise focused on rapid deployment, joint forcible entry, multinational airborne operations, and theater-wide crisis response.
- [[Exercise Immediate Response]]: Recurring Europe-based exercise focused on multinational movement, reception and staging, logistics, live-fire training, and operational interoperability.
- [[Exercise Dynamic Front]]: Recurring NATO fires exercise focused on artillery interoperability, targeting, fire-support coordination, digital fires networks, and multinational command-and-control.
- [[Exercise Combined Resolve]]: Recurring multinational training event focused on brigade-level maneuver, interoperability, and realistic combat training at European training areas.
- [[Exercise Saber Junction]]: Recurring U.S. Army Europe exercise focused on brigade combat team readiness, combined arms maneuver, and interoperability with NATO partners.
- [[Exercise Allied Spirit]]: Recurring NATO-partner land exercise focused on multinational brigade-level operations, command-and-control, and interoperability at combat training centers.
- [[Exercise BALTOPS]]: Annual Baltic Sea maritime exercise focused on amphibious operations, mine countermeasures, air defense, anti-submarine warfare, and maritime interoperability.
- [[Exercise Sea Breeze]]: Recurring Black Sea maritime exercise focused on naval interoperability, maritime security, amphibious operations, and regional security cooperation.
- [[Exercise Trojan Footprint]]: Annual U.S. Special Operations Command Europe exercise focused on special operations integration, unconventional warfare, resistance support, and NATO interoperability.
- [[Exercise Austere Challenge]]: Recurring U.S. European Command command-post exercise focused on joint and combined theater-level planning, crisis response, and command-and-control.
- [[Exercise Astral Knight]]: Recurring U.S. Air Forces in Europe exercise focused on integrated air and missile defense, air operations, and theater-level command-and-control.
- [[Exercise Formidable Shield]]: Biennial NATO maritime exercise focused on integrated air and missile defense, ballistic missile defense, and live-fire shipboard air-defense operations.
- [[Exercise Dynamic Manta]]/Mongoose: Recurring NATO anti-submarine warfare exercises focused on submarine detection, tracking, prosecution, and maritime patrol coordination.
- [[Exercise Cold Response]]/Nordic Response: Recurring Norway-centered high-north exercise focused on cold-weather operations, reinforcement, amphibious operations, and NATO collective defense.

Middle East/Central Command
- [[Exercise Eager Lion]]: Annual Jordan-hosted multinational exercise focused on crisis response, border security, command-and-control, counterterrorism, cyber defense, and combined operations.
- [[Exercise Bright Star]]: Biennial Egypt-hosted multinational exercise focused on large-scale joint and combined operations, interoperability, air-ground integration, and regional security cooperation.
- [[Exercise Native Fury]]: Recurring U.S. Central Command exercise focused on maritime prepositioning, port opening, logistics, force deployment, and crisis-response sustainment.
- [[Exercise Eagle Resolve]]: Recurring Gulf-region exercise focused on regional interoperability, consequence management, command-and-control, and collective responses to security threats.

- [[Exercise Desert Flag]]: Annual United Arab Emirates-hosted multinational air exercise focused on tactical air combat, strike coordination, and coalition air-force interoperability.

Africa

- [[Exercise Flintlock]]: Annual U.S. Special Operations Command Africa exercise focused on African and allied special operations interoperability, counterterrorism, and security-force capacity.

- [[Exercise Shared Accord]]/United Accord: Recurring Africa-focused exercise series focused on peacekeeping, humanitarian assistance, command-post training, and partner-force interoperability.

Latin America/Caribbean

- [[Exercise Resolute Sentinel]]: Annual U.S. Southern Command exercise focused on humanitarian assistance, engineering, medical readiness, air operations, and security cooperation in Latin America.

- [[Exercise CENTAM Guardian]]: Recurring Central America exercise focused on disaster response, security cooperation, command-and-control, and regional interoperability.
- [[Exercise New Horizons]]: Recurring U.S. Southern Command humanitarian and civic-assistance exercise focused on engineering projects, medical services, and partner-nation capacity building.

Domestic/Functional

- [[Exercise Red Flag-Alaska]]: Recurring Pacific Air Forces advanced air combat exercise in Alaska focused on large-force air operations, coalition interoperability, and realistic air and surface threats.

- [[Exercise Arctic Edge]]: Recurring U.S. Northern Command exercise focused on homeland defense, Arctic operations, cold-weather mobility, and joint operations in extreme environments.

- [[Exercise Global Thunder]]: Annual U.S. Strategic Command exercise focused on nuclear command-and-control, strategic deterrence, global strike, and continuity of operations.

- [[Exercise Space Flag]]/Global Sentinel: Recurring space exercise family focused on space-domain awareness, orbital warfare scenarios, coalition space operations, and space command-and-control.
