A highly-expensive non-classified [[Department of Defense|DoD]] acquisition program.
Requires an eventual RDT&E expenditure exceeding $525 M, or a procurement cost over $3.065B.
Investments like these ([[F-35 Lightning II]], [[Virginia-Class Attack Submarine]]) are among the military's most critical modernization efforts, and are governed by script milestone decisions under the Defense Acquisition System and are closely monitored by Congress.


Classified as [[Major Defense Acquisition Program|Acquisition Category I]] programs.
