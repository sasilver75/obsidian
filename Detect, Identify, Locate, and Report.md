A core tactical mission set in the context of [[Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance|ISR]] and [[Electronic Warfare|EW]].

Dictates the chronological sequence of battlefield intelligence operations:
1. Detect
2. Identify
3. Locate
4. Report

Primary platforms:
- Air-launched Effects (ALEs): [[Red Wolf|Green Wolf]]
- Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS): [[MQ-1C Gray Eagle|MQ-1C Gray Eagle Extended Range]], [[Shield AI V-Bat|V-Bat]], ...
- Electronic Warfare Vehicles: Tactical ground systems
