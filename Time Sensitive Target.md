---
aliases:
  - Time-Sensitive Target
  - Target of Opportunity
---
A target that requires immediate or rapid action because the target presents a serious threat, is fleeting, or creates a short-lived opportunity.
- The target still requires identification, authority, rules-of-engagement compliance, collateral-risk consideration, and coordination.

Examples:
- Mobile missile launcher exposed for a few minutes
- Enemy aircraft preparing to launch
- Command vehicle briefly located by signals intelligence
- High-priority ship entering weapon range
- Air defense system turning on radar briefly
- Enemy unit massing for an imminent attack

Often handled through [[Dynamic Targeting]], commonly using a framework like [[Find, Fix, Track, Target, Engage, Assess|F2T2EA]].
