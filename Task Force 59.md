US [[United States Navy|Navy]] Task Force 59 (TF-59) is the first dedicated "Unmanned and Artificial Intelligence Task Force," established in 2021 and headquartered in Bahrain under US 5th Fleet, with the primary mission of rapidly integrating maritime drones and aI into everyday naval operations to enhance regional security.

Also has a "==Task Group 59.1,==" which focuses on taking the capabilities of TF 59's unmanned systems and charting ground with manned and unmanned ***teaming*** concepts.
