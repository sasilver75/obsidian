Weapon Open System Architecture (WOSA) is a U.S. [[Department of Defense|Department of Defense]] (DoD) technical standard and design framework used to develop munitions. 

Part of the broader [[Modular Open Systems Approach|Modular Open Systems Approach]] (MOSA), WOSA is designed to eliminate vendor lock, accelerate new technology deployment, and dramatically lower lifecycle costs by allowing the government to swap out parts—like seekers, propulsion, and software—from competing vendors
