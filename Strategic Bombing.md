A military strategy of aerial bombardment designed to destroy a country's long-term capacity and will to wage war.
Targets essential enemy infrastructure like factories, oil refineries, railways, and command centers, rather than immediate frontline military units.

The ultimate goal is to paralyze the enemy's economy and force a surrender or collapse.

Compare this with [[Tactical Bombing]], which supports immediate ground combat by striking enemy troops or tanks on the battlefield.


> Strategic bombing is sometimes abused to carpet bomb population centers, firebomb Tokyo, etc.

> "After 30+ years of COIN... we 'won' this by winning the 'how many moles can we whack at a time,' which forced us to get very good at identifying objects (people, places, things) that were critical nodes in an insurgency network. This is inherently [[Dynamic Targeting]]. Now we come to this Strategic Bombing idea... which is much more [[Deliberate Targeting]]."
