https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_generation_warfare

Broadly defined as "military and quasi-military operations other than conventional warfare," and may include:
- Use of covert forces
- Subversion
- Diversion
- Sabotage
- Espionage
- Biowarfare
- Sanctions
- Propaganda
- Guerilla Warfare

This is typically done to avoid escalation into conventional warfare as well as international conventions.
 
> Another type of warfare— new in its intensity, ancient in its origin—war by guerrillas, subversives, insurgents, assassins; war by ambush instead of by combat, by infiltration instead of aggression, seeking victory by eroding and exhausting the enemy instead of engaging him. It preys on unrest.
