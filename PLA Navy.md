300,000 active service members, organized into three major fleets:
- The North Sea fleet headquartered at Qingdao (Northern Theater Command Fleet)
- The East Sea Fleet headquartered at Ningbo (Eastern Theater Command Fleet)
- The South Sea Fleet headquartered at Zhanjiang (Southern Theater Command Fleet)

The navy also includes a 25,000 strong ==Marine Corps==, organized into seven brigades, as well as a 18,000 strong ==Naval Aviation Force== operating several hundred attack helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft.
