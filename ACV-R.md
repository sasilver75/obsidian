A specialized variant of the ACV family ([[ACV-R]], [[ACV-C]], [[ACV-P]], [[ACV-30]])

Designed by [[BAE Systems]] to provide direct field support, maintenance, and mobile recovery for immobilized or damaged vehicles during amphibious and land operations. It's equipped with a winch and crane to tow/lift/extract disabled vehicles without needing rear-echelon assistance. Carries integrated welding, diagnostic tools, and repair equipment.
