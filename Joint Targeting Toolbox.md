The joint [[Targeting]] tool of record.

A suite of software modules specifically engineered to optimize the joint targeting process throughout the entire [[Joint Targeting Cycle]]. 

Pulls data from [[Modernized Integrated Database|MIDB]] (or [[Machine-assisted Analytic Rapid-repository System|MARS]] in the future?) and provides functionality for target development and analysis, weaponeering, and the nomination of applicable targets for attack.

JTT is used to access target details and target lists, and to create the [[Joint Integrated Prioritized Target List]] (JIPTL). 

Uses the [[Global Command and Control System]] (GCCS)'s Integrated Imagery and Intelligence  ([[Global Command and Control System - Integrated Imagery and Intelligence|GCCS-I3]]; [[Global Command and Control System - Integrated Imagery and Intelligence]]).
