The planned targeting against known or anticipated targets before execution.
- Compare with [[Dynamic Targeting]], which is targeting *during execution,* usually against newly detected, mobile, fleeting, or otherwise not-handled-in-the-deliberate-cycle targets.

Deliberate Targeting involves more complete planning depth, producing targeting lists, air tasking order inputs, fire support plans, etc. It has more time for review. Uses something more like the [[Joint Targeting Cycle]], rather than [[Find, Fix, Track, Target, Engage, Assess|F2T2EA]].

> A joint force knows an enemy integrated air defense system has several radar sites, command nodes, missile batteries, fiber links, and logistics depots. The staff analyzes the target system, decides which nodes matter most, nominates targets, checks legal and collateral restrictions, matches weapons/platforms to desired effects, and assigns missions through planned orders.
