An identification friend or foe device mounted on military ground vehicles used by the [[United States Army|Army]] and [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] to distinguish themselves from teh enemy during battle.

First developed after the Persian Gulf War to reduce friendly fire incidents among ground forces. Originally created as a hasty expediency; the panel appears as a contrasting dark (cooler) area through thermal viewers in white hot mode.
