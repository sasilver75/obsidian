Procedural controls established by commanders to facilitate the rapid engagement of targets while providing safeguards and preventing fratricide for friendly forces.

FSCMS fall into two categories based on their operational function:
- Permissive Measures
	- [[Coordinated Fire Line]] (CFL): Allows conventional surface-to-surface fires beyond the line without additional.
	- [[Fire Support Coordination Line]] (FSCL): A joint measure permitting air and surface attacks beyond the line, requiring prior notification to affected friendly commanders.
	- [[Free-Fire Area]] (FFA): An area where any weapon system can fire without coordination with the establishing headquarters.
	- [[Kill Box]]: A three-dimensional area used to facilitate the integration of joint fires.
- Restrictive Measures
	- [[No-Fire Area]] (NFA): An area where fires and targeting effects are strictly prohibited, except under specific self-defense or approved conditions.
	- [[Restrictive Fire Area]] (RFA): An area with specific restrictions where fires exceeding safety limits require coordination.
	- [[Restrictive Fire Line]] (RFL): A line established between converging friendly forces requiring explicit coordination for any fire crossing it.
