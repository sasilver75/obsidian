
The Army's first rucksack-sized quadrotor drone from [[Skydio]]. The Army's first [[Program of Record]] quadcopter drone.

Newer replacements are being deployed in 2026.
The [[RQ-28A]] was the Tranche 1 winner for the Army's Short-Range Reconnaissance (SRR) program, while the [[Black Widow]] won the subsequent competition for later tranches.
