A type of [[Intelligence]].
Intelligence derived from imagery, maps, terrain data, and location-based information. It helps answer where things are, how terrain affects operations, and what can be seen or inferred from geospatial data.

The exploitation and analysis of imagery and geospatial information to describe, asses, and visually depict physical features and geographically-referenced activities on or about teh earth.

GEOINT is necessary for visualizing the operating environment, enabling planners to view natural and cultural features. Most geospatial products are now presented in digital formats and available through web access and automated means.

[[Targeteer]]s need access to current imagery for target development and assessment, as well as [[Mensuration]] tools to provide precise coordinates with the accuracy necessary for precise munitions employment ([[Target Coordinate Mensuration]]). Point mensuration tools for these activities include a digital point position database, controlled and rectified by the [[National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency|NGA]].
