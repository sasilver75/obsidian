[[United States Army|Army]] context

Effectively, the COIC is doctrinally manned with every warfighting function enabling rapid integration across warfighting functions and staff sections.

Note that FM 6-0 clearly defines the integration of the [[Current Operations|CUOPS]] cell but remains vague on the [[Future Operations|FUOPS]] or Plans cells.
The manually merely states that all staff sections assist FUOPS "ass required," offering no concrete guidance on integrating all warfighting functions.
