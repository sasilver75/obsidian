SM = "Standard Missile"
ERAM = "Extended Range Active Missile"

A highly versatile ship-launched and air-launched multi-mission missile from [[Raytheon]] designed for the [[United States Navy|Navy]], combining the propulsion of the [[RIM-66C SM-2MR|SM-2R]] with the advanced radar seeker of the [[AIM-120 AMRAAM]] air-to-air missile, improving the capability of the SM against highly agile targets and targets beyond the effective range of the launching vessel's target illumination radars. Used for:
- Anti-Air Warfare
- Ballistic Missile Defense
- Surface (Anti-ship) and Land Strike
- Air-Launched Variant

The [[AIM-174B Gunslinger]] is the air-to-air variant of the SM-6.
