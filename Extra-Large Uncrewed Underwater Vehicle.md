Massive, autonomous, modular submarines designed by navies for months-long, long-range missions without human presence onboard.

They're *typically* used for [[Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance|ISR]], mine countermeasures, and seabed warfare.

Examples:
- [[United States Navy]]'s [[Orca XLUUV]]
- [[PLA Navy|PLAN]]'s [[AJX002]] and [[HSU100]]
- [[Anduril]]'s [[Anduril Ghost Shark XL-AUV]]
- Canadian Solus-XR
- Indian Jalkapi
