A [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] agile, modular, off-road vehicle designed to bolster infantry/reconnaissance mobility in austere, contested environments.
Based on the Polaris MRZR Alpha, which is internally transportable inside the [[MV-22 Osprey]] and [[CH-53K King Stallion]] helicopters.
