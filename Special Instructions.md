A set of instructions that provides information not otherwise available in the [[Air Tasking Order]], but is necessary for its completion.

This may include such information as the commander's guidance (often including the AOD itself), the [[Command and Control|C2]] battle management plan, [[Combat Search and Rescue|CSAR]] procedures, the communications plan, and general instructions for inter- and intra-theater airlift.
