Prioritizes intelligence efforts for joint targeting activities. The J-2 staff conducts [[Joint Intelligence Preparation of the Environment|JIPOE]] to assist in the development of targeting guidance. Normally, J-2 manages theater collection priorities via the [[Joint Collection Management Board]] (JCMB). Typically, the deputy directorate for targets (J-2T) focuses on the targeting process.

The air component [[A2 Officer|A-2]] staff should integrate into J-2 processes.
