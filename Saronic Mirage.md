[[Saronic Technologies|Saronic]]'s Mirage is a 52' Autonomous Surface Vessel (ASV) that can provide diverse capabilities across a range of maritime operations. With 2,500 NM of range and 3,500 lbs of payload capacity, Mirage is poised to serve as a force multiplier for manned and unmanned teams in every ocean.
