Uses a complementary *pair of* [[Joint Light Tactical Vehicle|JLTV]]s, designed to protect expeditionary forces.
Combines Radar, EW capabilities, [[FIM-92 Stinger]] missiles, and 30mm cannons to neutralize drones, helicopters, and fixed-wing aircraft.
- MADIS Mk1: Focuses on kinetic/electronic neutralization of traditional aircraft.
- MADIS Mk2: Functions as the C2 and radar variant, with 360-degree radar for tracking low-altitude targets, additional EW capabilities, and supporting communications suite to coordinate operations.

Also comes in an (interim?) L-MADIS variant, using the [[Ultra-Light Tactical Vehicle|ULTV]] as a host for radar and EW payloads for rapid deployment in littoral or remote island environments.
