JASSM = "Joint Air-to-Surface Standoff Missile"
Pron. "Jazz-Em"
Pron. "El-Razz-Em" ((???))

A family of *==low-observable==*, subsonic, air-launched [[Cruise Missile]]s designed to destroy valuable, heavily defended targets without requiring the launching aircraft to enter the densest part of the enemy's air-defense network.

The family has developed along two main branches:
- JASSM Land-Attack Variants: AGM-158A, AGM-158B, AGM-158B-2, AGM-157B-3, AGM-158D
- Long Range Anti-Ship Missile (LRASM) variants: AGM-158C-1 and the longer-range AGM-158C-3










The XR variant is still in development.
