Defines the spatial zone or set of flight/launch conditions where a weapon (e.g. missile, bomb) can be successfully launched from an aircraft or platform to hit a specific target.

Considers target range, aircraft, altitude, and weapon flight capabilities.



# Comparison with [[Weapon Engagement Zone]] (WEZ)
- LAR: a purely kinematic calculation; defines where a weapon can physically reach a target based on current speed, altitude, and heading. Assumes the target maintains its current flight path.
- WEZ: A tactical boundary; factors in target maneuverability, counter-measures, and evasion tactics. It defines the zone where a kill is highly probable, even if the enemy defends themselves.
- A LAR iw weapon-centric, whereas a WEZ is environment- and threat-centric.
