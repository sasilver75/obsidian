The [[United States Army]]'s acquisition hub created to close the critical gap between the pace of modern threats and the speed of capability delivery.


> Battlefield threats change rapidly, and any delay puts our Soldiers and battlefield superiority at risk. The Army requires a novel approach to accelerate capability development, prioritize innovation, synchronize efforts across PAEs (Portfolio Acquisition Executives), and foster allied nation industry capabilities to prepare for near-peer conflict.
