An armored vehicle program initiated by the [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] to replace the aging [[LAV-25]] family of vehicles.

The U.S. Marine Corps Advanced Reconnaissance Vehicle (ARV) is ==not yet in service==. The vehicle is currently in the rapid prototyping and pre-production development phase. Prototype deliveries from [[General Dynamics]] and [[Textron Systems]] are expected by late 2028.
