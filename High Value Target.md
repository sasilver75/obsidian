A target that is important to the enemy.
A target that the *enemy commander* needs in order to be able to successfully perform the enemy commander's mission.

The key-point is that it's high-value from the enemy's perspective:
- Air defense command node
- mobile missile launcher
- bridge needed for enemy reinforcement
- logistics depot supporting an offensive
- senior command post
- radar needed for enemy integrated air defense

A High-Value target may or may not be worth attacking *right now.* The target matters to the enemy, but the target might not matter enough to the friendly commander's current plan to action immediately.

Note that a HVT is not automatically a [[High Payoff Target|High-Payoff Target]]; just because it's important to the enemy doesn't mean that striking it would support the current commander's mission, or that it's authorized, or that it wouldn't consume assets needed elsewhere.
