A [[PLA Rocket Force|PLARF]] [[Hypersonic Glide Vehicle]] developed by the PRC; launched by the [[DF-17]] [[Medium-Range Ballistic Missile]].

Likely operational since late October, 2019.

The DF-ZF is thought to reach speeds between Mach 5 (3,836 mph (6,173 km/h; 1,715 m/s)) and Mach 10 (7,680 mph (12,360 km/h; 3,430 m/s)).
