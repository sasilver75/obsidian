A new [[Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office|CDAO]] approach to scaling data analytics and AI capabilities.

It's a way to leverage a competitive acquisition process to rapidly procure and integrate best-in-class technology from industry to meet specific DoW requirements and ensure interoperability.

A holistic approach to align the architecture (platforms, data, applications) and acquisitions approaches (governance, budget, incentives and industry engagements).
