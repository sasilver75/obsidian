Black Widow [[Short Range Reconnaissance]] (SRR) Drone
Manufactured by [[Red Cat|Teal Drones]]/[[Red Cat]]

The [[RQ-28A]] was the Tranche 1 winner for the Army's Short-Range Reconnaissance (SRR) program, while the [[Black Widow]] won the subsequent competition for later tranches.

"Developed to address vulnerabilities exposed in [[Electronic Warfare]] environments, the Black Widow is build from the ground up for contested airspace and electromagnetic jamming."
