The headquarters, commands, and agencies responsible for developing and executing joint military operations.

Consists of the [[Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff]] (CJCS), [[Unified Combatant Command|Combatant Command]]s (CCMDs), services, and defense agencies.
