An attack or damage inflicted by a weapon that doesn't destroy a military vehicle, but results in it taking no further part in its intended mission.


In the context of a [[MIM-104 Patriot|Patriot]] intercept:
> Patriot intercepts that don't disable the ballistic missile warhead but nevertheless minimize damage on the ground... either damaging the warhead to the point that it only burned at ground impact, or deflecting the ballistic missile from its initial path to impact with no significant ground damage to personnel or major structures.
