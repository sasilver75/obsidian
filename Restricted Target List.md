A list of restricted targets nominated by elements of the joint force and approved by the [[Joint Force Commander]] or directed by higher authorities.

Targets are restricted because certain types of actions against them may have negative political, cultural, or propaganda implications, or may interfere with projected friendly operations. 

Actions that exceed specified restrictions are are prohibited until coordinated and approved by the establishing headquarters.
