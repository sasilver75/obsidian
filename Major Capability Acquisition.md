In the context of the [[Adaptive Acquisition Framework]]... when a military program is funneled into the MCA pathway (the process), it is assigned an [[Acquisition Category]] ([[Major Defense Acquisition Program|ACAT I]], [[Acquisition Category II|ACAT II]], [[Acquisition Category III|ACAT III]]) depending on its total estimated cost and strategic importance.

If that program is expensive enough to be classified as [[Major Defense Acquisition Program|ACAT I]], it is legally designated as a [[Major Defense Acquisition Program]] (MDAP).
