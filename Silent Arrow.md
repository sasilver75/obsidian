A family of autonomous cargo drones.
- GD-2000: Flagship, heavy-payload glider (1,500 pounds of cargo up to 35 nautical miles; 8.4:1 glide ratio) produced for the [[United States Department of Defense|DoD]] and 16 allied nations, confirmed operational deployments in the middle east with [[US Special Operations Command|SOCOM]], deployable from military aircraft like the [[C-130 Hercules]], [[C-17 Globemaster III]], and [[V-22 Osprey]].
- CLS-200: Motorized iteration, carrying 500 pounds of cargo 200 nautical miles
- CLS-300: Motorized iteration, carrying 1000 pounds of cargo 300-500 nautical miles
