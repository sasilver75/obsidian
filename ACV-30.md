An [[Infantry Fighting Vehicle]] (IFV) variant of the Marine Corps Amphibious Combat Vehicle (ACV), manufactured by BAE Systems. It's armed with a remotely-operated, stabilized 30mm cannon, offering the largest organic weapon system in the Marine Corp's ground combat fleet.

Part of a larger ACV family ([[ACV-R]], [[ACV-C]], [[ACV-P]], [[ACV-30]])
