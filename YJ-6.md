Chinese air-launched anti-ship cruise missile.
In service 1986 - Present

![[Pasted image 20260709131416.png]]

Variants:
- YJ-6: Base variant
- YJ-61: Improved variant, range extended to 200km
- YJ-63: Air-launched LACM sharing visual characteristics of the HY-2, HY-4, and YJ-6
- C-601: Export variant of YJ-6
- C-611: Export variant of YJ-61
- C-603: Export variant of YJ-63
