The "Continental Staff System", also known as the "General Staff System" (GSS), is used by most NATO countries in structuring their militaries' staff functions. In this system, which is based on one originally employed by the French Army in the 19th century, each staff position in a headquarters or unit is assigned a letter-prefix corresponding to the formation's element and one or more numbers specifying a role.

This applies to [[Staff Officer|S-Staff]], [[A-Staff]], [[N-Staff]], [[Joint Staff|J-Staff]], [[General Staff|G-Staff]], etc.

The staff numbers are assigned according to custom, not hierarchy, traceable back to French practice:
- 1 for manpower or personnel (e.g. [[S1 Officer]])
- 2 for intelligence and security
- 3 for operations
- 4 for logistics
- 5 for plans
- 6 for signals
- 7 for military education and training
- 8 for finance and contracts
- 9 for civil-military cooperation or civil affairs

Because the original continental staff system only covered branches 1 through 6, it's not uncommon to see 7-9 iether omitted or having various meanings.

These staff functions traditionally were prefixed by the simple G (referring to "Great General Staff" in Prussian), but now we have new prefixes:
- A: [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] headquarters
- C: Combined (in the sense of multiple nations) headquarters
- F: Certain forward to deployable headquarters
- G: For [[United States Army|Army]] or [[United States Marine Corps|Marine]] general staff sections within headquarters of organizations commanded by a general officer
- J: For joint (in the sense of multiple services) headquarters, including the [[Joint Chiefs of Staff]]
- M: For [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] headquarters ((I couldn't find instances of this being true, though))
- N: For [[United States Navy|Navy]] headquarters
- S: For [[United States Army|Army]] or [[United States Marine Corps|Marine]] executive staff sections within headquarters of organizations commanded by a field grade officer (i.e. [[Major]] through [[Colonel]]).
- U: For United Nations military operations mission headquarters
- CG: For [[United States Coast Guard|Coast Guard]] Assistant commandants (Headquarters staff)
