Uninhabited islands in the East China Sea, administered by Japan.
- Japan administers teh Senkaku Islands as part of the city of Ishigaki in Okinawa Prefecture.
- Japan's central government has denied landing requests from even local authorities.

The islands are the focus of a territorial dispute between Japan, China, and Taiwan.
- A major catalyst for ownership has been the discovery of potential offshore oil/natural gas reserves beneath the seabed of he East China Sea, which was confirmed in official reports of several international organizations in 1968/1969.

The island group is known to consist of five uninhabited islets and three barren rocks.[75] China has identified and named as many as 71 islets that belong to this group after the Japanese Cabinet released names for 39 uninhabited islands.
