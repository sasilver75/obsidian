A visual intelligence product used by planners and commanders to evaluate terrain, weather, and infrastructure in an array of operation.

Key features typically highlighted on a MCOO include:
- Mobility classifications (categorizing terrain as restricted, unrestricted, severely restricted)
- Mobility corridors and avenues of approach (likely paths for advancing or retreating forces)
- Obstacles (natural or man-made obstructions)
- Key-Terrain and Engagement Areas (strategic high ground or locations ideal for battle)
