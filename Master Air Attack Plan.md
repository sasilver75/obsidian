The time-phased air scheme of maneuver that details how and when air assets will strike targets during a specific [[Air Tasking Order]] (ATO) period.

Created in the Combat Plans Division (CPD) of an [[Air Operations Center]] (AOC).

The MAAP is structured in a few key steps:
1. Target Selection: Planners take guidance from the Joint Force Air Component Commander and ensure that the daily target selection meets those objectives.
2. Force Application: Planners decide which aircraft, weapons, and support packages (like refueling, EW) are needed to strike those specific targets.
3. Time-Phasing: Planners map out exactly when and where each mission will happen to synchronize the operation safely and effectively.

Once the MAAP is approved, it is handed off to the ATO Production Team, which translates the MAAP into the finalized [[Air Tasking Order]].
