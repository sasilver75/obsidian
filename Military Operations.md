

Subordinate Operations:
- [[Operation Anaconda]] (2002): Major early Afghanistan battle in the Shah-i-Kot Valley.
- [[Operation Phantom Fury]] (2004): Second Battle of Fallujah, one of the major urban battles of the Iraq War.
- [[Operation Red Wings]] (2005): Failed SEAL reconnaissance mission in Afghanistan.
- [[Operation Kayla Mueller]] (2019): The successful raid that killed ISIS leader Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi.
- [[Operation Eagle Claw]] (1980): Failed U.S. military rescue mission intended to free 52 American hostages held in Tehran.

Major Operations:
- [[Operation Enduring Freedom]] (2001-2014): Main US war operation in Afghanistan after September 11.
- [[OEF-Philippines]] (2002-2017): Deployment of US SOF to southern Philippines to advise/assist in dismantling of Abu Sayyaf terrorist network.
- [[Operation Iraqi Freedom]] (2003-2010): US led invasion/occupation/counterinsurgency campaign in Iraq.
- [[Operation New Dawn]] (2010-2011): Marked the formal end of the combat phase known as OIF.
- [[Operation Neptune Spear]] (2011): Raid that killed Osama bin Laden in Abottabad, Pakistan.
- [[Operation Odyssey Dawn]] (2011): U.S. opening phase of the Libya intervention.
- [[Operation Juniper Shield]] (2012-Present): US counterterrorism support mission in the Sahara/Sahel.
- [[Operation Spartan Shield]] (2012-Present): A US CENTCOM mission that deters regional threats across the middle east.
- [[Operation Inherent Resolve]] (2014-Present): US-led coalition campaign against ISIS; now largely advisory.
- [[Operation Atlantic Resolve]] (2014-Present): US/NATO deterrence and reassurance mission in Europe after Russian invasion of Ukraine.
	- The actual funding program for Ukraine is called Ukraine Security Assistance Initiative (USAI), rather than a distinct operation name.
- [[Operation Freedom's Sentinel]] (2015-2021): Post-OEF Afghanistan mission; counterterrorism plus train/advise/assist.
- [[Operation Pacific Eagle]] (2017-Present): US support to the Philippines against IS-linked groups.
- [[Operation Allies Refuge]] (2021): US evacuation/airlift from Afghanistan for US personnel, allies, and at-risk Afghans.
- [[Operation Enduring Sentinel]] (2021-Present): Successor to Operation Freedom's Sentinel after Afghan withdrawal.
- [[Operation Poseidon Archer]] (2024-2025): US/UK strikes on Houthi targets in Yemen.
- [[Operation Southern Spear]] (2025-Present): US SOUTHCOM/Joint Task Force campaign against drug trafficking in western hemisphere.
- [[Operation Rough Rider]] (2025): Intensified US strike campaign against the Houthis in Yemen.
- [[Operation Midnight Hammer]] (2025): US strike on Iranian unclear facilities at Fordow/Natanz/Isfahan.
- [[Operation Hawkeye Strike]] (2025-2026): US retaliatory airstrike campaign against IS targets in Syria after the December 2025 Palmyra attack.
- [[Operation Absolute Resolve]] (2026): US military raid/strike operation in Venezuela that captured Nicolás Maduro.
- [[Operation Epic Fury]] (2026): Name for major US-Israeli campaign against Iran.
- [[Operation Project Freedom]] (2026): Name for short US operation to escort shipping through/near the Strait of Hormuz after the Iran crisis.
