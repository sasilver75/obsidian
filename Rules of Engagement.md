Special rules placing restraints on the use of force. 
Could be rules placing certain target off-limits, or rules requiring a certain level of identification before targets can be engaged.


A commander's tool to regulate the use of armed force in military oeprations, providing a framework encompassing national policy goals, mission requirements, and the law.
