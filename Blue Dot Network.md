A 2019 multilateral organization that promotes a certification framework for quality infrastructure projects, between the governments of Australia, Czech Republic, Japan, Spain, Switzerland, United Kingdom, and the United States, supporting invetsment in high-quality infrastructure projects around the world, especially by the private sector.
- Founders were Australia, Japan, United States

Seen a a copy of/answer to China's [[Belt and Road Initiative]], and is named after Carl Sagan's *Pale Blue Dot.*

The US International Development Finance Corporation managed to raise $60B for the project. 


After the perceived success of the BDN, In 2021, the G-7 announced the adoption of the Build Back Better World (B3W) initiative, which was later rebranded in 2022 to the [Partnership for Global Infrastructure and Investment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partnership_for_Global_Infrastructure_and_Investment) (PGII)... as of 2024, few details of the initiative have been announced.
