"If you look at a lot of past wars we've fought against large states, many of the taskings were not ATO taskings, they were [[Mission-Type Orders|MTO Tasking]]s... where you'd get an MTO-type task like 'Go to this area and attrit X type of thing by 25% over the next 24 hours' instead of 'drop X weapon on Y point'. With MTO Tasking, I now have to do a minimal [[Target Systems Analysis|TSA]]. These days, people hardly know what MTO is, because we haven't done that much over the last 20 years. We're not going to be able to micromanage every single sortie."
