# Maneuver, Fire Inventory

Ground Maneuver
- [[M1A2 Abrams]]: Current U.S. Army main battle tank used for armored maneuver, direct fire, and survivable close combat.
- [[M1E3 Abrams]]: Planned next-generation Abrams modernization intended to reduce weight and improve survivability, power, electronics, and growth capacity.
- [[M2 Bradley]] (IFV): Infantry fighting vehicle that carries infantry squads and provides direct fire with a 25mm cannon, machine gun, and anti-tank missiles.
- [[M3 Bradley]] (CFV): Cavalry fighting vehicle variant of the Bradley optimized for armored reconnaissance and security missions.
- [[M7 BFIST]]: Bradley Fire Support Team vehicle equipped with sensors and fire-support systems for forward observers and targeting teams.
- [[XM30 Mechanized Infantry Combat Vehicle]] (MICV / OMFV): Developmental next-generation infantry fighting vehicle intended to replace the Bradley in armored brigade combat teams.
- [[Armored Multi-Purpose Vehicle]] (AMPV): Tracked armored support vehicle family replacing M113 variants in armored formations for medical, command, mortar, and general-purpose roles.
- [[M113 Armored Personnel Carrier]] (APC): Legacy tracked armored personnel carrier and support-vehicle family being replaced in armored brigades by AMPV.
- [[Stryker]]: Eight-wheeled armored vehicle family used by Stryker brigade combat teams for infantry transport, reconnaissance, command, mortar, medical, and fire-support roles.
- [[M1296 Infantry Carrier Vehicle Dragoon]] (ICVD): Stryker infantry carrier variant upgraded with a 30mm cannon for greater direct-fire capability.
- [[Maneuver Short-Range Air Defense|M-SHORAD]]: Stryker-based short-range air-defense system used to protect maneuver forces from aircraft, helicopters, and unmanned aircraft.
- [[M1301 Infantry Squad Vehicle]] (ISV): Lightweight air-transportable vehicle that gives infantry squads faster tactical mobility without heavy armor.
- [[Joint Light Tactical Vehicle]] (JLTV, L-ATV): Protected light tactical vehicle replacing portions of the HMMWV fleet for mobility, command, weapons-carrier, and utility missions.
- [[HMMWV]] (Humvee): Legacy light tactical vehicle family used for transport, command-and-control, weapons carriage, ambulance, and utility roles.
- [[M-ATV]]: Mine Resistant Ambush Protected All-Terrain Vehicle designed for protected mobility in rough terrain and high-threat environments.
- [[M88 Hercules]]: Armored recovery vehicle used to recover, tow, and repair heavy tracked vehicles such as Abrams tanks.
- [[M1150 Assault Breacher Vehicle]] (ABV): Abrams-based engineer vehicle used to breach minefields, obstacles, and fortified positions with plows and line charges.
- [[M1074 Joint Assault Bridge System]] (JAB, JABS): Abrams-based armored bridge layer used to emplace assault bridges for armored maneuver forces.
- [[Heavy Expanded Mobility Tactical Truck]] (HEMTT): Heavy wheeled logistics truck family used for fuel, cargo, ammunition, recovery, and resupply missions.
- [[Palletized Load System]] (PLS): Heavy logistics truck system that moves flatracks, containers, ammunition, and sustainment loads using self-loading cargo handling.
- [[M1070]]: Heavy Equipment Transporter tractor used with trailers to move Abrams tanks and other heavy combat vehicles over long distances.
- [[Cougar]]: Mine Resistant Ambush Protected vehicle used for protected mobility and route-clearance support.
- [[Caiman]]: Mine Resistant Ambush Protected vehicle used for troop transport and protected mobility in blast-threat environments.
- [[M1124 MaxxPro]]: Mine Resistant Ambush Protected vehicle family used for protected troop movement, convoy operations, and route-security missions.
- [[Buffalo]]: Route-clearance Mine Resistant Ambush Protected vehicle equipped with an articulated arm for investigating and clearing suspected explosive hazards.
- [[RG-31 Nyala]]: Mine Resistant Ambush Protected vehicle used for protected patrol, convoy security, and explosive-hazard missions.

Fires
- [[M119|M119A3]]: Lightweight 105mm towed howitzer used for mobile cannon artillery support, especially in light and airborne formations.
- [[M777|M777A2]]: Lightweight 155mm towed howitzer used for medium-range cannon artillery fire support.
- [[M109 Paladin]]: Self-propelled 155mm howitzer used by armored brigade combat teams for mobile, protected artillery fires.
- [[M992 Field Artillery Ammunition Support Vehicle]] (FAASV, "Cat"): Tracked ammunition carrier that resupplies M109 Paladin batteries with artillery rounds and propellant.
- [[M270 Multiple Launch Rocket System]] (MLRS): Tracked rocket-artillery launcher used to fire GMLRS, ATACMS, and PrSM-family munitions.
- [[M142 High Mobility Artillery Rocket System]] (HIMARS): Wheeled rocket-artillery launcher used to fire precision rockets and long-range missiles with high tactical mobility.
- [[Guided Multiple Launch Rocket System]] (GMLRS, ER GMLRS): Precision-guided rocket family for HIMARS and MLRS launchers, with extended-range variants increasing reach.
- [[Army Tactical Missile System]] (ATACMS): Long-range surface-to-surface ballistic missile fired from HIMARS or MLRS launchers for deep precision strike.
- [[Precision Strike Missile]] (PrSM, "Prism"): Next-generation surface-to-surface missile replacing ATACMS for longer-range precision fires.
- [[Typhon Mid-Range Capability]] (MRC): Ground-launched mid-range fires system that gives the Army land-based Tomahawk and SM-6 missile capability.
- [[Long-Range Hypersonic Weapon]] (LRHW, "Dark Eagle"): Ground-launched hypersonic missile system intended for very long-range, high-speed strike against time-sensitive or defended targets.
- [[M224 Mortar]]: Lightweight 60mm mortar used by infantry units for close indirect fire support.
- [[M252 Mortar]]: 81mm medium mortar used by infantry units for longer-range indirect fire support than 60mm mortars.
- [[M120 Motar]] (M121): 120mm mortar system used for heavy battalion-level indirect fire support from ground-mounted or carrier-mounted configurations.
- [[M982 Excalibur]]: GPS-guided 155mm artillery projectile used for precision cannon fires.
- [[M1156 Precision Guidance Kit]] (PGK): Guidance fuze kit that improves the accuracy of conventional 155mm artillery projectiles.
- [[Advanced Field Artillery Tactical Data System]]: Digital fires command-and-control system used to plan, coordinate, approve, and execute artillery, rocket, mortar, naval, and air-delivered fires.
- [[ANTPQ-53|AN/TPQ-53]]: Counterfire radar used to detect, classify, track, and locate enemy rockets, artillery, and mortars.
- [[ANTPQ-50|AN/TPQ-50]]: Lightweight counterfire radar used to detect and locate enemy mortar, artillery, and rocket launches.

Air Defense
- [[MIM-104 Patriot]]: Army air and missile defense system used to defeat aircraft, cruise missiles, and tactical ballistic missiles.
- [[PAC-2 GEM-T]]: Patriot interceptor variant optimized for aircraft, cruise missile, and tactical ballistic missile defense using proximity-fuzed fragmentation effects.
- [[PAC-3 MSE]]: Hit-to-kill Patriot interceptor variant with improved range and maneuverability for ballistic missile and air-breathing threat defense.
- [[Terminal High Altitude Area Defense]] (THAAD): High-altitude ballistic missile defense system designed to intercept ballistic missiles in the terminal phase.
- [[Integrated Battle Command System]] (IBCS): Networked air and missile defense battle-management system that connects sensors, launchers, and command nodes across formations.
- [[Lower Tier Air and Missile Defense Sensor]] (LTAMDS, "GhostEye"): New Patriot-family air and missile defense radar designed to provide 360-degree sensing and improved threat tracking.
- [[ANMPQ-64 Sentinel|AN/MPQ-64 Sentinel]]: Mobile short-range air-defense radar used to detect and track aircraft, helicopters, cruise missiles, and unmanned aircraft.
- [[ANTWQ-1 Avenger|ANTWQ-1 Avenger]]: HMMWV-mounted short-range air-defense system armed with Stinger missiles.
- [[FIM-92 Stinger]]: Man-portable infrared-guided surface-to-air missile used for short-range defense against aircraft, helicopters, and unmanned aircraft.
- [[Enduring Shield]]: Army Indirect Fire Protection Capability launcher intended to defend against cruise missiles, rockets, artillery, mortars, and unmanned aircraft.
- [[Coyote]]: Small expendable interceptor and unmanned aircraft family used primarily for counter-unmanned aircraft missions.
- [[KuRFS]]: Ku-band radar system used for counter-unmanned aircraft and short-range air-defense target detection and tracking.

Aviation / UAS
- [[AH-64E Apache]]: Modern Army attack helicopter used for anti-armor, close combat attack, reconnaissance, security, and manned-unmanned teaming.
- [[AH-64D Apache Longbow]]: Earlier Apache variant with Longbow fire-control radar and digital avionics for attack and reconnaissance missions.
- [[UH-60M Black Hawk]]: Army utility helicopter used for air assault, troop transport, logistics, command-and-control, and general support.
- [[HH-60M Black Hawk]]: Medical evacuation Black Hawk variant configured for casualty evacuation and en route care.
- [[CH-47F Chinook]]: Heavy-lift cargo helicopter used to move troops, artillery, vehicles, supplies, and sling loads.
- [[MH-47G Chinook]]: Special operations Chinook variant with aerial refueling, advanced sensors, and mission systems for long-range infiltration and resupply.
- [[MH-60M Black Hawk]]: Special operations Black Hawk variant used by the 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment for infiltration, exfiltration, assault support, and precision missions.
- [[MH-60L Direct Action Penetrator]]: Heavily armed special operations Black Hawk gunship variant used for direct-action support.
- [[AH-6M Little Bird]]: Light special operations attack helicopter used for close air support, escort, and precision fires.
- [[MH-6M Little Bird]]: Light special operations assault helicopter used to insert and extract small teams in confined or urban terrain.
- [[UH-72 Lakota]]: Light utility helicopter used for training, domestic support, medical evacuation, and non-combat utility missions.
- [[MV-75 Cheyenne II]]: Future Army tiltrotor assault aircraft selected under Future Long-Range Assault Aircraft to replace portions of the Black Hawk fleet.
- [[MQ-1C Gray Eagle]] (Legacy): Army medium-altitude long-endurance unmanned aircraft used for intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance, targeting, and strike.
- [[RQ-7 Shadow]]: Tactical unmanned aircraft system used for brigade-level reconnaissance, surveillance, target acquisition, and communications relay.
- [[RQ-11B Raven]]: Small hand-launched unmanned aircraft used for short-range reconnaissance and surveillance.
- [[RQ-20 Puma]]: Small unmanned aircraft used for tactical reconnaissance, surveillance, and target observation.
- [[RQ-28A]]: Short-range reconnaissance quadcopter used by small units for local intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance.
- [[Black Widow]] Short Range Reconnaissance: Small-unit short-range reconnaissance unmanned aircraft intended to give squads and platoons organic aerial sensing.
- [[Switchblade 300]]: Small loitering munition used by dismounted units against personnel and light targets.
- [[Switchblade 600]]: Larger loitering munition used against armored vehicles and higher-value point targets.
- [[ME-11B HADES]]: Army high-altitude intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance, and electronic-sensing aircraft for deep sensing and targeting support.

Sensors
- Nett Warrior: Dismounted soldier situational-awareness system that connects leaders to digital maps, messaging, position data, and tactical networks.
- IVAS: Integrated Visual Augmentation System that combines night vision, augmented reality, navigation, targeting, and training functions for soldiers.
- ENVG-B / AN/PSQ-42: Enhanced Night Vision Goggle-Binocular that fuses image intensification and thermal imaging for improved night and obscured-visibility operations.
- AN/PVS-14: Monocular night-vision device used by soldiers for movement, observation, and aiming in low-light conditions.
- AN/PAS-13: Thermal weapon sight family used to detect and engage targets through darkness, smoke, and obscurants.
- LRAS3: Long Range Advanced Scout Surveillance System used by reconnaissance forces for long-range target detection, identification, and location.
- CROWS: Common Remotely Operated Weapon Station that lets crews aim and fire vehicle-mounted weapons from under armor.
- [[Joint Battle Command-Platform|JBC-P]] / [[Blue Force Tracking|Blue Force Tracker]]: Tactical command-and-control and friendly-force tracking systems that share unit positions, messages, and situational awareness.
- CPCE: Command Post Computing Environment system that provides a common digital mission-command workspace for Army command posts.
- DCGS-A: Distributed Common Ground System-Army intelligence system used to collect, process, exploit, analyze, and disseminate intelligence data.
- TITAN: Tactical Intelligence Targeting Access Node that fuses sensor data to support long-range targeting and deep-sensing operations.
- TLS-BCT / TLS-EAB: Terrestrial Layer System variants that provide electronic warfare, signals intelligence, and cyber/electromagnetic support at brigade and echelons-above-brigade levels.
- Electronic Warfare Planning and Management Tool: Army software tool used to plan, coordinate, deconflict, and manage electromagnetic warfare operations.
