A Highly Elliptical Orbit (HEO) is an elongated, oval-shaped Earth orbit. It features a very low [[Periapsis|Perigee]] (closest point to Earth, often under 1,000 km) and a very high [[Apogee]] (farthest point, exceeding 35,700 km). This shape allows satellites to "dwell" or linger over high-latitude polar regions where standard [[Geostationary Orbit]]s fail.
