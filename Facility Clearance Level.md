An administrative determination by the US government that a business or organization is eligible to access, store, or handle classified information.

There are three core FCL levels matching national classification standards:
- [[Confidential Classification|Confidential]]
- [[Secret Classification|Secret]]
- [[Top Secret Classification|Top Secret]]

An FCL applies to the business entity, not to an individual room or to an employee's personal clearance.
