https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIMPAC

The world's largest international maritime exercise, held every two years on even-numbered years from June 24 to July 31 around the Hawaiian Islands.
Hosted and administered by [[US Indo-Pacific Command|INDOPACOM]].

The event features 30 participating nations, over 40 warships and submarines, 140+ aircraft, and 30,000 personnel.
