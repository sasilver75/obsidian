A research and development agency of the US [[United States Department of Defense|DoD]] responsible for the eevelopment of emerging technologies for use by the military.
Originally known as the Advanced Research PRojects Agency (ARPA).


# Current Program Offices
- ==Defense Sciences Office== (DSO): Identifies and pursues high-payoff research initiatives across a broad spectrum of science and engineering disciplines (novel materials and structures, sensing and measurement, computation and processing, enabling operations, collective intelligence, global change).
- ==Information Innovation Office== (I2O): Aims to ensure US technological superiority in all areas where information can provide a decisive military advantage.
- ==Microsystems Technology Office== (MTO): Development of high-performance, intelligent microsystems and next-generation components to ensure US dominance in [[Command, Control, Communicatinos, Computers, Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance|C4ISR]], [[Electronic Warfare|EW]], [[Directed Energy]], which depend on microsystems and components.
- ==Strategic Technology Office== (STO): Focusing on technologies that have a global theater-wide impact and that involve multiple Services.
- ==Tactical Technology Office== (TTO): Engages in high-risk, high-payoff military research, emphasizing the "system" and "subsystem" approach to the development of aeronautic, space, and land systems, as well as embedded processors and control systems.
- ==Biological Technologies Office== (BTO): Fosters, demonstrates, and transitions breakthrough fundamental research, discoverings, applications integrating biology, engineering, and computer science for national security.
