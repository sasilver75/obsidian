A private venture, cheap [[Hypersonic Weapon|Hypersonic]] missile designed to be mass-produced and affordable by Chinese municipalities along the Chinese eastern seaborne.

In November 2025, the Beijing-based company Lingkong Tianxing released a promotional video of the Yukong YKJ-1000 missile via its own social media platform, claiming a speed of Mach 5.
