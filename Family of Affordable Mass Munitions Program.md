An [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] program to procure nearly 28,000 low-cost [[Air-Launched Cruise Missile]]s over five years for roughly $12.6B. Aims to buy thousands annually to scale production and expand stockpiles.
- Similar in some sense in spirit to the Army's [[Low-Cost Containerized Missiles Program]]
- Similar in some sense in spirit to the Navy's [[Coalition Heterogenous Affordable Offensive Strike Program]] (CHAOS)
