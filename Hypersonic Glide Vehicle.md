https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypersonic_glide_vehicle

A type of warhead for ballistic missiles that can maneuver and glide at sonic speed, used in conjunction with [[Ballistic Missile]]s to significantly change their trajectories after launch.
- Conventional ballistic missiles follow a predictable ballistic trajectory and are vulnerable to interception.
- The in-flight maneuverability of HGVs makes them unpredictable and hard to effectively target.
