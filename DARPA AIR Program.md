AIR = "Artificial Intelligence Reinforcements"

A program from [[Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency|DARPA]] that is a follow-up/successor to earlier dogfighting programs ([[DARPA ACE Program]])

> The Artificial Intelligence Reinforcements (AIR) program is developing dominant, AI-driven tactical autonomy for multi-ship, beyond visual range (BVR) air combat missions. Autonomy solutions will initially be developed and demonstrated on manned F-16 testbeds and then transferred to an unmanned combat aerial vehicle (UCAV).

The goal is to start operational testing in 2027.

Fundamentally AIR is addressing two technical areas:
1. Creating fast and accurate models that capture uncertainty and automatically improve with more data. Developing AI-driven algorithmic approaches that enable real-time, distributed autonomous tactical execution within uncertain, dynamic, and complex operational environments
2. The AIR program is also developing the hardware, computational processing, and software frameworks needed to rapidly design, test, and implement future iterations of advanced autonomous capabilities.

[[Lockheed Martin]] has been contracted for $4.6M to build surrogate models of aircraft, sensors, EW systems, and more, to create extremely realistic simulated environments for large-scale air combat training involving entire formations of platforms, instead of individual ones... for AI agents 

[[BAE Systems]] received $4M by supporting that endeavor, and improving simulation models of EW, kinematics of weapons, and physics of aerial maneuvers... and a rapid iteration process to produce fully-realistic simulations of new systems that come online in the new future.

==EpiSci== and ==PhysicsAI== (alumni of the DARPA dogfighting programs) were selected as a partnership to develop the actual algorithms for multi-agent autonomy and missionized team-based aircraft.
