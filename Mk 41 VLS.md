The world's most widely used universal naval missile launcher, distinguished by its centralized maganize and ability to fire varied weapons, from Standard Missiles to [[BGM-109 Tomahawk Land Attack Missile|Tomahawk]]s. It is a [[Vertical Launch System]] (VLS).


> Mk 41 VLS is available in different versions that vary in size and weight. There are three lengths. 209 in (5.3m) for the self-defense version, 266 in (6.8m) for the tactical version, and 303 in (7.7m) for the strike version.
