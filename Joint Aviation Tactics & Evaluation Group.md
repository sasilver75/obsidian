A secretive, invite-only staff organization within [[Joint Special Operations Command|JSOC]] studying aviation needs, sourcing specialized aircraft, and developing and testing new, covert aviation-based technologies for special missions units.

Deeply integrated with the Army's [[Aviation Technology Office]] (ATO), which manages clandestine aviation assets for [[1st Special Forces Operational Detachment-Delta|CAG]].
