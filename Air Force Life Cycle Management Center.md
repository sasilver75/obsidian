# Armament Directorate
- Designs, develops, produces, fields, and sustains a family of air-to-ground and air-to-air munitions for both US and allied nations to defeat a spectrum of enemy targets. The information they provide may be beneficial during weaponeering and conducting [[Munitions Effectiveness Assessment]] (MEA).
