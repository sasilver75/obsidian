A proposed trade agreement between 12 Pacific Rim countries, negotiated under Obama and signed by the US in 2016 under Biden (but negotiated since 2008), though Trump withdrew the United State from it in January 2017, ensuring it could not be ratified as required and did not enter force.

Signatories:
- Australia
- Brunei
- Canada
- Chile
- Japan
- Malaysia
- Mexico
- New Zealand
- Peru
- Singapore
- Vietnam

The largest economy in the Pacific Rim not involved was China (the benefits to China are "intangible"/not powerful enough); China reacted by accelerating its own trade initiatives in Asia.

In 2018, the eleven remaining countries in the TPP went on to sign a revised version of the agreement, known as the [[Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership]] (CPTPP).
 
The Biden Administration "was not able" to reinstate it (?), but did launch the [[Indo-Pacific Economic Framework]] in 2022.
