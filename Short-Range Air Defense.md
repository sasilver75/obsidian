A group of anti-aircraft weapons and tactics used to protect ground forces, bases, and infrastructure from low-altitude aerial threats like Helicopters, low-flying Fixed Wing aircraft, [[Cruise Missile]]s, and [[Unmanned Autonomous System|UAS]] systems.

Because these threats operate at close range and often utilize [[Nap-of-the-Earth|Terrain Masking]]/NOE, SHORAD systems need to be highly mobile and quick to react.



Systems range from:
- [[Man-Portable Air Defense System]]s like [[FIM-92 Stinger]]
- [[Maneuver Short-Range Air Defense|M-SHORAD]], the Army platform which mounts an Avenger air defense turret on a Stryker vehicle, perhaps using [[FIM-92 Stinger]]s and [[AGM-114 Hellfire]] missiles, a 30mm automatic cannon, or a 7.62mm machine gun.
- [[DE M-SHORAD]] (Directed Energy): Increment 2 of the M-SHORAD program, uses a 50kW laser on a Stryker vehicle.
