F-35A: [[Conventional Takeoff and Landing]] (CTOL) variant tailored for standard runways, operated primarily by the [[United States Air Force|Air Force]]
F-35B: [[Short Takeoff and Vertical Landing]] (STOVL) variant, with a swivel-nozzle and lift fan, letting it operate from amphibious ships and austere airfields. Primarily designed for the [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] and international allies.
F-35C: Carrier Variant (CV) designed specifically for [[United States Navy|Navy]] aircraft carriers, featuring larger folding wings, a tailhook, and heavy-duty landing gear.
