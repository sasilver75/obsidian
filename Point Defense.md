
Close-range protection *of a specific asset, unit, or small location against threats that have reached the asset's immediate vicinity.*

A point might be a warship, airfield, command post, radar installation, armored vehicle, bridge or ammunition depot.

Point defense is commonly the final decisive layer, attempting to track and destroy an incoming aircraft/missile/rocket/mortar round before it strikes a protecte weapon.

It contrasts with [[Area Defense]], which protects a broader region, or multiple dispersed assets.


# Comparison with [[Short-Range Air Defense]] (SHORAD)
- Point defense describes the defensive mission and protected footprint, while SHORAD describes a category of air-defense capability based largely on engagement range and target environment. 
	- A short-range weapon can be used for point defense, but it (e.g. a [[FIM-92 Stinger]]) can also be used to protect a maneuvering formation, rather than a fixed point.
- Note that Point Defense ==does not necessarily mean air defense;== naval torpedo defenses and armored-vehicle active protection systems can also perform point defense.
