
ACV-P: "Amphibious Combat Vehicle - Personnel"

The primary, eight-wheeled ship-to-shore troop transport designed to replace legacy AAVs. Part of a larger ACV family ([[ACV-R]], [[ACV-C]], [[ACV-P]], [[ACV-30]]).
Manufactured by [[BAE Systems]], carries a 3-person crew and 13 combat-loaded Marines, and can swim up to 12 nautical miles from naval ships to the beach.
