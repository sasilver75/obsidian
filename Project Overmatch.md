A high-priority [[United States Navy|Navy]] initiative to develop a resilient, software-driven naval operational architecture that connects sensors, shooters, and commanders across all domains.

Serves as the Navy's contribution to the Pentagon's [[Combined Joint All-Domain Command and Control]] (CJADC2).

Objectives:
- All-domain integration of ships, submarines, aircraft, ground forces, and uncrewed autonomous systems into a single cohesive network.
- Use of modern data architectures, cloud-hosted analytics, and the [[Overmatch Software Armory]] to rapidly field commercial and custom code.
- Decision advantage to empower distributed maritime operations by speeding up execution of effects chains in contested environments.

> While the project is already six years into development ((started 2020)), it is far from being rolled out successfully, with spending being allocated through FY 2029. The recent [[Exercise Rim of the Pacific|RIMPAC]] 2026 military exercise tested these integrations at scale, and was a crucial step towards fielding the technology in a real-world scenario.
