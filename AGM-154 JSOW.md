JSOW = "Joint Standoff Weapon

In Service: 1998 - Present

A  [[Glide Bomb]] resulting from a joint venture between the [[United States Navy|Navy]] and [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] to deploy a standardized medium-range precision-guided weapon.

Intended to be used against soft targets such as parked aircraft, trucks, armored personnel carriers, and surface to air missile sites.

Uses a tightly-coupled [[GPS INS|GPS/INS]] for navigation, capable of day/night and adverse weather operations.

### Variants
- AGM-154A (baseline JSOW): Warhead is 145 [[BLU-97B Combined Effects Bomb|BLU-97/B Combined Effects Bomb]] (CEB) submunitions, with a shaped charge for armor-defeat and a fragmenting case for material destruction, and a zirconium ring for incendiary effects.
- AGM-154B (anti armor): The warhead is 6x [[BLU-108|BLU-108/B]] from the [[CBU-97 Sensor Fuzed Weapon]] program, each of which releases four projectiles that use infrared sensors to detect targets. The program concluded development but the Navy didn't procure it.
- AGM-154C (unitary variant): Uses an infrared seeker with autonomous guidance, carrying the BROACH warhead, designed to attack hardened targets. Entered service in February 2005.

# Production and Upgrades
- JSOW Block III (JSOW-C1): Declared [[Initial Operating Capability|IOC]] in October 2017.
- AGM-154A-1 (JSOW-A1): Replaces the [[BLU-97B Combined Effects Bomb|BLU-97/B CEB]] ([[Unexploded Ordnance|UXO]] problems) with the [[BLU-111]] warhead.
- Powered JSOW (JSOW-ER): A [[Turbojet]] engine for a powered JSOW [[Cruise Missile]].
