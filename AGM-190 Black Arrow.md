Known as "Havoc Spear" by [[Air Force Special Operations Command|AFSOC]] and "Black Arrow" by manufacturer [[Leidos]].
- Development began in 2021 with the Small Cruise Missile program from AFSOC, an effort to build "affordable mass" in munitions.
- Leidos had some experience already rapidly fielding the [[GBU-69B]] (Small Glide Munition); Black Arrow's intended scalability utilizes the same open system architecture and modular airframe practices as the GBU-69, of which Leidos had delivered 4,000 units as of April 2025.


A low-cost small [[Cruise Missile]] designed to carry both warheads and non-kinetic payloads, fireable from a variety of platforms, including palletized drop (e.g. via [[Dragon Cart]]), [[Ramp Launch Tube]]s, or conventionally launched from a stores pylon on a fixed-wing aircraft.

Meant to be a mission-adaptable delivery platform to be produced at scale, in large quantities.

Weighs ~200lbs, operational range in excess of 400 nautical miles.
