A [[United States Department of Defense|DoD]] program intended to pioneer ways to cheaply produce large amount of weapons or systems for the military.

It was announced on August 23, 2023 as a means of quickly producing weapons to deter and counter China, and that the first weapons produced under Replicator were going to be autonomous systems.

The Replicator program announced its first batch on contracts on May 6, 2024, which included purchases of uncrewed watercraft, aerial drones, and anti-drone defenses of "various sizes and payloads from several traditional and non-traditional vendors."
- Other contracts remain classified, including some in the maritime domain and some in the [[Counter-Unmanned Aerial System|C-UAS]] portfolio.

> "While the first phase, Replicator 1, was focused on deploying thousands of autonomous systems across multiple domains, Replicator 2 is specifically aimed at countering the threat posed by small UAS."

On September 30, 2024, Defense Secretary Lloyd Austin III announced a new phase of the program, dubbed ==Replicator 2,== that would focus on anti-drone defense systems.
- Joint Interagency Task Force 401 announced Jan 11, 2026 its first acquisition under the Replicator 2 initiative, a contract for two advanced DroneHunter F700 systems, delivered by April.
	- The DroneHunter is a reusable, artificial intelligence-driven interceptor drone that provides a unique and effective solution to counter small UAS, especially in settings where personnel, infrastructure and surrounding activity require careful control of effects.  


> Some of the money from Replicator went to existing products, and others to projects in flights that were basically reoriented towards the replicator program.
