Head of the [[Department of Defense]], appointed by the [[President of the United States]].
A member of the [[National Security Council]].








There are currently six undersecretaries, overseeing distinct portfolios:
1. Under Secretary of War for Policy
2. Under Secretary of War for Acquisition and Sustainment
3. Under Secretary of War for Personnel and Readiness
4. Under Secretary of War for Intelligence and Security
5. Under Secretary of War for Research and Engineering
6. Under Secretary of War (Comptroller) / Chief Financial Officer
