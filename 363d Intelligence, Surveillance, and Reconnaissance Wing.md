 > The [[United States Air Force|USAF]]’s lead wing for [[Targeting]]. It provides operations planning and execution support to major commands, component numbered air forces, and theater AOCs. The 363 ISRG, formerly known as the Air Force Targeting Center, executes targeting functions, including intelligence and nonlethal targeting support, through end-to-end analysis and production. Subordinate 363 ISRG units also include Air Force Reserve Command and Air National Guard targeting units that provide steady-state and surge-to-war target production and analysis capability accomplished through the use of imagery analysts, targeteering analysts, signals analysts, all-source analysts, and intelligence applications. The 363 ISRW also executes additional targeting functions through the 365 ISRG, including exercise support and direct intelligence support to United States Air Force Central Command (USAFCENT).
