Through its national laboratories, provides significant [[Chemical. Biological, Radiological, and Nuclear|CBRN]] analysis data related to facilities and installations associated with the proliferation of [[Weapons of Mass Destruction|WMD]].

Can also provide prediction models and assist in consequence analysis of CBRN threats. 

The Department's Office of Intelligence and CI provides timely technical intelligence analysis supporting policymakers, national security missions in defense, homeland security, cyber security, intelligence, and energy security.
