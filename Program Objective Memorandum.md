A service or defense agency's proposed program plan inside the [[Planning, Programming, Budgeting, and Execution|PPBE]] process.

A DoD component's fiscally constrained proposal for allocating resources across a multiyear period.

Military departments/defense agencies use the POM to recommend the forces, acquisition programs, personnel, operations, infrastructure, and support activities they believe are necessary to implement strategic guidance.

A POM therefore expresses priorities and tradeoffs: increasing resources for one program normally requires reducing another program, accepting additional risk, or requesting a higher funding topline.

A principal product of PPBE
