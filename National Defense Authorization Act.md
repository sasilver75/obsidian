The major annual defense policy bill.

It setes policy, authorizes programs, sets personnel end strength, and recommends funding levels, but usually does not provide spendable money by itself.



Some items (e.g. the [[Neros Technologies|Neros]] Archer drone) need to be "NDAA-compliant" in order for customers (e.g. the Marine Corps) to purchase them.
