An [[United States Army|Army]]-developed mission management software used by joint and coalition forces that can reside on all classified networks and facilitate the entire [[Joint Targeting Cycle]].

Provides a suite of tools and interfaces that enable nominating, vetting, assigning, and planning cross-component, multi-domain dynamic targeting missions. 
- Can also build target lists for the [[Deliberate Targeting]] process.
- Interfaces with systems and applications such as the [[Global Command and Control System]] (GCCS) family of systems, the [[Theater Battle Management Core System]] (TBMCS), [[Modernized Integrated Database|MIDB]], and certain coalition systems.
- Provides integration between multiple joint [[Command and Control|C2]] systems of the uniformed services involved in the targeting process, including the [[Air Operations Center]] (AOC).
- JADOCS increases situational awareness from [[Unified Combatant Command|CCMD]] [[Joint Fires Element|JFE]] through component fires, coordinating an executive level update of the target, including the estimated and actual times on target as well as a [[Common Operating Picture]].

In the [[Air Operations Center|AOC]], [[Joint Automated Deep Operations Coordination System|JADOCS]] is typically used by the dynamic targeting cell, the PR cell, and the SIDO team in the [[Combat Operations Division]] of the AOC.
