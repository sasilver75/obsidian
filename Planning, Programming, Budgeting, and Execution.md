The primary framework used by the [[Department of Defense|Department of Defense]] to turn strategy into a concrete multi-year budget/spending plan.

A [[Program Objective Memorandum]] (POM) is a service or defense agency's proposed program plan inside of PPBE.

A continuous overlapping cycle, rather than a four-step sequence. While one budget is being executed, another is being defended before Congress and last-year programs are being planned.
