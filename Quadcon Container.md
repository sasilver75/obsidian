

A modular, military-grade mini-container measuring roughly 5' x 8' x 8'6"

Four of these units lock together with connectors to match the exact dimensions and shipping footprints of a single 20' [[ISO Container]].
- In the same way, it takes three [[Tricon Container]]s to match that 20' ISO Container footprint.
