The COFA states (or Freely Associated States) are three sovereign Pacific Island nations in a special treaty relationship with the U.S.: the Federated States of Micronesia (FSM), the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI), and the Republic of Palau.

> "The COFA states... are more like a highway from the West Coast of the United States through to Asia. They are of high geostrategic importance."

> "Kwajalein Atoll in the Marshall Islands, where US Army Garrison Kwajalein Atoll is located... has a lot of facilities that have to do with the way that geostationed satellites are launched. IF you launch from Kazakhstan or Central China or North Korea, just the way they launch, they tend to go over a little spot in the middle of the Pacific as your first look, so those kinds of things become very important."
