Occupied a significant amount of territory in Iraq and Syria from 2013 to 2016, but lost most of it between 2017 and 2019.
In 2014, the group proclaimed itself to be a worldwide caliphate and claimed religious and political authority over all Muslims worldwide.

Its affiliates maintain a significant territorial presence in the [[Sahel]] region of Africa as of 2025.

It also has ISIS-East Asia (ISIS-EA), or Dawlah Islamiyah/Maute Group in the Philippines, Malaysia, and New Zealand which has clashed on several occasions with the Armed Forces of the Philippines. Even after the defeat at the siege of Marawi in 2017, it resurged in 2023 under the name Dawlah Islamiyah. It's possible that it might be defunct as of May 2026, though even the prior month, ten militants were killed in a raid in Marantao, Lanao del Sur.
