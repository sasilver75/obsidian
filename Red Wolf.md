In Jan 2026, [[L3Harris]] was selected to manufacture Red Wolf vehicles for the [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] Precision Attack Strike Munition (PASM) program, filling a gap in modern warfare with long-range precision weapon capabilities.

Extends the range of weapons launched from [[Vertical Takekoff and Landing|VTOL]] platforms to proven distances of 200 nautical miles (230 miles), far exceeding the single-digit range of other rocket-launched missiles. The system's [[Beyond Visual Line of Sight]] 

L3 Harris calls it a vehicle as well as a launched effect, which is a catch-all term for unmanned systems designed to do all sorts of things (kinetic effects, electronic warfare, watch a target, or be a decoy).

> "Our proven Red Wolf system can bring affordable mass to the Marine's arsenal of advanced modern munitions within the timeline that US official have outlined to support the most lethal fighting force in the world."
> - Christopher Kubasik, Chairman and CEO L3Harris

Can be launched from air, ground, or maritime platforms (the only launched effect to have successfully deployed from a [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] [[AH-1Z Viper]]). 

Red Wolf is part of a "wolf pack" of launched effects, from precision kinetic strike and EW to target tracking ,decoy, communications, and more.
- ==Red Wolf==: Kinetic precision strike
- ==Green Wolf==: [[Electronic Warfare|Electronic Attack]] and [[Detect, Identify, Locate, and Report]] (DILR)
