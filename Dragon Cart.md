Developed by the [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] research lab and [[Lockheed Martin]] under the name "Rapid Dragon"

A palletized and disposable weapons module airdropped to deploy flying munitions, typically [[Cruise Missile]]s, from unmodified cargo planes.

In service 2021-Present

The pallets, called "deployment boxes," provide a ==low-cost method allowing unmodified cargo planes== like the [[C-130 Hercules]] or [[C-17 Globemaster III]] ==to be temporarily repurposed as standoff bombers== capable of mass-launching any variant of long or short range [[AGM-158 JASSM]] cruise missiles against land or naval targets.

The size of the deployment boxes are configurable, ==ranges from 4 to 45 [[AGM-158 JASSM|AGM-158B JASSM-ER]] cruise missiles==.

The system can be thought of fas a smart and disposable bomb bay in a box that includes an interface allowing targeting information gathered from allied units in the area to be fed to the munitions from a distant fire control center.

"Future development will generalize the system beyond the AGM-158 missile family to include JDAM bombs, sea mines, drones, and other missile systems."
- Note that in 2026, the Air Force has begun a five-year program called [[Family of Affordable Mass Munitions]] (FAMM) to stockpile 28,000 compatible munitions with an allocated $12 billion. These FAMM-related cruise missiles can be mixed with JASSM munitions for saturation attacks with the higher end missiles focused on priority targets.
- There's also ongoing research re: integrating Boeing's lower cost but shorter range 

> Due to the vulnerability of sophisticated air defense systems such as [[S-300]] and [[S-400 Triumf|S-400]] to mass attacks from low flying cruise missiles, it is thought that Rapid Dragon is well suited for [swarm tactics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarming_\(military\) "Swarming (military)") missions to [suppress enemy air defenses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suppression_of_Enemy_Air_Defenses "Suppression of Enemy Air Defenses") with large numbers of [[AGM-158 JASSM|AGM-158B JASSM-ER]] optionally accompanied by swarms of miniature spoofing decoy drones released from a second airdrop module.
