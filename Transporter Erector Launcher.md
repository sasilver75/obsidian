A missile vehicle with an integrated tractor unit that can transport, elevate to a firing position, and launch one or more missiles.

Used for both surface-to-air missiles as well as surface-to-surface missiles.

Early on, such missiles were launched from fixed sites and had to be loaded onto trucks for transport, making them more vulnerable to attack.
