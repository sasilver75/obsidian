Chartered in 1968 to serve as the [[Department of Defense|DoW]]'s focal point for munitions effectiveness information.

Under the oversight of the [[Secretary of Defense|SECDEF]]'s Office of the Director for Operational Test and Evaluation, this program develops, advances, and sustains modern targeting software.

These tools, frequently referred to as [[Joint Munitions Effectiveness Manual|JMEM]] products, are used to develop operational effectiveness and [[Collateral Damage Estimation|CDE]]s and optimize all *non-nuclear* munitions for the DoW.
