MICV = "Mechanized Infantry Combat Vehicle"
OMFV = "Optionally Manned Fighting Vehicle" (Previous name for the MICV program)

A program to replace the [[M2 Bradley]] IFV.
- [[Rheinmetall]] and [[General Dynamics]] selected to go forward in the competition in 2023.
