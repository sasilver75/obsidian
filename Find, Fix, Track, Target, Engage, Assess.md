- Find: detect the existence of a possible target
- Fix: determine the target’s location and identity well enough to act
- Track: maintain custody of the target
- Target: decide what effect is desired and what capability should create that effect
- Engage: execute the action
- Assess: determine what happened

Example
> A mobile missile launcher is detected after firing. Sensors find it, analysts and systems fix its identity/location, ISR tracks it, the fires cell decides whether and how to engage it, a shooter engages, and battle damage assessment determines whether the launcher was destroyed, escaped, or needs re-attack.



> Aside: This is easy to confuse with [[Find, Fix, Finish, Exploit, Analyze, Dissmeinate|F3EAD]] on the acronym side. Just remember that F2T2EA is much more... conventional, in nature? You can think about air sorties when you think about F2T2EA. F3EAD is more common in SOF, where you can think about hitting houses, and the EAD process is rifling through someone's computer, finding their friend's address, and sharing this information to other (e.g.) active squadrons/troops that can quickly take action on it.
