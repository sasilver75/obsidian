_Pī Lì-10_, lit. "Thunderbolt-10"
Formerly known as the PL-ASR (PiLi-Advanced Short Range)

A short-range infrared-homing, active radar homing air-to-air missile, in service since 2015.
"It is the latest-generation Chinese short-range air-to-air missile."

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PL-10


Variants:
- PL-10E: Export version (e.g. Pakistan)
- PL-10 Active Radar: IIR seeker replaced with a miniature active radar.
