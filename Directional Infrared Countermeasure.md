See also: [[Infrared Countermeasure]] (IRCM)

These are designed to protect aircraft from heat-seeking (infrared) missiles by actively tracking them and injected modulated laser energy directly into the missile's seeker to disrupt its guidance system, "dazzling" it.

These are "soft-kill" electronic countermeasures; instead of physically destroying the missile ("hard kill"), a DIRCM relies on the EM spectrum, using high-powered lasers to emit specific, encoded frequencies that blind or confuse the infrared sensors on an incoming missile.
