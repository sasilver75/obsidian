Refers to the next higher [[Army Echelons|Echelon]] of command that directly oversees and supports a subordinate unit or [[Commanding Officer]].

The ISIC is responsible for the unit's administrative, tactical, and material readiness.

Can be both a specific person and the headquarters organization they command, depending on the context of the conversation.
- It technically refers to the [[Commanding Officer]] of the next higher unit, but service members frequently use it to refer to that person's entire headquarters staff.
