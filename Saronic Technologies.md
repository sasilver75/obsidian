Develops a number of [[Unmanned Surface Vehicle]]s designed for defense, surveillance, strike, and at-sea logistics:
- [[Saronic Corsair]]: A 24-foot autonomous sea drone that can travel up to 35 knots, cover over 1,000 nautical miles, and carry payloads of up to 1,000 pounds. 
- [[Saronic Mirage]]:  A 40-foot vessel with 2,000-pound payload capacity and 2,000 nautical mile range.
- [[Saronic Cipher]]: A 60-foot vessel capable of carrying a 10,000-pound payload with a range of up to 3,000 nautical miles
- [[Saronic Marauder]]: An 180-foot vessel built entirely for autonomy, featuring a large a payload deck designed to host multiple [[ISO Container]]s and delivering heavy capabilities across complex naval environments.
