Formerly called Prompt Global Strike (PGS)

A military effort to develop a system that can deliver a precision-guided *conventional weapon strike* anywhere in the world within one hour, in a similar manner to a nuclear [[Intercontinental Ballistic Missile|ICBM]].

The development history (since to 2000s) had included fears that a submarine-launched ballistic missile would trigger the Russian nuclear-launch warning system, possibly provoking nuclear war (an enemy can't be sure a launched ICBM has a conventional warhead).
- It possibly being a [[Hypersonic Glide Vehicle]] is important, and that it flies in an endo-atmospheric (in atmosphere, <100km altitude), non-ballistic trajectory, which is much flatter and lower than a normal ICBM, is important to prevent other nuclear-armed nations from mistakenly thinking it's a nuclear-tipped missile.

In 2023, the [[United States Navy|Navy]] awarded [[Lockheed Martin]] a contract to integrate the Conventional Prompt Strike weapon system onto the [[Zumwalt-Class Destroyer]]s.
