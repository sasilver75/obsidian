In service: 2001-Present with the [[United States Air Force|USAF]]
- ==The USAF plans to retire its Global Hawks in 2027.==

A high-altitude [[Unmanned Autonomous System|UAS]] introduced in 2001.

Provides a broad overview and systematic surveillance using high-resolution [[Synthetic Aperture Radar]] (for [[Moving Target Indication|MTI]]) and [[Optical|EO]]/[[Infrared|IR]] sensors with long loiter times over target areas.

Cost overruns led to the original plan to acquire 63 aircraft being cut to 45.

The initial cost of the first 10 aircraft was $10M in 1994 dollars, but by 2001, this had risen to $50.9M ($103M in 2024 dollars).

The [[United States Navy|Navy]] has developed the Global Hawk into the [[MQ-4C Triton]] maritime surveillance platform.
