https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islands_Forum

An inter-governmental organization aiming to enhance cooperation among countries and territories of [[Oceania]]:
- Australia
- Cook Islands
- Fiji
- French Polynesia
- Kiribati
- Marshall Islands
- Micronesia
- Nauru
- New Caledonia
- New Zealand
- Niue
- Palau
- Papua New Guinea
- Samoa
- Solomon Islands
- Tonga
- Tuvalu
- Vanatu

Then there are a number of associate members (e.g. Guam, American Samoa, ...), Observers (Timor Leste, World Bank, UN Secretariat), Dialogue Partners (Canada, EU, US, ...), and a Development Partner (Taiwan)
