The [[Air Operations Center]] (AOC) that executes [[US Strategic Command|STRATCOM]]'s [[Air Tasking Cycle]] through all phases of conflict.

It provides the [[Joint Force Air Component Commander|JFACC]] with assessments of operational-level plans and target development for global bomber missions.

It also provides situational awareness of all USSTRATCOM airborne/alert aircraft through all phases of conflict. Other responsibilities include building enduring processes with the geographic CCMDs and other functional AOCs through planning and integration to enable unity of effort in support of military and national security operations.
