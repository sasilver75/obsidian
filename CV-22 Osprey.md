CV-22B is the specific, final production model.

CMV-22B Is the [[United States Navy|Navy]]'s tiltrotor aircraft designed for the Carrier Onboard Delivery (COD) mission. Transports high-priority cargo (up to ~6,000lbs), mail, and personnel from shore bases directly to aircraft carrier flight decks.
