A clear, tactical targeting objective assigned to fire support systems (E.g. artillery, mortars, or air support) to support the ground commander's maneuver plan. 

Every essential fire support task is built using four core elements:
1. ==Task==: What the fires must do to the enemy (e.g. disrupt, delay, limit, or destroy)
2. ==Purpose==: The friendly-focused "why," explaining how the fires supports the maneuvers plan
3. ==Method==: How the fires will be executed, including the ==TTLODAC== breakdown
	- Target
	- Trigger
	- Location
	- Observer
	- Delivery system
	- Attack guidance
	- Communications
4. ==Effect==
	- The desired quantitative or qualitative result on the target (e.g. casualties, percent of neutralization)
