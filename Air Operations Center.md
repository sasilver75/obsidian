A type of [[Command and Control]] center used by the [[United States Air Force|USAF]], and the senior element of the [[Theater Air Control System]] (TACS). The [[Joint Force Commander]] (JFC) assigns a [[Joint Force Air Component Commander]] (JFACC) to lead the AOC.

When there is more than one U.S. military service working in an AOC (e.g. [[United States Navy|Navy]] aviation), it is called a [[Joint Air Operations Center]] (JAOC). When allies operate in tandem with the USAF, it's called a [[Coalition Air Operations Center|Combined Air Operations Center]] (CAOC).


To facilitate airpower operations, the AOC relies on the expertise of joint and coalition teams within five divisions. These five organizations fuse information that eventually becomes the [[Air Tasking Order]]:
- [[Strategy Division]] (SRD)
	- Includes Strategy Plans Team (SPT), Strategy Guidance Team (SGT), Operational Assessment Team (OAT), Information Operations Team (IOT).
- [[Combat Plans Division]] (CPD)
	- Target Effects Team, Master Air Attack Plan Team, Air Tasking Order Production Team, Command and Control Planning Team
- [[Combat Operations Division]] (COD)
	- Chief of Combat Operations (CCO), Offensive Ops Team, Defensive Ops Team, ISR Operations Team, Joint Personnel Recovery Cell, Interface Control, Weather Specialty Team, Cyber Liaison Team, Naval Amphibious Liaison Element, Battlefield Coordination Development, Special Operations Liaison Element
- [[Air Mobility Division]] (AMD)
	- AMD Chief, Deputy AMD Chief, Superintendent, Air Mobility Control Team (ACMT), Airlift Control Team (ALCT), Air Refueling Control Team (ARCT), Aeromedical Evacuation Control Team (AECT), Unique Missions Support Team (AMDU).
- [[Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance Division]] (ISRD)
	- Analysis, Correlation and Fusion, Targeting and Tactical Assessment, Imagery Support Element (ISE), ISR Plans
