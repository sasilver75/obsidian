Each [[Unified Combatant Command|Combatant Command]] ([[Unified Combatant Command|CCMD]]) stands for Combatant Commander. 
They are four-star generals or admirals responsible for commanding a Unified Combatant Command, overseeing all military operations and forces within a specific geographic region (e.g. [[US Indo-Pacific Command|Pacific Command]]) or a functional area (e.g. [[US Cyber Command|Cyber Command]]).


When someone deploys over seas, they're being temporarily loaned out to a combatant commander, falling under their [[Operational Control]]. Administratively ([[Administrative Control|ADCON]]) they still belong to their home unit. When that service member is at home, that home unit has both [[Operational Control|OPCON]] and [[Administrative Control|ADCON]] of that member.
