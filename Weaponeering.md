`The process of deciding what weapon, delivery method, quantity, and employment conditions are needed to create a desired effect on a target.
- The process of matching a target and desired outcome to a feasible weapon solution

Answers questions like:
- What effect is required (destroy, disable, suppress, delay, crater, etc.)
- What target characteristics matter (hardness, size, structure, mobility, redundancy, surroundings)
- What weapon is suitable (kinetic, non-kinetic, precision-guided, unguided, electronic, cyber, etc.)
- How many weapons or attacks are likely required?
- What are the risks of failure, collateral damage, fratricide, escalation, or legal/policy violation?

Usually, weaponeering is one part of the broader [[Targeting]] process.
- Targeting asks: "What should be affected, and why?"
- Weaponeering asks: "Given that decision, what means could plausibly produce the intended effect?"


> A planner may need to disable an enemy radar site without damaging nearby civilian infrastructure. Weaponeering would compare possible options:  power, jamming the radar, attacking a command link, or using a cyber effect. The “best” option depends on the desired duration of disablement, available assets, legal constraints, risk to civilians, and how quickly the radar can be repaired.
