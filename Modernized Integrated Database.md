MIDB is DoW's authoritative, all-source repository of worldwide general military and target intelligence.

The [[Defense Intelligence Agency]] is the worldwide functional manager for MIDB.

The MIDB architecture consists of databases that continuously replicate worldwide amongst hundreds of nodes on a variety of networks and between different security levels. This architecture provides the infrastructure for data and exchange between intelligence and operational consumers from national to tactical levels.

MIDB provides a baseline source of intelligence on installations, facilities, military forces, population concentrations, C2 structures, equipment, and target details.

MIDB is being replaced by the DIA's new [[Machine-assisted Analytic Rapid-repository System]] (MARS). They're operating concurrently during the transition.
