A [[Class III USV|MDUSV]] (Class III) operated by the [[United States Navy|Navy]].
- ==I believe it's a singular vessel?==
	- It seems that a second vessel "Seahawk" was built in 2021.

Launched in 2016 as part of the DARPA Anti-Submarine Warfare Continuous Trail Unmanned Vessel (ACTUV) program. In 2018, handed off to the [[Office of Naval Research]] (ONR).

Designed and built by [[Leidos]]

Fully fueled with 14,000 gallons of diesel, enough to go from San Diego to Guam and back to Pearl Harbor on a tank of gas.

Survivable through [[Sea State]] 7, seas up to 20ft high.

Has a trimaran hull, which increases stability without requiring a weighted keel, giving her higher capacity for linear trajectories and better operations in shallow waters, though the greater width decreases maneuverability.

Using Sea Hunter and Seahawk as ==prototypes==  for research and experimentation efforts, the to determine the most effective way to use USVs in operations, and how to best integrate them into the fleet.
