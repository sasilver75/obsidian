The Ramp Launch Tube has become a major capability for transforming standard cargo aircraft into standoff missile carriers.
- A gravity-fed deployment system used to drop tube-launched munitions such as the [[AGM-190 Black Arrow]] directly from the cargo ramps of military aircraft. Once munitions slide out of the rear of the aircraft via gravity or deployment chute, the munitions tail fins deploy, its wings extend into a flight position, and it guides itself to the target.
- Lets aircraft like the [[AC-130J Ghostrider]] to perform strikes without requiring external pylons.

Notable deployments/systems include:
- [[AGM-190 Black Arrow]]: A 200-pound class small [[Cruise Missile]] frequently integrated with RLTs; once ejected tail-first from the tube, the missile's fines deploy and the engine ignites mid-air.
- The Harvest HAWK KC-130J (retired in 2024) used an integrated pressurized "Derringer door" on the cargo ramp to launch and reload standoff munitions quickly.
- CLT-4 Launcher: A specialized aluminum missile cradle for rapid alignment and launching of [[Common Launch Tube]]s (CLTs)
- [[Dragon Cart]]: An alternate but related palletized airdrop system.
