These merge the picture of what has been done ([[Battle Damage Assessment]]; BDA) with how  it was done ([[Munitions Effectiveness Assessment]]; MEA) and compares the result with [[Measures of Effectiveness]] (MOE).

These typically come from the [[Air Operations Center|AOC]]'s [[Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance Division]]'s (ISRD's) [[Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance Division|Targets and Tactical Assessment]] (TTA) team.
