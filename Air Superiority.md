See also: [[Air Supremacy]], [[Air Parity]]


> That degree of control of the air by one force that permits the conduct of its operations at a given time and place ==without prohibitive== interference from air and missile threats.


Note that control of the air isn't always the same over the entire area of operations; it can differ depending on where you are.

> "Control of the air is a necessary pre-condition for control of the surface."
