A [[Group 5 UAS|Group 5]] autonomous attack rotorcraft. A joint development with [Archer Aviation](https://archer.com/), bringing the commercial VTOL sector's rapid innovation in electric propulsion and rotor design directly into defense.

[[Vertical Takekoff and Landing|VTOL]] with a hybrid-electric powertrain, reducing power demand and fuel burn in cruise and minimizing acoustic signature to enhance survivability during low-altitude ingress.

> For example, Thunder’s main payload module can be configured with ten air-to-ground missiles (e.g. [[AGM-114 Hellfire]], [[AGM-179 JAGM]], [[Barracuda|Barracuda-100]]), sixteen launched effects (e.g. [[Anduril Altius|Altius]]-600), or seventy-six 70mm rockets (e.g. [[Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System|APKWS]]), plus an additional twelve "counter-UAS effectors", in the nose payload module, providing a mix of effects to address a wide variety of threats during a single sortie.

> Shane Arnott, Anduril’s SVP of Maneuver Dominance, framed the collaboration as pulling commercial eVTOL performance and producibility into a defense airframe. Archer required a clean-sheet approach rather than a modification of an existing airframe, said Adam Goldstein, Archer’s founder and CEO, calling the result “the most sophisticated vertical lift aircraft ever made.”
