An [[Anduril]] family of autonomous, software-defined [[Cruise Missile]]s.


Designed for large-scale, low-cost mass manufacturing to provide an attritable and scalable precision strike capability for the US and allied forces.

Barracudas require significantly fewer tools ("10 or fewer tools") and parts to assemble compared to traditional missiles, allow manufacturing to be scaled rapidly.
