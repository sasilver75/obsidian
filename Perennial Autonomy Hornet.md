From [[Perennial Autonomy]]

Pneumatically launched AI-enabled mid-range strike drone. As of early-mid July 2026, they're turning the tide of the entire Ukraine war in Kiev's favor by absolutely fucking up Russian logistics networks.

Tested by the US [[United States Army|Army]] in March 2026.


As per Daily Mail video:
- Weighs ~30lbs, about ~9lbs taken up by warhead. Cruising speed 60-75mph, thought to cost ~$5000 for a single drone. The most important statistic is its range, which is allegedly up to 200 miles.
- Electronic motor attached to pusher propeller, much quieter than the two-stroke petrol engines typically used in these types of drones.
- This drone plugs an important gap in the Ukrainian army. Two that are already well-covered:
	- Close-range fight: Ukraine's most famous FPV drones do the dirty work here. Job is to hunt down Russian troops and tanks on the battlefield, using pinpoint accuracy to destroy as many of them as they can.
	- Long-range strike campaign: Fires drones over Russian army and into Russia itself, targeting oil refineries, weapons factories, and key logistics hubs. 
	- Medium-range: Things that were still on the battlefield, but not on the front line: supply convoys, fuel and ammo dumps, command posts, and so on. Things that were absolutely vital to the functioning of Russia's army, but beyond the range of Russia's FPVs. Ukraine didn't have a great answer for this, allegedly. ==This is where Hornet comes in.==

Note that a mount on top between the wings (not shown here) can be used to mount [[Starlink]] terminals.
- Main body and wings are apparently made of a type of ==styrofoam (**Expanded polypropylene (EPP)**),== which is light, cheap, and almost invisible to radar.
- In the nose of the drone is a camera, also a thin tube called a "pitot", which is a forward-facing sensor that measures total air pressure created by the aircraft's forward motion.
- The drone is mounted on a rail, which uses compressed air to catapult the drone into the air, where the motor takes over. It uses human operation or its AI to fly to the target.

In May 2026, they demonstrated their airborne launch system for its Hornet loitering munition, a platform supplied to Ukrainian forces, which deploys the drone from an aerostat (balloon).
- According to PA, the aerial launch enabled the drone to cover up to 42km while consuming only around 5% of its battery capacity. 
- "With a [[Starlink]] terminal, this means that strike range could jump from ~100-120km to 1.5-2x further."
- This enables a zero footprint launch.
