Naval Term, it seems.

I believe it means using ordnance from sea-based platforms (surface ships, submarines, carrier-based aircraft) to attacks targets on land.

While [[Naval Gunfire Support]] (NGFS) typically uses ship-based artillery for direct tactical support of forces at the coastline, strike warfare employs long-range missile and even aircraft for deep operational or strategic destruction far inland.
