
Mk 70 Payload Delivery System (PDS) uses combat-proven [[Mk 41 VLS]] to provide mid-range precision fires capabilities.
Enables rapid deployment of offensive capability to non-traditional platforms and locations.

> It's a containerization of VLS cells... so that you can put it on (say) a [[Saronic Marauder]] or a [[Saildrone Spectre]].
