Originally designed by Blue Force Technologies as an aggressor platform, initially ccalled "Grackle", then "REDmedium," then "Fury," to simulate high-end threats, such as the Chinese [[J-20 Weilong|J-20]].

Blue Force Technologies and the Fury were acquired by [[Anduril]] in 2023, and the Fury design was adapted as a submission for the [[United States Air Force|USAF]]'s [[Collaborative Combat Aircraft|CCA]] program, and eventually selected as one of the two winners of the CCA Increment I alongside the General Atomics [[FQ-42A Dark Merlin]] in June 2026.
- An estimated 150 FQ-44s and FQ-42s will be built by 2030

*It's a fighter-like [[Unmanned Combat Aerial Vehicle|UCAV]] that is approximately half the dimensions of the [[F-16 Fighting Falcon]].*
- Designed to fly at up to 50,000 feet and Mach 0.95, and cal pull a maximum of 9g while sustaining up to 4.5g at about 20,000 feet.
- The design is expected to provide the USAF with "affordable mass" to augment its crewed fighters in air-to-air missions. It's not "attritable," but it enables users and commanders to take greater risks and field greater numbers of them.
