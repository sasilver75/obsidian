The process of determining or estimating the geographic position of an object or person, yielding a set of geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) in a specific map datum/[[Coordinate Reference System]].

My also be expressed indirectly, as a distance in linear referencing or a bearing/range from a known landmark.
