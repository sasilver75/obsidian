An [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] program.

> MMAs will provide affordable, attritable, long-range combat power, and we'll field them at scale. With budget support, we'll field 100 MMA by 2029 at half the cost of a [[Collaborative Combat Aircraft Program|CCA]], and 1/10th the cost of a [[Fifth-Generation Fighter]]. So by 2032, 500 of these platforms will join our Air Force fleet.
