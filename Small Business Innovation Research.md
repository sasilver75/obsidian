Pron. "Sib-Urr", rhyming with "shiver"

They connect small, for-profit US businesses with military branches to develop dual-use technologies addressing critical national security needs.

STTRs are like SBIRs but require partnership with colleges, universities, or other federally-funded R&D centers (FFRDCs)
