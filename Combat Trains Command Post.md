A [[Battalion]] level forward logistics control node located close enough to support combat operations, but far enough back to avoid direct contact when possible.

Controls and coordinates the battalion's immediate sustainment needs.
- Ammunition, Fuel, Water resupply
- Maintenance and recovery coordination
- Casualty collection, medical evacuation coordination
- Coordination with battalion [[Tactical Operations Center|TOC]] and [[Field Trains Command Post]]s.

# Whats is a "Trains"
- Trains are the people, vehicles, equipment, and supplies that keep a combat unit operating. They're the units mobile sustainment package (fuel, food, water, etc.)
- Comes from the older idea of a wagon train, meaning the support vehicles and supplies following a fighting force. The logistics tail of a combat unit.

# Comparison (Closest-to-[[Forward Line of Own Troops|FLOT]], descending)
- [[Company Trains]]
	- Closest to [[Company]], main purpose is immediate company sustainment.
	- Usually the Company 1SG runs the trains day to day, company [[Executive Officer|XO]] might coordinate resupply, maintenance, [[Logistics Package|LOGPAC]]s. Coordinates with the [[Combat Trains Command Post|CTCP]], the Battalion [[S4 Officer|S-4]] officer, and the supporting [[Forward Support Company|FSC]].
- [[Combat Trains Command Post]] (CTCP)
	- Forward [[Battalion]] sustainment node near the fight.
	- Doctrinally led by the Battalion [[S4 Officer]].
	- Coordinates with the Battalion main [[Tactical Operations Center|TOC]], [[Company Trains]], the [[Field Trains Command Post]], and the [[Brigade Support Battalion]] (BSB)
- [[Field Trains Command Post]] (FTCP)
	- Runs the [[Battalion]]'s rear sustainment area and manages [[Field Trains Command Post|Field Trains]]. The logistics and administrative node kept further back than the CTCP, for less immediate but still essential support needs.
	- Often run by the FSC XO, Assistant S4, or a designated OIC/NCOIC.  
	- Coordinates heavily with the [[Brigade Support Battalion|BSB]] [[Commodity Manager]]s, the [[Brigade Support Area|BSA]], [[Support Operations Officer|SPO]] section, and the forward [[Combat Trains Command Post|CTCP]].
- [[Brigade Support Area]] (BSA)
	- [[Brigade]]-level rear area, larger sustainment hub.  The [[S4 Officer]] and [[Support Operations Officer|SPO]] help run operations, movement, defense, and support distribution.
