The total, cumulative area or volume that a sensing system (e.g. [[Satellite]]) can perceive by pointing, steering, or moving a sensor over time. 


# Comparison with [[Field of View]]
While the [[Field of View]] (FOV) is the specific angular cone or window that a sensor can capture at a single exact moment, it field of regard is much larger, because it includes everything that the sensor can see as it pans, tilts, rotates, or tracks. If a sensor is completely fixed and cannot move, its field of regard and viwe of view are identical.
