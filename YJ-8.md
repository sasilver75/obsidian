A [[People's Liberation Army]] air and surface-launched subsonic anti-ship [[Cruise Missile]], in service from teh early 1990s to present.

Operational range is only 26mi.

Variants:
- YJ-8: Basic version with fixed wings
- YJ-8A: Modified YJ-8 with folding wings
- YJ-81: Air-launched YJ-8 without the booster; often erroneously referred as YJ-8K
- YJ-82: Submarine-launched version
- C-801: Export version of the YJ-8 (surface-launched)
- C-801K: Export version of the YJ-81 (air-launched)
