The largest of the [[People's Liberation Army|PLA]]'s five services with 960,000 active duty personnel; approximately half of the PLA's total manpower of around 2 million personnel.

Organized into twelve group armies, sequentially numbered from the 71st Group Army to the 83rd Group Army, which are distributed to each of the PRC's five theater commands, receiving two to three group armies per command.

- Eastern Theater Command
	- 71st Group Army
	- 72nd Group Army
	- 73rd Group Army
- Southern Theater Command
	- 74th Group Army
	- 75th Group Army
- Western Theater Command
	- 76th Group Army
	- 77th Group Army
- Northern Theater Command
	- 78th Group Army
	- 79th Group Army
	- 80th Group Army
- Central Theater Command
	- 81st Group Army
	- 82nd Group Army
	- 83rd Group Army
