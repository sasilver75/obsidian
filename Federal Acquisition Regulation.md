A rigid, highly-regulated procurement tool used for traditional government buying. 

In contrast, an [[Other Transaction Authority|Other Transaction Agreement]] (OTA) is a flexible vehicle that lets agencies bypass standard federal rules to prototype cutting-edge technology with commercial or nontraditional partners.
- A FAR contract follows strict rulebooks, detailed clauses, formal bidding, and complex administrative processes,, often taking many months or years to award due to deep transparency, mandatory proposal windows, and formal protest rights. Best suited for traditional defense contractors, large-scale manufacturing, and long-term product sustainment.
- OTAs sit outside standard procurement laws, allowing customer terms, and ofer fast award cycles, direct communication between government and industry, and swift prototyping sessions. Tailored to attract small businesses, startups, and nontraditional defense contractors who normally avoid federal red tape.
