A [[Boeing]] aerial refueling drone that resulted from the Carrier-Based Aerial-Refueling System (CBARS) program.
- ==Interestingly==: Boeing's entry was not purpose-built for the refueling mission; it was designed to compete for a now-defunct Navy program called UCLASS (Unmanned Carrier-Launched Airborne Surveillance and Strike)... so... is it "really" a drone refueler? 😜 Or is it secretly a [[Unmanned Combat Aerial Vehicle|UCAV]]?
	- Some images have also shown [[AGM-158 JASSM|AGM-158C LRASM]] anti-ship missiles mounted on under-wing hardpoints, with an electro-optical sensor ball under the nose in front of the forward landing gear.
	- Note that it uses the same Rolls Royce engine as the very large [[RQ-4 Global Hawk]], which is known for its extreme endurance, so it's likely that the MQ-25 has a lot of range... for a refueler.
