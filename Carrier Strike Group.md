An operational [[United States Navy|Navy]] formation centered around an aircraft carrier, accompanied by [[Guided Missile Cruiser]]s, [[Guided Missile Destroyer]]s, a carrier air wing, [[Nuclear-powered Attack Submarine]]s, logistics vessels, and roughly 7,500 personnel.

These self-contained groups are able to project military power, provide national defense, and conduct sustained combat operations globally.
