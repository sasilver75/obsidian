A [[Shield AI]] product.

A [[Group 3 UAS|Group 3]] [[Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance|ISR]] [[Vertical Takekoff and Landing|VTOL]] drone for surveillance.

> "The most tactical, most logistically-simple UAS in the world. There's nothing else like it."

> "V-Bat delivers combat-proven, expeditionary, strategic and tactical-level ISR and targeting at a fraction of both the cost and logistical footprint of larger Group 4 and Group 5 drones with similar capabilities."

> "Designed, tested, and deployed for the [[Electronic Warfare]] battlefield, V-BAt has demonstrated real-world mission impact in [[Global Navigation Satellite System|GNSS]]-denied and comms-contested environments."

> "Ducted-fan technology enables industry-leading max takeoff weight-to-payload-ratio. Max payload capacity of 40 lbs, total maximum gross payoff weight of 165 lbs. No exposed rotor and fully unassisted launch and land allow for maximum operator safety and small expeditionary footprints."

> V-BAT's high-efficiency heavy-fuel engine maximizes fuel capacity, thrust, and economy, delivering endurance of 12+ hours for persistent ISR and targeting.

> Supports [[Beyond Visual Line of Sight|Beyond Line of Sight]] (BLOS) capability iwth V-BAT SATCOM, offering advanced SATCOM at a fraction of the traditional price.

> ==ViDAR==: Redefining ISR with the first passive, multi-domain AI-enabled optical sensor designed for use over sea and land. ViDAR provides unparalleled situational awareness with a 3,140 NM²/hr coverage rate—over 2.5 times that of the nearest competitor.



> Compact design ensures seamless assembly and mission readiness when it matters most. V-BAT is fully deployable by a 2-person team and packs up to fit in the back of a pickup truck or [[UH-60M Black Hawk]].


> V-BAT was competitively selected for the [[United States Coast Guard|Coast Guard]] Maritime Unmanned Aircraft System Services program, and is also deployed on nearly every class of [[United States Navy|Navy]] ship and with all seven [[Marine Expeditionary Unit|MEU]]s. Its unique design and controls enable takeoff and landing in high winds, on crowded flight decks, and aboard moving vessels with landing zones as small as 4.6 m x 4.6 m
