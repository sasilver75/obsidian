When the [[Predicted Impact Point]] (PIP) of a ballistic projectile is out of the expected area of coverage for a missile defense system (e.g. [[MIM-104 Patriot|Patriot]]), that is doing [[Point Defense]] or [[Area Defense]], it's called an "IPOUT," and the interception system will typically choose not to fire, conserving resources.

The missile is tracked and engageable, but on a non-threatening vector.
