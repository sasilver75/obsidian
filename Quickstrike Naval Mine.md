A family of naval mines ([[Quickstrike Naval Mine|Mk 62]], [[Quickstrike Naval Mine|Mk 63]], [[Quickstrike Naval Mine|Mk 65]]), which are converted general-purpose 500, 1,000, and 2,000-pound bombs respectively laid by aircraft in shallow water.

The [[Quickstrike Naval Mine|Mk 64 Mod 5]] is a "Quickstrike Extended Range (ER)" model.
