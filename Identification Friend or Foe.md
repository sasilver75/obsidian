A [[Combat Identification]] system designed for [[Command and Control]]; uses a transponder that listens for an *interrogation signal* and then sends a *response* that identifies a broadcaster.

Typically uses [[RADAR]] frequencies, but other EM frequencies (radio, infrared) can be used.

Enables military interrogation systems to identify aircraft, vehicles, or forces as friendly, as opposed to neutral or hostile.

Was first developed during WW2, with the arrival of radar, and several friendly fire incidents

Note that IFF can only *positively* identify friendly aircraft; if you don't receive a reply, the object cannot be positively identified as a foe.
