The [[Saronic Technologies|Saronic]] Cipher is a 60-foot, modular Autonomous Surface Vessel (ASV). Designed for the US military and allied forces, it features a range exceeding 3,000 nautical miles and can transport payloads of up to 10,000 pounds.
