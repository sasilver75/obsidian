#### Additional CCAs, not necessarily funded under the USAF CCA program

The [[XQ-58 Valkyrie]] has been flying since 2019, capable of being launched from a single point using [[Jet-Assisted Take-Off|JATO]], or launchable via a runway using a wheeled chassis that the drone leaves behind.

The Lockheed [[Vectis]] is a high-end skunkworks UCAV/CCA 

Shield AI's [[Shield AI X-Bat|X-Bat]] is much bigger than many CCA drones, and offers an interesting [[Vertical Takekoff and Landing|VTOL]] design.

There are also air-launched drones (that aren't competing for CCA funding, but fill similar roles):
- General Atomics [[X-68A LongShot]], a platform-agnostic air vehicle than can be launched from the F-15, B-2, etc.




General Atomics's :
- Gambit 1: The XQ-67, which was a prototype for the CCA program; The YFQ-42 was the successor based on the same platform.
- Gambit 2: The air-to-air focused Gambit 2 (YFQ-42)
- Gambit 3: A high-fidelity trainer aircraft
- Gambit 4: A stealthier flying-wing ISR drone for contested airspace
- Gambit 5: Meant for navy service aboard aircaft carriers
- Gambit 6: Emphasizes air-to-ground and [[Suppression of Enemy Air Defenses|SEAD]]; appears to be the next most-mature of the bunch, after Gambit 2. Might be competing for later increments in the CCA program. "This stealthy attack jet will be available for international procurement in 2027, with missionized versions."
