It seems that this is actually a specific *artillery rocket* from [[Lockheed Martin]] that's fired from [[M270 Multiple Launch Rocket System]] or [[M142 High Mobility Artillery Rocket System|M142 HIMARS]] launchers.

Standard variants exceed 52mi/84km in range, while newer Extended-Range (==ER==) GMLRS variants reach 93mi/150km.
