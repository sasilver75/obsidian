A functional component of [[US Strategic Command|STRATCOM]] that provides [[Unified Combatant Command|CCMD]]s, [[Joint Staff]], and other customers with effects-based, engineering, and scientific analysis and precision targeting options for selected networks and nodes to carry out the national security and military strategies of the of the United States during peace, crisis, and war.

JWAC provides innovating and accurate engineering and modeling-based targeting options with an understanding of risks and consequences, including [[Collateral Damage Estimation|CDE]]s.
