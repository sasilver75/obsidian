The assist in in the coordination and integration throughout the joint targeting process, the [[Joint Force Commander|JFC]] may approve the formation of a [[Joint Targeting Working Group|JTWG]], which supports the [[Joint Targeting Coordination Board]] by reviewing, as required, initial collection, consolidation, and prioritization on behalf of the JFC.

Chaired by the [[Joint Fires Element|JFE]] chief, [[J2 Officer|J-2]], or similar representative, and meets as required to discuss targeting integration and synchronization issues raised by the JFC, staff, planning teams, and the JFC's major subordinate commands.

Purpose:
1. Disseminate revised or new targeting guidance
2. Draft guidance for the JFC-proposed schemes to integrate fires and activities to create both lethal and nonlethal effects.
Responsibilities:
3. Review the JFC's broad targeting guidance and components proposed schemes of maneuver to verify compliance with the JFC's intent and guidance.
4. Disseminate the JFC's targeting guidance and priorities to component and JFC staff.
5. Review the [[Joint Target List|JTL]], [[No-Strike List|NSL]], [[Restricted Target List|RTL]], and other relevant lists.
