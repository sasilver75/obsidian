An advanced, multi-element antenna system designed to protect [[Global Positioning System|GPS]]/[[Global Navigation Satellite System|GNSS]] receivers from radio-frequency [[Jamming]] and [[Spoofing]] attacks. Unlike traditional omnidirectional antennas, a CRPA dynamically adjusts its reception pattern in real time to nullify malicious inference, while amplifying authentic satellite signals.

Maintains two components:
- An array of multiple antenna elements (e.g. 4 or 7)
- A specialized Antenna Electronics (AE) module

[[Null Steering]]: When a jammer or spoofer emits a strong signal, the antenna's processing algorithms calculate the signal's direction of arrival and creates deep "nulls" (blind spots) in that specific direction, effectively blocking the interference.

[[Beam Steering]]: At the same time as Null Steering, the system can dynamically focus or "steer" beams of maximum [[Gain]] towards genuine [[Global Navigation Satellite System|GNSS]] satellites to ensure continuous, uninterrupted [[Position, Navigation, and Timing]] data.
