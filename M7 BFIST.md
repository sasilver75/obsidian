BFIST = Bradley Fire Support Team

A specialized variant of the [[M2 Bradley]] used by the US to coordinate artillery, mortars, and close air support.

Instead of [[BGM-71 TOW|TOW]] anti-tank missiles, it mounts a Fire Support Sensor System (FSSS) that enables highly accurate "target on the move" designation under armor.
