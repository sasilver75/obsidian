A top-level [[Ontology]] adopted by other ontology Foundry efforts, such as the Industrial Ontologies Foundry. Over 700 projects use BFO; it is the only [[International Organization for Standardization|ISO]] standard for top-level ontology development.

We start here to build more specific content... livers, tanks, computers, etc.

![[Pasted image 20260701111323.png]]

Convictions:
- Realism: BFO is designed to represent the world, rather than simply concepts about the world.
	- There are some ontologies that model *conceptualism,* about how we *perceive* the world to be... but we reject that; we want to focus on trying to model the real world, rather than just the way we think the world is.
- Adequatism: Non-reductive, classes and relations motivated by research communities are not "paraphrased away", for example.
- Fallibilism: BFO is committed to tracking scientific research over time, which might change.
