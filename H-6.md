The ==H-6N== nuclear variant is a redesigned modernized strategic bomber carrying the [[JL-1]] air-launched ballistic missile; as of 2025, it's considered to be the only Chinese military aircraft assigned nuclear weapons. Capable of aerial refueling via the [[Y-20 Kunpeng]] variants. May also carry the [[CJ-10]]/KD-20 or [[KD-63]] land attack cruise missiles.

The ==H-6K== conventional variant can carry [[YJ-12]] supersonic cruise missiles and [[YJ-21]] hypersonic ballistic anti-ship missiles, as well as land-attack cruise missiles including [[CJ-10]] variants.

The H-6M mother ship variant has launched the [[WZ-8]] hypersonic unmanned surveillance and reconnaissance aerial vehicle.
