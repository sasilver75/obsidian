A US [[United States Department of Defense|DoD]] joint fires integration program focused on connecting sensors, command-and-control processes, targeting workflows, fires planning, shooters, and battle damage assessment into a faster machine-to-machine kill chain.

> "Began in 2019 as an interservice effort to manage the bewildering variety of potential targets and available weapons in a future US-China war. Tries to apply AI to the battle planning problem of 'who should shoot who?'"
