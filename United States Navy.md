The US Navy has seven active numbered fleets:
- 2nd Fleet: Western North Atlantic Ocean
- 3rd Fleet: Eastern Pacific Ocean
- 4th Fleet: Southern Atlantic Ocean
- 5th Fleet: Persian Gulf, Red Sea, and part of Indian Ocean
- 6th Fleet: Eastern North Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean
- 7th Fleet: Western Pacific Ocean and Indian Ocean
- 10th Fleet: Global



==US Navy Hull Classification Symbols==
Major Combatants
- CVN: [[Nuclear-powered Aircraft Carrier]]
	- A mobile airbase whose principal weapon is its embarked air wing, rather than the ship's own guns or missiles.
- CG: [[Guided Missile Cruiser]]
	- A large surface combatant traditionally capable of independent operations, fleet screening, command duties, and attacks against surface or land targets. Modern ones emphasize fleet air and missile defense, long range strike, and command facilities.
- DDG: [[Guided Missile Destroyer]]
	- A fast, general-purpose surface combatant that escorts larger ships while conducting air defense, anti-surface warfare, and land attack. Modern ones carry radar-directed surface-to-air missiles, cruise missiles, anti-ship weapons, torpedoes, guns, and helicopters.
- DDG(X): Next-generated Guided Missile Destroyer
- DDG 1000 ???
- FFG: [[Guided Missile Frigate]]
- LCS: [[Littoral Combat Ship]]
Submarines
- SSN: [[Nuclear-powered Attack Submarine]]
	- An attack submarine hunts enemy submarines and surface ships while also performing intelligence collection, surveillance, mine warfare, special-operations support, and land attack with cruise missiles.
- SSBN: [[Nuclear-powered Ballistic Missile Submarine]]
	- Carries submarine-launched ballistic missiles armed with nuclear warheads, forming the sea-based portion of the [[Nuclear Triad]]. Its central mission is strategic deterrence, hiding in a large patrol area so that adversaries cannot easily destroy US retaliatory capability.
- SSGN: [[Nuclear-powered Guided-Missile Submarine]]
	- A guided-missile submarine carrying large numbers of conventional [[Cruise Missile]]s, rather than strategic ballistic missiles. Can also carry special operations forces and deploy specialized underwater payloads.
- SSBN(X): Future Ballistic Missile Submarine, now Columbia Class
- SSN(X): Future Attack Submarine
Amphibious Warfare Ships
- LHA: [[Amphibious Assault Ship]], General-Purpose
	- An amphibious assault ship is a large aviation-capable vessel that transports Marines and supports their movement ashore by helicopter, tilt-rotor aircraft, landing craft, and amphibious vehicles.
- LHD: Amphibious Assault Ship, Multi-purpose/Dock
	- An amphibious assault ship is a large aviation-capable vessel that transports Marines and supports their movement ashore by helicopter, tilt-rotor aircraft, landing craft, and amphibious vehicles.
- LPD: [[Amphibious Transport Dock]]
	- Carries Marines, vehicles, vehicles, cargo, landing craft, amphibious vehicles, helicopters, and tilt-rotor aircraft. Its well deck allows landing craft to enter and leave through the stern, while its flight deck makes the LPD a secondary aviation platform.
- LSD: Dock Landing Ship
	- Build around a large floodable well deck used to transport, launch, recover, and support landing craft and amphibious vehicles. Also carries troops, vehicles, cargo, and a limited number of helicopters.
- LCU: Landing Craft Utility
	- Smaller vessels carried by or operating alongside amphibious ships to move personnel, vehicles, and cargo from ship to shore.
- LSM: Landing Ship Medium
- LCAC: Landing Craft Air Cushion

# Inventory

### Surface Ships and Submarines:
- [[Nimitz-Class Aircraft Carrier]]: Nuclear-powered aircraft carrier class that operates a carrier air wing for long-range strike, air defense, surveillance, and sea-control missions.
- [[Gerald R. Ford-Class Aircraft Carrier]]: Newest nuclear-powered aircraft carrier class, introducing electromagnetic aircraft launch, advanced arresting gear, improved power generation, and reduced crew requirements.
- [[Ticonderoga-Class Cruiser]]: Aegis guided-missile cruiser class used for carrier-strike-group air and missile defense, command-and-control, land attack, and anti-submarine warfare; now being retired.
- [[Arleigh Burke-Class Destroyer]]: Aegis guided-missile destroyer class used for air and missile defense, anti-submarine warfare, surface warfare, and Tomahawk land attack.
- [[Zumwalt-Class Destroyer]]: Low-observable guided-missile destroyer class built around an integrated electric-power system and being adapted for long-range conventional hypersonic strike.
- [[Freedom-Class Littoral Combat Ship]]: Monohull Littoral Combat Ship variant designed for fast near-shore surface warfare and modular missions; several early ships have been retired.
- [[Independence-Class Littoral Combat Ship]]: Aluminum trimaran Littoral Combat Ship variant designed for high speed, a large flight deck, surface warfare, and modular mine-countermeasures missions.
- [[Constellation-Class Frigate]]: Planned guided-missile frigate class intended to provide anti-submarine warfare, air defense, surface warfare, and escort capacity below the cost of a destroyer.
- [[Ohio-Class Ballistic Missile Submarine]]: Nuclear-powered strategic submarine class carrying Trident II ballistic missiles as the sea-based component of the US nuclear triad.
- [[Ohio-Class Guided Missile Submarine]]: Four converted Ohio-class submarines carrying large numbers of Tomahawk cruise missiles and supporting special-operations forces from a stealthy undersea platform.
- [[Columbia-Class Ballistic Missile Submarine]]: Next-generation nuclear-powered ballistic-missile submarine class being built to replace the Ohio class in the strategic-deterrence mission.
- [[Los Angeles-Class Attack Submarine]]: Nuclear-powered attack submarine class used to hunt ships and submarines, collect intelligence, and launch Tomahawk cruise missiles; progressively being replaced by the Virginia class.
- [[Seawolf-Class Attack Submarine]]: Small class of exceptionally fast, quiet, and heavily armed nuclear attack submarines optimized for demanding undersea warfare, including the specially modified USS _Jimmy Carter_.
- [[Virginia-Class Attack Submarine]]: Current-production nuclear attack submarine class designed for anti-submarine and anti-surface warfare, intelligence collection, special operations, and land attack in both deep and littoral waters.
- [[America-Class Amphibious Assault Ship]]: Large aviation-focused amphibious assault ship class that carries Marines, helicopters, MV-22 tiltrotors, and F-35B fighters, with later ships also incorporating a well deck.
- [[Wasp-Class Amphibious Assault Ship]]: Multipurpose amphibious assault ship class combining a large flight deck, Marine aviation facilities, troop spaces, and a well deck for landing craft and amphibious vehicles.
- [[San Antonio-Class Amphibious Transport Dock]]: Amphibious warship class that transports Marines, vehicles, cargo, landing craft, helicopters, and tiltrotor aircraft for expeditionary operations.
- [[Whidbey Island-Class Dock Landing Ship]]: Amphibious ship class built around a large well deck optimized to carry and launch Landing Craft Air Cushion vessels, Marines, vehicles, and equipment.
- [[Harpers Ferry-Class Dock Landing Ship]]: Cargo-enhanced dock landing ship class that trades some landing-craft capacity for additional vehicle and equipment stowage.
- [[Blue Ridge-Class Command Ship]]: Fleet flagship class equipped with extensive communications, planning, and command-and-control facilities for senior naval commanders and joint staffs.
- [[Avenger-Class Mine Countermeasures Ship]]: Low-magnetic mine-warfare ship class designed to detect, classify, sweep, and neutralize moored and bottom mines; being replaced by distributed airborne and unmanned systems.
- [[Spearhead-Class Expeditionary Fast Transport]]: High-speed shallow-draft catamaran used to move troops, vehicles, and cargo rapidly within a theater.
- [[Montford Point-Class Expeditionary Transfer Dock]]: Semi-submersible logistics platform that transfers vehicles and cargo from large sealift ships to landing craft when a conventional port is unavailable.
- [[Lewis B. Puller-Class Expeditionary Mobile Base]]: Large afloat staging base providing aviation, small-boat, command, maintenance, and accommodation facilities for special operations and other expeditionary missions.
- [[John Lewis-Class Fleet Oiler]]: New fleet-replenishment oiler class that transfers fuel and limited stores to Navy ships while underway and replaces the Henry J. Kaiser class.
- [[Henry J. Kaiser-Class Fleet Oiler]]: Fleet-replenishment oiler class used to deliver fuel and stores to warships at sea; progressively being replaced by the John Lewis class.
- [[Lewis and Clark-Class Dry Cargo and Ammunition Ship]]: Combat-logistics ship class that delivers ammunition, food, repair parts, and other dry or refrigerated stores to naval forces at sea.
- [[Supply-Class Fast Combat Support Ship]]: High-speed replenishment ship class that carries fuel, ammunition, and stores together to support carrier strike groups.
- [[Mercy-Class Hospital Ship]]: Converted supertanker hospital ship class providing large-scale surgical, intensive-care, diagnostic, and patient-treatment facilities for war or disaster response.
- [[Pathfinder-Class Oceanographic Survey Ship]]: Military Sealift Command survey ship class that collects hydrographic, acoustic, bathymetric, and environmental data supporting naval operations and charting.
- [[Victorious-Class Ocean Surveillance Ship]]: Small-waterplane-area twin-hull surveillance ship class that tows long passive acoustic arrays to collect undersea data for anti-submarine warfare.
- [[Navajo-Class Towing, Salvage, Rescue Ship]]: New Military Sealift Command class combining ocean towing, salvage, firefighting, diving support, and rescue functions in one vessel.

### Naval Aviation
- [[FA-18 Super Hornet|F/A-18 Super Hornet]]: Carrier-based multirole fighter family used for air defense, strike, close air support, reconnaissance, and aerial refueling.
- [[EA-18G Growler]]: Carrier-capable electronic-attack aircraft derived from the Super Hornet and used to detect, disrupt, deceive, and suppress enemy radar and communications systems.
- [[F-35 Lightning II|F-35C]] Lightning II: Carrier variant of the stealthy F-35 strike fighter, featuring larger wings and strengthened landing gear for catapult launches and arrested landings.
- [[E-2D Advanced Hawkeye]]: Carrier-based airborne early-warning and command-and-control aircraft that uses a powerful radar to detect threats and coordinate naval air operations.
- [[E-2C Hawkeye]]: Previous-generation carrier-based airborne early-warning and command-and-control aircraft being replaced by the E-2D.
- [[E-6B Mercury]]: Strategic communications and airborne command-post aircraft that connects national leaders with US nuclear forces and can direct nuclear operations if required.
- [[P-8A Poseidon]]: Land-based maritime patrol aircraft used for anti-submarine warfare, anti-surface warfare, intelligence collection, surveillance, and reconnaissance.
- [[MQ-4C Triton]]: High-altitude long-endurance uncrewed aircraft that provides persistent broad-area maritime surveillance and complements the P-8A.
- [[C-2A Greyhound]]: Legacy carrier-onboard-delivery aircraft used to carry personnel, mail, and high-priority cargo between shore bases and aircraft carriers.
- [[CV-22 Osprey|CMV-22B]] Osprey: Navy tiltrotor aircraft replacing the C-2A for carrier-onboard delivery, including transport of personnel, cargo, and F-35 engine modules.
- [[MH-60R Seahawk]]: Ship-based maritime helicopter used primarily for anti-submarine warfare, anti-surface warfare, surveillance, and targeting.
- [[MH-60S Seahawk]]: Multi-mission naval helicopter used for logistics, search and rescue, special-operations support, mine countermeasures, and armed surface warfare.
- [[MH-53E Sea Dragon]]: Heavy-lift helicopter used principally to tow airborne mine-countermeasures equipment and transport outsized cargo; approaching retirement.
- [[C-40A Clipper]]: Military Boeing 737 transport used to move Navy personnel and cargo between established airfields.
- [[C-37]]: Military Gulfstream executive-transport family used for rapid long-range movement of senior leaders and small staffs.
- [[C-130T Hercules]]: Navy tactical transport aircraft used for personnel, cargo, logistics, and fleet-support missions.
- [[C-130J Super Hercules]]: Modernized Hercules transport with improved engines, avionics, range, and payload performance for Navy logistics missions.
- [[C-26 Metroliner]]: Small twin-turboprop transport used for short-range passenger, cargo, training, and support missions.
- [[C-20 Gulfstream]]: Legacy military Gulfstream transport used for long-range movement of senior personnel and time-sensitive passengers or cargo.

Weapons/Munitions
- [[AIM-9 Sidewinder|AIM-9X Sidewinder]]: Short-range infrared-guided air-to-air missile used for highly maneuverable within-visual-range engagements and limited surface-attack missions.
- [[AIM-120 AMRAAM]]: Active radar-guided beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile used by fighters to engage aircraft without continuous terminal guidance from the launching aircraft.
- [[AGM-84 Harpoon]]: Sea-skimming anti-ship cruise missile launched from aircraft, ships, or submarines to attack surface vessels beyond the horizon.
- [[AGM-84E SLAM]]: Standoff Land Attack Missile derived from Harpoon and fitted with navigation and terminal guidance for precision attacks against land targets.
- [[AGM-84K SLAM-ER]]: Extended-range SLAM variant with improved range, survivability, terminal control, and precision against land and maritime targets.
- [[AGM-88E AARGM]]: Advanced Anti-Radiation Guided Missile used to detect and attack enemy air-defense radars, including radars that stop transmitting.
- [[AGM-88G AARGM-ER]]: Longer-range and more survivable AARGM variant designed to suppress or destroy advanced integrated air-defense systems.
- [[AGM-154 JSOW]]: Air-launched precision glide weapon that attacks defended land targets from standoff range without using an engine.
- [[AGM-158 JASSM|AGM-158C LRASM]]: Stealthy long-range anti-ship cruise missile with autonomous targeting capabilities for attacking defended surface combatants.
- [[BGM-109 Tomahawk Land Attack Missile]] (TLAM): Long-range subsonic cruise missile launched from surface ships or submarines for precision attacks against land targets.
- [[RIM-66C SM-2MR]]: Medium-range Standard Missile variant used by shipboard air-defense systems against aircraft and anti-ship missiles.
- [[RIM-116 Rolling Airframe Missile]] (RAM): Compact infrared and radio-frequency-guided missile used as a ship's short-range final defensive layer against missiles, aircraft, and small surface threats.
- [[RIM-156 SM-2ER]]: Extended-range Standard Missile variant providing longer-range fleet air defense than medium-range SM-2 versions.
- [[RIM-161 SM-3]]: Ship-launched exo-atmospheric interceptor designed to destroy ballistic-missile warheads during the midcourse portion of flight.
- [[RIM-162 ESSM]]: Evolved SeaSparrow Missile used for local ship and task-group defense against anti-ship missiles, aircraft, and some surface threats.
- [[SM-6]]: Long-range network-enabled Standard Missile used for air defense, terminal ballistic-missile defense, and anti-surface warfare.
- [[Naval Strike Missile]] (NSM): Low-observable sea-skimming anti-ship missile using imaging-infrared terminal guidance to strike surface vessels and selected land targets.
- [[UGM-133 Trident II]]: Submarine-launched ballistic missile carrying multiple nuclear warheads aboard Ohio- and future Columbia-class strategic submarines.
- [[RUM-139 VL-ASROC]]: Vertical-launch anti-submarine rocket that rapidly delivers a lightweight homing torpedo to a distant suspected submarine position.
- [[Mk 46 Torpedo]]: Legacy lightweight acoustic-homing torpedo used by ships and aircraft against submarines, now largely superseded by the Mk 54.
- [[Mk 48 ADCAP Torpedo]]: Heavyweight wire-guided and autonomous homing torpedo used by US submarines against surface ships and other submarines.
- [[Mk 54 Lightweight Torpedo]]: Current lightweight anti-submarine torpedo launched from aircraft and surface ships against submarines in deep or littoral water.
- [[Quickstrike Naval Mine]]: Family of air-delivered bottom influence mines based on general-purpose bomb bodies and triggered by the signatures of passing vessels.
- [[Hammerhead Mine]]: Developmental encapsulated anti-submarine mine designed to detect a hostile submarine and release a homing torpedo against it.
- [[Mk 82]]: 500-pound-class general-purpose bomb used unguided or as the warhead for precision-guidance kits such as JDAM and Paveway.
- [[Mk 83]]: 1,000-pound-class general-purpose bomb used unguided or fitted with precision-guidance kits for medium-yield strikes.
- [[Mk 84]]: 2,000-pound-class general-purpose bomb used against large or hardened targets and as the warhead for several guided-bomb variants.
- [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|JDAM]]: GPS and inertial guidance kit family that converts compatible unguided bombs into all-weather precision-guided weapons.
- [[GBU-54 Laser Joint Direct Attack Munition]]: JDAM variant adding laser guidance so the weapon can engage moving or relocatable targets while retaining GPS and inertial guidance.
- [[GBU-10 Paveway II]]: 2,000-pound-class laser-guided bomb used for precision attacks against large point targets.
- [[GBU-12 Paveway II]]: 500-pound-class laser-guided bomb used for lower-yield precision attacks against point targets.
- [[GBU-16 Paveway II]]: 1,000-pound-class laser-guided bomb providing an intermediate warhead size for precision attack.
- [[GBU-24 Paveway III]]: 2,000-pound-class laser-guided bomb with improved guidance, range, and flight-path control compared with Paveway II weapons.
- [[Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System]] (APKWS): Laser-guidance kit that converts a Hydra 70 rocket into a relatively low-cost precision weapon for lightly protected targets.
- [[Hydra 70]]: Family of unguided 70mm air-launched rockets carrying different warheads and serving as the rocket basis for APKWS.
- [[AGM-114 Hellfire]]: Precision air-to-surface missile used against vehicles, small boats, personnel, and other point targets.
- [[Mk 45]] 5-inch gun: Automated 5-inch naval gun system used for surface warfare, air defense, and naval gunfire support of forces ashore.
- [[Mk 110]] 57 mm gun: Rapid-fire medium-caliber naval gun used against small boats, aircraft, missiles, and surface targets, principally aboard Littoral Combat Ships.
- [[Mk 38]] 25mm gun: Shipboard stabilized or manually operated 25mm cannon system used for close-range defense against small craft and other surface threats.
- [[Mk 46 Gun Weapon System]] 30 mm gun weapon system: Remotely operated stabilized 30mm cannon used mainly by amphibious ships for close-range defense against small, fast surface craft.
- [[Phalanx CIWS]]: Radar-controlled rapid-fire 20mm Close-In Weapon System providing a final defensive layer against incoming missiles, aircraft, and surface threats.
- [[Mk 70 Mod 1]]: Containerized launcher derived from the Mk 41 Vertical Launch System that enables deployment of missiles such as SM-6 and Tomahawk from suitable platforms ashore or afloat.

Sensors/Combat Systems
- [[Aegis Combat System]]: Integrated ship combat system that combines radar, command-and-decision software, fire control, and missiles for air, ballistic-missile, surface, and strike warfare.
- [[Ship Self-Defense System]] (SSDS): Combat-direction system that integrates sensors and defensive weapons aboard aircraft carriers and amphibious ships for rapid defense against close-in threats.
- [[Mk 41 VLS]]: Modular below-deck Vertical Launching System that stores and launches multiple missile families from cruisers, destroyers, and future frigates.
- [[Mk 57 VLS]]: Peripheral Vertical Launching System used by Zumwalt-class destroyers, with missile cells distributed along the hull edges for payload flexibility and survivability.
- [[Cooperative Engagement Capability]] (CEC): Sensor-networking system that combines high-quality measurements from multiple platforms into shared fire-control-quality tracks.
- [[Naval Integrated Fire Control-Counter Air]] (NIFC-CA): Networked operational capability that links sensors and weapons across ships and aircraft so one platform can engage a target detected by another.
- [[Link 16]]: Secure, jam-resistant tactical data link used to exchange tracks, commands, identification, and situational-awareness information among US and allied forces.
- [[ANSPY-1|AN/SPY-1]]: Fixed-array multifunction radar central to earlier Aegis cruisers and destroyers for wide-area search, tracking, and missile guidance support.
- [[ANSPY-3|AN/SPY-3]]: Active electronically scanned array X-band multifunction radar used on Zumwalt-class destroyers for horizon search, precision tracking, and fire control.
- [[ANSPY-6|AN/SPY-6]] (EASR): Scalable active electronically scanned array radar family providing improved sensitivity for air and missile defense; its rotating variants are called Enterprise Air Surveillance Radar.
- [[ANSPQ-9B|AN/SPQ-9B]]: Rotating X-band radar optimized to detect and track low-flying anti-ship missiles, aircraft, and surface contacts near the horizon.
- [[ANSPS-48|AN/SPS-48]]: Long-range three-dimensional air-search radar used to detect aircraft and provide bearing, range, and altitude information.
- [[ANSPS-49|AN/SPS-49]]: Long-range two-dimensional air-search radar used for wide-area detection and tracking of airborne targets.
- [[ANSPS-73|AN/SPS-73]]: Surface-search and navigation radar used to detect ships, small craft, and nearby navigational hazards.
- [[ANSLQ-32 SEWIP|AN/SLQ-32]]: Shipboard electronic-warfare family that detects, identifies, and counters hostile radar emissions, with SEWIP upgrades adding improved sensing and electronic attack.
- [[Nulka Active Missile Decoy]]: Ship-launched hovering decoy that transmits a false radar target to seduce incoming anti-ship missiles away from the vessel.
- [[Mk 36 SRBOC]]: Shipboard launcher system that deploys chaff and infrared decoys to confuse radar-guided and heat-seeking missiles.
- [[ANSLQ-25 Nixie|AN/SLQ-25 Nixie]]: Towed acoustic decoy system designed to lure incoming homing torpedoes away from a surface ship.
- [[ANSQQ-89|AN/SQQ-89]] Undersea Combat System: Integrated surface-ship anti-submarine warfare suite that combines hull sonar, towed arrays, acoustic processing, fire control, and data from offboard sensors.
- [[ANSQS-53|AN/SQS-53]] Hull-Mounted Sonar: Active and passive bow-mounted sonar used by cruisers and destroyers to detect, classify, and track submarines and underwater objects.
- [[TB-37]] Multifunction Towed Array: Long passive sonar array streamed behind a surface ship to detect and localize quiet submarines while reducing interference from the towing ship.
- [[ANAWS-22|AN/AWS-22]] Airborne Low Frequency Sonar: Dipping-sonar system carried by the MH-60R helicopter to actively search for and track submerged submarines.
- [[SSQ-53]]: Passive directional sonobuoy that listens for underwater acoustic signals and reports bearing information to an anti-submarine warfare aircraft.
- [[SSQ-62]]: Active sonobuoy that emits acoustic pulses and detects echoes from submerged targets for localization and tracking.
- [[SSQ-101]]: Air-deployable active receiver sonobuoy used in multistatic fields to detect echoes from acoustic sources transmitted elsewhere.
- [[SSQ-125]]: Multistatic active coherent source sonobuoy that transmits controlled low-frequency signals for reception by distributed sonobuoys.
- [[ANAPY-9|AN/APY-9]] on the E-2D: Ultra High Frequency active electronically scanned array radar providing the E-2D with long-range detection and tracking of aircraft, missiles, and difficult low-observable targets.
- [[ANAPY-10|AN/APY-10]] on P-8A: Multimode maritime radar used for surface search, imaging, navigation, periscope detection, and overland surveillance from the P-8A.
- [[ANAPG-79|AN/APG-79]] on F/A-18E/F and EA-18G: X-band active electronically scanned array fire-control radar used for air-to-air search, targeting, mapping, and maritime strike.
- [[ANAPG-81|AN/APG-81]] on F-35C: F-35 active electronically scanned array radar providing air-to-air, air-to-surface, electronic-warfare, and high-resolution mapping functions.
- [[ANALQ-99|AN/ALQ-99]] Tactical Jamming System (Legacy): Externally carried electronic-attack system used by the EA-18G to disrupt hostile radars and communications, now being replaced by Next Generation Jammer pods.
- [[ANALQ-249|AN/ALQ-249]] Next Generation Jammer: Modular EA-18G electronic-attack pod family designed to detect and jam modern radar and communications threats across multiple frequency bands.
- [[ANAAQ-24 LAIRCM|AN/AAQ-24 LAIRCM]] for Aircraft Self-Projection: Large Aircraft Infrared Countermeasures system that detects missile launches and uses directed infrared energy to disrupt heat-seeking missile guidance.

Unmanned/Future/Programmatic
- [[MQ-25 Stingray]]: Carrier-based uncrewed tanker aircraft in development to extend the combat radius of carrier aircraft while also providing intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance support.
- [[Orca XLUUV]]: Extra-large uncrewed undersea vehicle designed for long-endurance autonomous missions and carriage of modular undersea payloads.
- [[Knifefish]]: Uncrewed underwater vehicle system designed to detect, classify, and identify buried, bottom, and volume naval mines while keeping sailors outside the minefield.
- [[Razorback]]: Submarine-launched and recovered medium uncrewed undersea vehicle program intended to provide covert sensing and payload delivery from attack submarines.
- [[Lionfish]]: Small, portable uncrewed underwater vehicle family acquired for tasks such as intelligence collection, reconnaissance, mine countermeasures, and underwater inspection.
- [[Large Unmanned Surface Vessel]] (LUSVs): Planned large optionally crewed surface vessels intended to carry substantial missile or other modular payloads as distributed adjuncts to crewed warships.
- [[Medium Unmanned Surface Vessel]] (MUSVs): Planned medium-sized uncrewed surface vessels focused primarily on distributed sensing, surveillance, communications relay, and electronic-warfare payloads.
- DDG(X): Next-generation guided-missile destroyer program intended to succeed current destroyers with greater electrical power, cooling, payload capacity, range, and growth margin.
- SSN(X): Next-generation nuclear attack submarine program intended to follow the Virginia class with improved stealth, speed, sensors, survivability, and undersea combat capability.
- F/A-XX: Next-generation carrier-based combat-aircraft program intended to provide future air dominance, strike, sensing, and crewed-uncrewed teaming beyond the Super Hornet era.
- Conventional Prompt Strike (CPS): Non-nuclear hypersonic weapon program intended to strike high-value targets at very long range with short flight times from naval platforms.
- Next-Generation Jammer: Family of electronically scanned EA-18G jamming pods replacing the AN/ALQ-99 across mid-, low-, and high-frequency threat bands.
- Virginia Payload Module (VPM): Additional hull section containing four large payload tubes that substantially increases the missile and future-payload capacity of later Virginia-class submarines.
- Integrated Combat System / Aegis modernization: Navy effort to converge combat-system software, sensors, weapons, and rapid upgrade pathways across multiple surface-ship classes.
- [[Low-Cost Uncrewed Combat Attack System|LUCAS]]: Low-cost autonomous combat-aircraft initiative intended to field attritable uncrewed aircraft that can complement crewed fighters in sensing and attack missions.
- [[Seahawk]]: Autonomous surface-vessel demonstrator used to develop long-duration uncrewed navigation, sensing, command-and-control, and fleet-integration concepts.
