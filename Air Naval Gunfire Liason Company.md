A US [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] [[Company]]-sized unit that provides liaison teams and fire-support specialists to help another force request, coordinate, and control air, naval surface, artillery, and other supporting fires. ~150-200 service members.

> ==ANGLICOs are not designed to support U.S. Marine Corps maneuver elements.== Instead, the doctrinal purpose of ANGLICO is to provide fire support and coordination in support of units adjacent or attached to the [[Marine Air-Ground Task Force|MAGTF]]

The important part is liaison: ANGLICO is not a gun battery or aircraft unit, it's a unit that helps other forces access and synchronize fires from many sources:
- [[Close Air Support]] from aircraft
- Naval surface fire support from ships
- Artillery or rockets from another unit
- Mortars
- [[Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance|ISR]] assets
- Multinational or joint fires

ANGLICO helps bridge those seams between services, nations, command chains, and communication networks.

Typical Functions:
1. Liaison: ANGLICO communicates between the supported force and fire providers
2. Fire-support Planning: ANGLICO helps plan how fires supports the maneuver plan.
3. Targeting Support: ANGLICO helps identify, nominate, prioritize, and coordinate targets.
4. Close Air Support Coordination: ANGLICO may include personnel who can coordinate or control aircraft delivering fires near friendly forces.
5. Naval Surface Fire Support Coordination: ANGLICO can help requests and adjust fires from naval guns.
6. Deconfliction: ANGLICO helps prevent aircraft, artillery, naval fires, and friendly maneuver from interfering with or endangering one another.
7. Communications: ANGLICO often provides radio and data connectivity between units that can't talk efficiently.

>  -  A foreign partner battalion is operating near the coast.
  - The battalion needs naval gunfire from a U.S. destroyer and close air support from U.S. aircraft.
>  - The battalion does not have the right radios, procedures, terminal attack controllers, or fire-support coordination expertise.
  - An ANGLICO team embeds with the battalion and coordinates the fires.
