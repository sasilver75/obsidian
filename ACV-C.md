ACV-C: "Amphibious Combat Vehicle - Command and Control"

Part of a larger ACV family ([[ACV-R]], [[ACV-C]], [[ACV-P]], [[ACV-30]])
Specialized eight-wheeled armored vehicle used to provide mobile [[Command and Control]] in the context of open-ocean ship-to-shore mission sets. Serves as a secure, on-the-move command center.
