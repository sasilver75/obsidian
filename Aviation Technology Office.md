Previously known as Flight Concepts Division (or as E Squadron in [[1st Special Forces Operational Detachment-Delta|Delta Force]]), ATO is an [[United States Army|Army]] special operations aviation organization associated with highly-specialized aviation support, aircraft modification, and sensitive missions.

Unlike [[160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment|SOAR]], ATO flies modified helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft, low-visibility or non-standard aircraft, experimental aviation capabilities, etc. Often flies civilian-looking aircraft, or even enemy-looking aircraft.
