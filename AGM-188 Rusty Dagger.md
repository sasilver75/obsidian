A low-cost, mass-producible, air-launched [[Cruise Missile]] from [[Zone 5]] (now owned by [[Kongsberg]]).

A mini cruise missile designed for large-scale usage. 
- Things like the [[BGM-109 Tomahawk Land Attack Missile|Tomahawk]], [[AGM-158 JASSM]], [[AGM-158 JASSM|AGM-158C LRASM]], and ... are expensive/exquisite "use when it really matters" weapons, the Rusty Dagger is meant to fill the layer below them; good-enough range, enough warhead, enough precision, and cheap enough to buy by the thousand.


Range ~250 miles
~500lb munition
[[GPS INS]] navigation, with emphasis on GPS-degraded resilience
