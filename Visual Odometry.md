The process of estimating a system's position and orientation by analyzing sequential camera images.

By detecting visual features (e.g. corners) and tracking them across consecutive frames, VO calculates how the camera moved, providing real-time trajectory without relying on [[Global Navigation Satellite System|GNSS]] like [[Global Positioning System|GPS]].

See also [[Visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping]] (vSLAM)
