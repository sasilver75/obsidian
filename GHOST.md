A low-cost, [[3D Printing|3D Printed]] mid-range [[Cruise Missile]] from [[CoAspire]]


July 24, 2026: [U.S. Navy Orders Low-cost Missiles to Expand Fleet, Allied Long-Range Maritime Strike Capabilities](https://news.usni.org/2026/07/24/u-s-navy-orders-low-cost-missiles-to-expand-fleet-allied-long-range-maritime-strike-capabilities)
- Selected for the [[United States Navy|Navy]]'s [[Coalition Heterogenous Affordable Offensive Strike Program|CHAOS Program]] ... awarded $70M through the [[Other Transaction Authority]] for the development of its RAACM-ER missile, which can be launched from land, air, and sea platforms against maritime targets at ranges up to 1,000 nautical miles.
- This cruise missile has already been tapped by the [[United States Army|Army]] and [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] in recent months.
	- Adopted similarly to the RAACM-ER "GHOST" variant for the US Army's [[Low-Cost Containerized Missiles Program]] and the Air Force's [[Family of Affordable Mass Munitions Program]].
- [[Portfolio Acquisition Executive]] Munitions will also award another defense firm for the CHAOS program.
