A term heavily utilized by the [[United States Department of Defense|DoD]] to keep major acquisitions competitive, affordable, and rapidly deployable... using a strategy mandating that complex systems use severable, "plug-and-play" modules, relying on consensus-based open standards rather than proprietary, closed-vendor ecosystems.

This enables hardware and software to be easily upgraded, replaced, and shared across platforms.

See the lower-level [[Weapon Open System Architecture]]
