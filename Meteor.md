A European active radar guided [[Beyond Visual Range]] air-to-air missile (BVRAAM).
Offers multi-shot capability, and has the ability to engage highly maneuverable targets, small targets, and cruise missiles.

Has a solid-fueled ramjet motor that lets it cruise at aspeed of over Mach 4 and provides the missile with thrust and mid-course acceleration.

Two-way data link.

Has 3-6x the [[No-Escape Zone]] of current (2018) air-to-air missiles of its class.
