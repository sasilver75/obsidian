A major command is a significant [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] organization subordinate to Headquarters, US Air Force. Major commands have a headquarters staff and subordinate organizations, typically formed in numbered air forces, centers, wings, and groups.

A major command (MAJCOM) represents a major Air Force subdivision having a specific portion of the Air Force mission.

It sits directly below the top national headquarters of a service branch and oversees broad missions, multiple bases, and subordinate units.
