A massive, very long range air-to-air missile from [[Raytheon]] used by the [[United States Navy|Navy]].

A derivative of the RIM-147B Standard Extended Range Active Missile (ERAM) surface-to-air missile.

Has been confirmed to be capable of being carried and launched ==only== by the [[FA-18 Super Hornet]].

Range: 150–250 mi
