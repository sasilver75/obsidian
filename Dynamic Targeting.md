Used when a target appears, moves, changes status, or becomes relevant during execution. The target may not have been known, may not have been selected earlier, or may require faster action than the deliberate cycle allows.
- Compare with [[Deliberate Targeting]], where the force comfortably has enough time to analyze/nominate/validate/prioritize/assign capabilities against targets before execution.

> A mobile missile launcher appears briefly after firing, then begins moving toward cover. The force must determine whether the object is actually the launcher, whether engagement is authorized, what capability can act in time, and whether friendly forces or protected objects are nearby, and whether airspace or fires coordination allows action.

Often taught using [[Find, Fix, Track, Target, Engage, Assess|F2T2EA]] (Find, Fix, Track, Target, Engage, Assess). The common simplification is "sensor finds target, shooter attacks target."
