A piece of specialized [[Weaponeering]] software that supports the targeting of [[Hard and Deeply Buried Target]] (HDBTs) and [[Weapons of Mass Destruction|WMD]]s.

Can characterize buildings, bunkers, tunnels, and open-air targets. 
Can predict weapons effects from conventional weapons, and calculate the number of weapons and associated aimpoints that achieve a commander's objective.
