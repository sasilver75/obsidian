A heavy-duty, 10x10 tactical truck family manufactured by [[Oshkosh Defense]] for the [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]], designed to replace the older LVS. It is the Marine Corps' primary heavy-hauler, built to transport bulk cargo, heavy equipment, and shipping containers across severe terrain.
