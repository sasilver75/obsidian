Issued directly alongside an [[Air Tasking Order]] to define, restrict, and map out the physical geometry of the sky (corridors, no-fly zones, orbits) so that friendly forces are deconflicted and do not collide or fire on eachother.

Where friendly aircraft should and should not go.

Implements the [[Airspace Control Plan]] (ACP), activating specific [[Airspace Coordinating Measure]]s (ACMs) and procedural rules.
