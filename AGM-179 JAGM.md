JAGM = "Joint Air-to-Ground Missile"

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AGM-179_JAGM

Reached [[Initial Operating Capability]] in 2022 with the Marine Corps.

==A program to *develop* an air-to-surface missile to replace the current air-launched [[AGM-65 Maverick]] and [[AGM-114 Hellfire]] missiles.

The [[United States Army|Army]], [[United States Navy|Navy]], [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]] collectively plans to buy tens of thousands of JAGMs.
