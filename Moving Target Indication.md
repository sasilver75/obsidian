A mode of operation of a [[Radar]] to discriminate a target against the [[Clutter Zone]].

It encapsulates a variety of techniques used for finding moving objects (e.g. an aircraft) and filter out unmoving ones, like hills or trees.
- It contrasts with *[[Stationary Target Indication]] (STI),* which uses details of the signal to directly determine the mechanical properties of the reflecting objects, thereby finding targets whether moving or not.

Modern radars generally perform a variety of MTI techniques as part of a wider suite of signal processing being carried out by digital signal processors. MTI may be specialized in terms of the type of clutter and environment:
- Airborne MTI (AMTI) ((Especially relevant for use with [[Drone]]s, for instance; I've seen this also used with [[Synthetic Aperture Radar|SAR]]))
- Ground MTI (GMTI)

Or may be combined mode: Stationary and Moving Target Indication (SMTI)
