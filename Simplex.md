Communication in which information flows *only in one direction*.
- Broadcast radio
- TV broadcast
- One-way sensor feed

Mental model:
> A loudspeaker. The speaker can send sound to listeners, but listeners can't send anything back through the same system.

Mechanically, a simplex link typically has a fixed [[Transmitter]] and a fixed [[Receiver]]. The receiver can detect/decode/record the signal, but it can't transmit back over that same communication path.
