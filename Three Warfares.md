三种战法
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_warfares

> "Three warfares has been labelled a tool for distraction by Western analysts, with the aim of distracting from the "much larger organizational and operational infrastructure that exists under the CCP".

An official strategy of the [[People's Liberation Army|PLA]] employing:
1. ==Media or public opinion warfare==
	- "One tactic may be "to open for selective study the parts of the PLA that help deliver the message that the GPD and Propaganda Department want delivered to foreign audiences while concealing other areas of PLA activity."
	- "Aimed at influencing domestic and international public opinion to build support for China's military actions and dissuade an adversary from pursuing actions contrary to China's interests."
2. ==Psychological warfare==
	- Should "sap the enemy's morale, disintegrate their will to fight, ignite the anti-war sentiment among citizens at home, heighten international and domestic conflict, weaken and sway the will to fight among its high level decision makers, and in turn lessen their superiority in military strength."
	- "To seek to undermine an enemy's ability to conduct combat operations through operations aimed at deterring, shocking, and demoralizing enemy military personnel and supporting civilian population."
3. ==Legal warfare== ("lawfare").
	- For the PLA, legal warfare in the modern international context is a way to "expose the enemy," "publicize one's own humanitarianism... win over the universal sympathy and support from the international community, to compel the opponent to bog down in isolation and passivity."
	- "Uses international and domestic law to claim the legal high ground or assert Chinese interests. It can be used to thwart an opponent's operational freedom and shape the operational space. It is also used to build international support and manage possible political repercussions of China's military."

Believed to be inspired by Sun Tuz's *The Art of War*, particularly the notion of winning without fighting.
