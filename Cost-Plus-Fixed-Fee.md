A contract/payment model where a buyer reimburses a contractor for all allowable, actual expenses incurred during performance, plus an additional pre-negotiated, fixed profit fee that never changes regardless of final project costs. This structure shifts financial risk to the buyer.

> "[[Cost-Plus Pricing|Cost-Plus Contract]]" is a broad category of contracts that reimburse expenses, while cost-plus-fixed-fee is just one specific type within that family.
