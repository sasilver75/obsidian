The military process of accurately characterizing detected objects in the battlespace as:
- Friendly
- Enemy
- Neutral
- Unknown

Main types of CID techniques:
- ==Cooperative CID==: Systems like [[Identification Friend or Foe]] (IFF) where active electronic transponders or data links exchange challenge-and-response signals.
- ==Non-Cooperative CID==: Methods that don't need a reply from the target, such as a high-resolution radar, thermal signatures, or visual and optical recognition. 
- ==Physical Markers==: Special tools like [[Combat Identification Panel]]s (CIPs) are placed on ground vehicles to show a distinct thermal signature through night-vision optics.
