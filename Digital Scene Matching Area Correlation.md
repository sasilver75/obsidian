https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TERCOM

> DSMAC was a terminal navigation system which could guide missiles in real time by using camera inputs to determine location. DSMAC was used in Tomahawk Block II onward, and proved itself successfully during the first Gulf War. 

> Due to its ability to visually identify targets instead of simply attacking estimated coordinates, its accuracy exceeded GPS guided weapons during the first Gulf War.

> DSMAC systems are often combined with TERCOM as a terminal guidance system, allowing point attack with conventional warheads.
