In a [[Command and Control]] (C2) context, a ==track== refers to a localized, real-time representation of an object (such as an airship, ship, vehicle, or threat) being monitored.

*Tracking* is the process that allows a commander to identify, follow, and analyze movement so that informed tactical decisions can be made.


A [[Target-Quality Track]] is one that has enough fidelity ([[Track Quality]]) that it can be targeted. While "Quality" is a range running from 1-15 or similar, anything above 8 is pretty good. Higher fidelity TQTs increase the likelihood of hit, while lower fidelity increases uncertanity around the target.
