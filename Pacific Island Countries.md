Consists of 14 independent nations in the Pacific Ocean, divided into three ethnogeographic regions:
- Micronesia
	- Kiribati
	- Marshall Islands
	- Micronesia (FSM)
	- Nauru
	- Palau
- Melanesia
	- Fiji
	- Papua New Guinea
	- Solomon Islands
	- Vanatu
- Polynesia
	- Cook Islands
	- Niue
	- Samoa
	- Tonga
	- Tuvalu
