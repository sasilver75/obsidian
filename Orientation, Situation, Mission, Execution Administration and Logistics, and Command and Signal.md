The 5-paragraph format typically used to structure operational field orders (e.g. [[Operational Order|OPORD]]s)

> Even though the acronym is OSMEAC, six paragraphs, it's still a 5 Paragraph Order. Like the Hobbit to the Lord of the Rings. If you had to pick 5, it would be SMEAC.



Breakdown:
- ==Orientation==: Gives the team a physical layout of the area using a map or terrain model, noting current location, direction, and surrounding landmarks.
- ==Situation==: Details enemy forces and friendly forces.
- ==Mission==: States clear "who, what, when, where, and why" of the objective
- ==Execution==: Explains the commander's plan, specific tasks for team members, and step-by-step instructions.
- ==Administration and Logistics==: Covers supplies like food, ammunition, and medical evacuation plans (beans, bullets, band-aids)
- ==Command and Signal==: Identifies the chain of command, radio frequencies, call signs, and passwords.
