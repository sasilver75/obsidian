From [[General Atomics]]

Communicates with other system that can detect threats, communicates it to its munitions, and fires them. The existing inventory of AIM-120s thusly have their ranges extended. Longshot is basically an air-breathing jet-booster for its AMRAAM payload.  It's basically an air-launched [[Cruise Missile]] that launches its own missiles. Intended to carry a pair of large air-to-air missiles like the [[AIM-120 AMRAAM]]. The LongShot itself is carried underwing on things like the [[F-15E Strike Eagle|F-15EX Eagle II]], or deployed from bays of bombers like the [[B-52H Stratofortress]] or [[B-21 Raider]]. You could deploy it from the [[F-35 Lightning II]]'s external weapons stations, but that would compromise stealth in a way that makes it likely not make sense. Once out of fuel or after expending munitions, the LongShot would simply crash like other cruise-based missile systems like the [[ADM-160 MALD]], so the platform is meant to be significantly cheaper; it also tells us something about the systems that are meant to be carried on-board. LongShots can extend weapon range of things like the [[AIM-120 AMRAAM]] to be similar to the upcoming [[AIM-260 JATM]]... or even carry the AIM-260 itself, when it's arrived. A palletized deployment like [[Dragon Cart|Rapid Dragon]] could allow a cargo aircraft to deploy many of these at once, flooding enemy airspace with threats too potent to be ignored, depleting interceptors, munitions, manpower, etc.

First flight is slated to happen sometime in 2026.

> "An air-launched unmanned air vehicle with the ability to employ multiple air-to-air weapons, with the goal of extending engagement ranges, increase missile effectiveness, and reduce the risk to manned aircraft."
