MQ-9 is the overarching military designation for the unmanned, multi-role drone, while MQ-9A serves as the specific, official manufacturer variant designation built by [[General Atomics]].

The Reaper has a 950 horsepower turboprop engine compared to the [[MQ-1B Predator]]'s 115 horsepower engine. This lets the Reaper carry 15 times more ordnance payload and cruise at about three times the speed of the MQ-1.

The average unit cost of an MQ-9 is estimated at $34M in 2024 dollars.

The MQ-9 has been further developed into the MQ-9B which (based on mission and payload) are referred to by General Atomics as SkyGuardian or SeaGuardian.
