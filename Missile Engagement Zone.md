Pron. "Mezz"

A specific type of [[Weapon Engagement Zone|WEZ]] where that responsibility belongs strictly to [[Surface-to-Air Missile]]s.

Can sometimes be further broken down by altitude and system capability into zones like:
- HIMEZ (High-Altitude MEZ)
- LOMEZ (Low-Altitude MEZ)
- SHORADEZ (Short-Range Air Defense Engagement Zone)
