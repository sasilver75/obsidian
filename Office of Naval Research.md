Serves as the primary science and technology (S&T) and a research-funding organizations for their respective military branches.
Heavily funds external basic research at universities and private companies.

For in-house research, ONR oversees and manages the [[Naval Research Laboratory]] (NRL), which acts as the Navy's corporate research and is quite similar to the [[Air Force Research Lab]].
