The AN/SLQ-32 (often nicknamed "Slick 32") is the U.S. Navy's primary shipboard electronic warfare (EW) system. The Surface Electronic Warfare Improvement Program (SEWIP) is an ongoing, multi-block upgrade designed to modernize this system's antennas, receivers, and software to detect and jam advanced anti-ship missiles.
