A 60-lb precision-guided bomb developed by [[Dynetics]]


Became operational in 2017; in 2018 [[US Special Operations Command|SOCOM]] placed a production order for 1000 GBU-69/B units. 

Has been integrated into the [[AC-130J Ghostrider]], deployable from ramp-mounted [[Common Launch Tube]]s ([[Ramp Launch Tube]]s?) or dropped conventionally. Also integrated into platforms like [[MQ-1C Gray Eagle]] and [[MQ-9 Reaper|MQ-9A Reaper]].

Above: See the single-piece wing, which rotates into flight position after launch, and three (one obscured in picture) "lattice"-type flip-out tail fins. The weapon has a two-way datalink for inflight targeting updates. A notable feature is the cmoparatively large blast-fragmentation warhead, which contributes more than *half the weapon's total weight.*
