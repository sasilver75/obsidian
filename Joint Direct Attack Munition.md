First entered service in 1999 to solve a slew of problems the US encountered using (highly effective) laser-guided bombs in the first Gulf War, but laser designators were a lot less effective when viibility was obscured (clouds, dust, rain, etc.); we wanted higher precision when direct line of sight wasn't possible. They found it with their constellation of GPS satellites, and used the JDAM kit to upgrade existing dumb bombs to PGMs using GPS... without the need of a laser designator. Could be dropped 15 miles from target using air flow and the fins, together with guidance systems, to strike targets.

A "bolt-on" guidance package/kit that converts unguided gravity bombs ("dumb bombs") into all-weather Precision-Guided Munitions (PGMs).
- Guided by an integrated [[GPS INS]] system.
- JDAM-equipped bombs range from 500 to 2,000 pounds.
- When installed a bomb (often times a [[Mk 82]]/[[BLU-111]], [[Mk 83]]/[[BLU-110]], [[Mk 84]]/[[BLU-109]/[[BLU-137]]]), we call it a guided bomb unit ([[Joint Direct Attack Munition|GBU-38]], [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|GBU-32]], [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|GBU-31]](/B) respectively)!

# JDAM-ER (Extended Range) (GBU-62)

The [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|JDAM-ER]] (extended range) variant, a wing kit that triples the range of JDAM to 50 miles with the same accuracy, costing $10,000/unit to turn the typical JDAM PGM into a [[Glide Bomb]] with extended range.
![[Pasted image 20260713010004.png]]


JDAM is also used for anti-ship deployments; In 2014, the [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] performed the first-ever drop of a precision-guided aerial sea mine, consisting of a [[Quickstrike Naval Mine]] equipped with a JDAM kit; the mine detonates when a ship passes within lethal range. Historically, aerial mine-laying was dangerous, because the delivery aircraft had to fly low and slow, making it vulnerable to hostile file; with JDAM-guided mine placement, you can more accurately and more safely place mines in harbor mouths, shipping channels, canals, rivers, and inland waterways, reducing the number of mines required. # JDAM-LR (Long Range) (GBU-75)
- An additional jet-powered guidance kit slapped onto the -ER model that turns standard "dumb" bombs into affordable, long-range [[Turbojet]]-powered [[Cruise Missile]]s.

![[Pasted image 20260713180416.png]]

![[Pasted image 20260713180435.png]]

Variants of the JDAM-LR, each with a specific purpose:
- Decoy JDAM-LR
- Quickstrike JDAM-LR Shallow Water Mine
- Maritime iteration made for ship hunting

About more than just increasing the range of America's JDAMs: About increasing onboard power generation for new capabilities, like a two-way datalink, or advanced seekers to improve the weapon's accuracy, even against moving targets and in heavily jammed environments.

Graduating from stand-in munitions to full-fledged stand-off weapons. 

Allows for non-stealth fighters and heavy bombers like the [[B-52H Stratofortress]] to deploy them from outside enemy airspace without fear of being shot down.

Two JDAM-LRs launched from F/A-18 Super Hornets saw each weapon travel over 230 miles using the existing JDAM interface already present on the F/A-18... reducing the time/costs associated with integrating a new weapon across America's wide variety of tactical/strategic aircraft. This means the weapons can be fielded into operational service much faster than any clean sheet weapon could be.
- The JDAM-LR has a ==300 nm/350 mile== stated max range.

Typically carries the [[Mk 82]] 500lb general-purpose gravity bomb, using a single turbojet engine and a hardened [[GPS INS]] guidance system.

In the Decoy version, they swap the explosive payload for an extra fuel tank, giving a range of over ==700 nm/805 miles==.

The QuickStrike LR Shallow water Mine:
- Just an evolution of an earlier JDAM-based weapon called the Quickstrike ER, which is a 2,000 pound general purpose bomb fitted with underwater mine components like a new fuze, married up to the JDAM ER glide wing kit, resulting in a 2,000 pound undersea mine that can be dropped 40 miles from its intended area. Parachutes deploy to slow impact with the surface; it then sinks to the bottom of waterways and waits for its onboard Mk57 target detection device (fuze) to detect signatures of enemy vessels as they approach. They can differentiate between types of ships and count vessels as they pass by. Even with a few hundred feet of water above them, the detonation is enough to bring down huge warships.
- With the LR, they just swapped out the glide wings for a new [[Turbojet]] + Glide Wing kit, making it possible to mine waterways from 300+ miles away, dramatically reducing risk and really complicating matters for adversary planners.

The Maritime Strike variant
- It's likely also just a turbojet-equipped version of another existing weapon; this time, likely the [[QUICKSINK]], which builds on the JDAM with a radar seeker in the nose and an infrared seeker in a side ferring... enabling it to strike moving targets in bad weather conditions... this makes it into a potent ship-killer.
- With the addition of the JDAM LR glide wings and turbojet propulsion, this anti-ship bomb quickly becomes an anti-ship cruise missile.


The price per JDAM-LR hasn't been described yet... but may be ~$200,000, making them way cheaper than many long-range cruise missiles in service today like the [[AGM-158 JASSM]] line, and a similar price point to new "cheap" missiles like the [[Common Multi-Mission Truck|CMMT]], [[Anduril Barracuda|Barracuda-500]], while delivering much larger (5-10x) payloads.
- Note that this price is about ~5x the price of a standard JDAM
