Not a stand-alone weapon system; a "bolt-on" guidance package that converts unguided gravity bombs into Precision-Guided Munitions (PGMs).

![[Pasted image 20260713010628.png]]

![[Pasted image 20260713010849.png]]




The [[Joint Direct Attack Munition|JDAM-ER]] (extended range) variant, a wing kit that triples the range of JDAM to 50 miles with the esame accuracy, costing $10,000/unit.
![[Pasted image 20260713010004.png]]


JDAM is also used for anti-ship deployments; In 2014, the [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] performed the first-ever drop of a precision-guided aerial sea mine, consisting of a [[Quickstrike Naval Mine]] equipped with a JDAM kit; the mine detonates when a ship passes within lethal range. HIstorically, aerial mine-laying was dangerous, because the delivery aircraft had to fly low and slow, making it vulnerable to hostile file; with JDAM-guided mine placement, you can more accurately and more safely place mines in harbor mouths, shipping channels, canals, rivers, and inland waterways, reducing the number of mines required.

![[Pasted image 20260713010532.png]]

A direct-attack bomb version called "Quicksink" was tested in 2022, and was successfully used to sink multiple ships during the 2024 [[Exercise Rim of the Pacific|RIMPAC]] exercise.
