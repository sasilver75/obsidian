A [[United States Navy|Navy]] group of amphibious ships that carries/supports/deploys as Marine force, usually a [[Marine Expeditionary Unit]]. Together, the package is called an ARG/MEU.

Usually has three amphibious ships:
- Amphibious Assault Ship (Aviation-centered flagship; carries helicopters, MV-22s, F-35Bs, AV-8Bs, Marines ,command staff)
- Amphibious Transport Dock: Carries Marines, vehicles, landing craft, aircraft, command/logistics support
- Dock Landing Ship: Carries landing craft, vehicles, cargo, amphibious equipment

Q: Is it really just three ships?
A: Typically, yes. These three ships carry and support an MEU, but these are large, multi-role ships carrying ~2000+ Marines and sailors, many vehicles, C2 equipment, weapons, fuel, sustainment.
