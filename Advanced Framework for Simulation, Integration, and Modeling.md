A U.S. government-owned, open-source military simulation framework used for multi-domain operations analysis, system acquisition, and virtual wargaming. Managed by the Aerospace Systems Directorate of [[Air Force Research Lab|AFRL]].

> AFSIM's real value lies in its modular and extensible architecture enabling develops to write custom plug-ins or adaptors in C++. The degree of flexibility and control over simulation inputs and outputs has helped AFSIM emerge as a true "polyglot" in the defense ecosystem.

> AFRL shares AFSIM with industry partners through an Information Transfer Agreement (ITA), making it the ideal tool to support internal research and development activities.


Other notable simulations:
- SAFE-SiM
- STORM (Synthetic Theater Operations Research Model)
- JCATS (Joint Conflict and Tactical Simulation)
- OneSAF (One Semi-Automated Forces)
- ACES (Advanced Center for Experimentation and Simulation)
