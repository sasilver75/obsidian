Enables the application of information power at the strategic level as a [[Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff]]-controlled activity under the supervision of the JS Director for Operations.

This center supports global integration for the Information joint function and leads Joint Information Force development to improve the DoW's ability to gain/maintain/exploit information advantages cross the competition continuum. 

Planners should coordinate with the JIWC to enable the application of informational power and facilitate information environment related intelilgence that can be tailored for integration into [[Target Systems Analysis|TSA]] and [[Electronic Target Folder]]s.
