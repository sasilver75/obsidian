LRSO = "Long-Range Standoff"

A ==Nuclear==-armed, stealth air-launched [[Cruise Missile]] developed by [[Raytheon]] for the U.S. Air Force, designed to replace the aging [[AGM-86 ALCM]]; it will be integrated into the [[B-52H Stratofortress]] and the new [[B-21 Raider]].

Designed to carry the W80-4 thermonuclear warhead over distances exceeding 1,500 miles, it will arm the [[B-52H Stratofortress]] and the upcoming [[B-21 Raider]].

==Not yet in service==; Full-scale projection is supposed to begin around 2027, while the [[Initial Operating Capability]] is projected to be 2030.
