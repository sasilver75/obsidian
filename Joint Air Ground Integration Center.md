A collaborative, joint operations staff cell, typically seated in a [[United States Army|Army]] division or corps command post, which collates airspace controllers, fires, targeting, intelligence, and air defense personnel to enable rapid, real-time coordination of air and land operations.

Typically located within a [[Division]]'s [[Current Operations Integration Cell]] (COIC).
