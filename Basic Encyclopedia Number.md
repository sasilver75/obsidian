A unique 10-character alphanumeric identifier used by military and intelligence agencies to catalog fixed, physical installations and potential targets worldwide, such as airfields, bridges, and factories.

Rooted in Cold War-era "Bombing Encyclopedia" compiled by the US [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] and [[Defense Intelligence Agency]].

Format: Typically combines:
- A [[World Aeronautical Chart]] (WAC) locator
	- A regional index map or grid system used to identify, index, and locate specific sheets within the standard World Aeronautical Chart series.
	- ((Interestingly. WACs are no longer produced or actively used for navigation since 2015 when discontinued by the FAA due to declining demand, budget pressures, and the shift toward digital moving maps. Still, the first four characters of the 10-character BE number uses the legacy 4-digit WAC region code as its primary prefix locator to designate the specific global map quadrant.))
- A specific installation number

Used in target intelligence packages, mapping coordinates, and operational planning to ensure precise identification of facilities.
