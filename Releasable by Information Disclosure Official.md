A [[Limited Dissemination Control]]



A permissive foreign disclosure and release marking used to indicate that the originator has authorized a Senior Foreign Disclosure and Release Authority (SFDRA) to make further sharing decisions for uncaveated intelligence material (intelligence with no restrictive dissemination controls) in accordance with existing procedures, guidelines, and implementation guidance. Note: Only agencies that are eligible to use RELIDO in the intelligence community (IC) classified information context may use this LDC on CUI. It is defined and applied in the same manner as in the IC context.
