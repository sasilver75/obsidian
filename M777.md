A highly-mobile, lightweight 155mm towed howitzer used by the [[United States Army|Army]], [[United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps]], and several international militaries.
Weighs under 10,000 lbs due to its titanium construction, so that it can be transported by helicopter or cargo aircraft and features a digital fire control system optimized for precision-guided munitions.
