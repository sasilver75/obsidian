Assists the commander in directing and controlling operations. The J-3 typically synchronizes and integrates joint operations via a [[Joint Operations Center]] (JOC) and may serve as the chair of the [[Joint Targeting Coordination Board]] (JTCB). 

The J-3 can also organize and serve as the chair for the [[Joint Fires Element]] (JFE) if the JFC approves this optional staff element. 

The air component [[A3 Officer|A-3]] staff should integrate into J-3 and JFE processes.
