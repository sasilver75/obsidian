A [[United States Department of Defense|DoD]] designation for "major systems" that fall below the thresholds of [[Major Defense Acquisition Program|ACAT I]].

These programs require an eventual total Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation (RDT&E) expenditure of more than $200 M, or procurement of more than $920 M.

ACAT II programs do not require DoD level oversight; instead, the [[Milestone Decision Authority]] (MDA) is typically the Component Acquisition Executive (CAE), making management and execution much more decentralized to the respective service branch (e.g. [[United States Army|Army]], [[United States Navy|Navy]], [[United States Air Force|Air Force]])
