A [[Vertical Launch System]] that is an evolution of the [[Mk 41 VLS]].
Unlike the Mk 41, the Mk 57 is designed to be installed on the ship *periphery*, rather than in centralized magazines. 
Developed by [[Raytheon]], provides backwards compatability with existing missiles while allowing new missiles with significantly increased propulsion and payloads.
The primary improvement is the exhaust gas management system that can accommodate new missile designs having up to 45% greater rocket motor mass flow rate than that of Mk41
