https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical_line_of_operation

A military term defining the directional orientation of a force in time and space relative to an enemy.

Has a geographical focus.
- Interior lines are operations inside a ring or position
- Exterior lines involve operating on the outside of an enemy position, which can encircle the adversary but requires longer movement arcs.

Seems to be a term that's somewhat gone by the wayside.
