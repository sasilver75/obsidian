The composite of conditions, circumstances, and influences that affect the employment of capabilities and dictate a commander's decisions.

It encompasses not just the physical domains (land, air, maritime, space, cyberspace), but also the human, informational, and temporal dimensions of a battlespace.


# PMESII-PT
- Political
- Military
- Economic
- Social
- Information
- Infrastructure
- Physical Environment
- Time

By analyzing these variables, the military mitigates risk and can formulate mission [[Course of Action|COA]]s.
