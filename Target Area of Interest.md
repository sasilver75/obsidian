A specific geographic area where a commander can directly influence the battle by destroying, delaying, or disrupting the enemy. 

This is where the actual attack or engagement takes place.

See also: [[Named Area of Interest]]


TAIs cannot exist outside of an [[Named Area of Interest|NAI]] as they are both inextricably linked to the commander's priority information requirements and decision points.

> "Boxes on the ground where we want to engage targets because it is favorable to us. That box should be in some place where... our tank battalion is canalized. A TAI should have a time of activation and deactivation that matches appropriately when an [[Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance|ISR]] asset tasked with target acquisition"


> *The geographic area where [[High Value Target]]s can be aquired and engaged by friendly forces.*
