The Joint Strike Missile is a multi-role air-launched [[Cruise Missile]] jointly developed by Norway's [[Kongsberg]] and America's [[Raytheon]]; it is derived from the [[Naval Strike Missile]] (basically an air-launched version with some changes).

Serial production began in 2021.

This missile will be integrated with the [[F-35 Lightning II]]; studies have shown that the F-35 should be able to carry two of these in its internal bays, while four additional missiles could be carrier externally.


Compared to the Naval Strike Missile, the Joint Strike Missile features:
-  A larger warhead
- A form factor that lets it fit into the F-35's internal bays
- The ability to attack sea and land targets
- Compatibility with various aircraft as launch platforms
- Improved range: Estimates include 100 nautical miles low-low, or more than 300 nautical miles in a hi-low flight profile.
