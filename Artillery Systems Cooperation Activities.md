It is a secure multinational software interface and encrypted communication protocol allowing [[North Atlantic Treaty Organization|NATO]] and allied artillery fire control systems to automatically "speak" to one another.
- Allows a [[Forward Observer]] from one country to instantly pass targeting data to a firing unit (e.g. howitzer or rocket system) from another country, dramatically shortening response times.


A German [[Forward Observer]] can call in fire missions to a French fire direction control center, who can then farm it out to a US battery.
