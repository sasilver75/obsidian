A modified version of the Navy's shipborne close-in weapon system ([[Phalanx CIWS|CIWS]]).

Often used by the [[United States Army|Army]] for Counter-Rocket, Artillery, and Mortar (C-RAM) missions; acts as a last line of defense by detecting and destroying incoming projectiles in mid-air.
