Communication where both sides can transmit and receive at the same time.

Mental model:
> Telephone call. Both people can talk at once, even if conversational norms discourage it.

Mechanically, full-duplex requires some way to prevent each side's own transmission from blocking its reception. Common approaches include:
- Separate physical paths (one wire for transmit, another for receive)
- Separate frequencies (one frequency range for uplink, another for downlink)
- Echo cancellation (subtracting a devices own transmitted signal from what the device receives)

Examples:
- Modern switched Ethernet links
- Telephone calls
- Many fiber-optic network links.
