

Ukrainian Ground-Launched Cruise Missile

2540lb warhead with 1,900 mile range.
"It's a big missile"
- Flamingo is 6 tons, compared to a [[BGM-109 Tomahawk Land Attack Missile|Tomahawk]]'s 1.6 tons.
- With its booster, a Tomahawk is 6m long, a Flamingo si more like 12.
- Tomahawk 2.7m vs 6m wingspan Flamingo

Q: Why is it called the Flamingo?
A: Some of the original missiles came out with a manufacturing defect, where they were partially pink.
