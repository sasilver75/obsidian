Pronounced: "Jay-Ock"

The [[Joint Force Air Component Commander|JFACC]] can usually be found here.

When it's time to start operations, the JAOC sends out the [[Air Tasking Order]] to subordinate units known as [[Air Expeditionary Wing]]s (AEWs), who are people on loan from the service chiefs at a deployed location; it's the wing commander's staff at each of these bases that receives the ATO. Since they don't want to hand out the *entire* ATO to each aircrew, they break it apart into more manageable chunks at the Wing in a process known as "Shredding"; each of these chunks is called an [[ATO Fragment]] ("Frag"), and contains just the information for a specific flight to know when/where they need to be. This is done securely online, these days.
