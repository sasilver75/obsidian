An autonomous weapon station (M2, M320. M134, M240) to handle the full kill chain, up to [[Group 3 UAS|Group 3]] UAS.
- "The only autonomous weapon station on the market, exclusively available to the United States and its partners."


On July 20, 2026, the USMC [selected](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/07/marine-corps-selects-new-robotic-weapon-system-for-l-madis-integration/) Bullfrog for the [[Marine Air Defense Integrated System|L-MADIS]] integration (Light Marine Air Defense Integrated System), part of the service's Ground Based Air Defense (GBAD) program... through a prototype [[Other Transaction Authority|Other Transaction Agreement]] (OTA) contract. The contract’s initial value with the Marine Corps is roughly $6.2 million, according to the firm. The service is requesting approximately $1.27 billion in its fiscal 2027 budget request for GBAD capabilities, according to budget documents.
