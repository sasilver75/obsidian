An autonomous, air-launched [[Electronic Warfare|EW]] countermeasure used by military aircraft by standard [[Flare]]/[[Chaff]] dispensers, emitting powerful radio-frequency signals that lure enemy [[Radar]]-guided missiles away from teh host aircraft.


Notable examples:
- Leonardo [[BriteCloud]]
- Elbit Systems Nano SPEAR


Unlike towed decoys like [[ANALE-55 Fiber-Optic Towed Decoy|AN/ALE-55 FOTD]], which remain physically connected to the aircraft, EADs are "fire and forget" devices, offering flexibility without requiring structural modifications to the aircraft.
