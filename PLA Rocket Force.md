The strategic and tactical missile force of the [[People's Liberation Army|PLA]], managing China's arsenal of land-based ballistic, hypersonic, cruise and cruise miless, both nuclear and conventional.

Comprises more than 120,000 personnel and six ballistic missile "bases", plus 3 support bases in charge of storage/engineering/training, respectively.

These six operational Bases are independently deployed in five theaters throughout China, and each controls a number of brigades.

> "According to United States Department of Defense estimates, this includes 400 ground-launched cruise missiles, 900 conventionally armed short-range ballistic missiles, 1,300 conventional medium-range ballistic missiles, 500 conventional intermediate-range ballistic missiles, as well as 400 intercontinental ballistic missiles."
