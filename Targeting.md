> Targeting is the process of selecting and prioritizing targets and matching the appropriate response to them, considering operational requirements and capabilities.

> The Joint Targeting Process categorizes adversary threat elements into Target Systems, Target System Components, and Target Elements

> A target is an entity (person, place, thing) considered for possible engagement or action to alter or neutralize the function it performs for the adversary. The target's operational importance is determined by assessing whether engaging the target is consistent with planned operations and will help achieve the commander's objectives and end state. Standard types of targets are facility, individual, virtual, equipment, or organization.
