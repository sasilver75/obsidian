Pron. Nye-Wick

The [[United States Navy|Navy]]s' technical authority and acquisition commander for [[Command, Control, Communications, Computers, Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance|C4ISR]], business information technology, and space systems.

The command was formerly known as Space and Naval Warfare Systems Command (SPAWAR) and was named in June 2019 to better align its identity with its mission.

Has two... locations:
- Naval Information Warfare Center (NWIC) Pacific (NWIC PAC)
- Naval Information Warfare Center (NWIC) Atlantic (NWIC LANT)
