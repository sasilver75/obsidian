Classified intelligence subject to special handling (e.g. in a [[Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility|SCIF]]) and access controls within defined compartments. It's not a classification level (but a control mechanism layered on top secret); it's usually associated with [[Top Secret Classification|Top Secret]] information, though not always.


Covers intelligence sources and collection methods.
Each compartment must be granted separately.
Compartments have codewords.
SCI material can only be read within a [[Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility|SCIF]]


See also: [[Special Access Program]] (SAP)
