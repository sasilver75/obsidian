A [[Major Command]] (MAJCOM) of the [[United States Air Force|USAF]].
Conducts, development, testing, and evaluation, and provides the acquisition and lifecycle management services and logistics support.


> Manages installation and mission support, discovery and development, test and evaluation, and life cycle management services and sustainment for every major USAF weapon system. AFMC also provides effective nuclear materiel management (NMM) support to the warfighter by managing human capital, organizations, processes, and procedures used to conduct, execute, and support nuclear deterrence. NMM responsibilities span all AFMC and HQ organizations and focus on providing agile cradle-to-grave support to the warfighter.
