ESSM = "Evolved SeaSparrow Missile"

A medium-range, ship-launched surface-to-air interceptor manufactured by [[Raytheon]] and NATO, designed for fleet defense against maneuverable anti-ship cruise missiles, low-altitude aircraft, and high-speed surface targets.

One launch cell does not always equal one missile. Four RIM-162 ESSM can be quad-packed into one compatible [[Mk 41 VLS]] cell.
- A [[Ticonderoga-Class Cruiser]] has 122 Mk 41 cells, while an [[Arleigh Burke-Class Destroyer]] has either 90 or 96 cells, depending on the flight.
