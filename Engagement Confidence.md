In contrast to [[Track Quality]], ==[[Engagement Confidence]]== is the scalar that a targeting cell actually wants, the answer to the question of whether the track is real, the identification is right, the weapon can reach, the [[Rules of Engagement|ROE]] permits, and the kill probability is worth the round. 
- There isn't a [[United States Military Standard|MIL-STD]] or [[Standardization Agreement|STANAG]] for this, so [[Aegis Combat System|Aegis]] calls it one thing, [[Advanced Field Artillery Tactical Data System|AFATDS]] calls it another, [[F-35 Lightning II]] mission systems compute it internally and don't publish the formula, and modern AI-assisted targeting stacks each roll their own "targeting confidence score" that isn't cross-comparable with any other vendor's.
- It's not likely that this gap is going to be closed by a standards body in the next five years.
