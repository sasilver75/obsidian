A [[Hypersonic Weapon|Hypersonic]] missile from [[Lockheed Martin]] specifically designed to fit in the internal weapons bay of the [[F-35 Lightning II|F-35A]]/[[F-35 Lightning II|F-35C]] and the [[F-22 Raptor|F-22A]].

==Not yet in service==
Missile was unveiled in 2024, with Lockheed pitching it to both the [[United States Navy|Navy]] and [[United States Air Force|Air Force]]; it's also been considered for deployment on submarines and surface warships.

Development work began in 2017, originally for the AF's Stand-in Attack Weapon (SiAW) program, reflecting a shift towards ==more affordable, yet effective hypersonic strike weapons.==
- Unlike larger hypersonic [[Cruise Missile]]s, the Mako offers a degree of standoff range and rapid response capabilities.
