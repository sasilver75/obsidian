In Service 2023-Present with the [[United States Army|Army]]
[[United States Navy|Navy]] intends to field the weapon aboard its [[Zumwalt-Class Destroyer]]s by 2025, and later on its [[Virginia-Class Attack Submarine]]s in 2028.

A large two-stage rocket booster carrying an unpowered [[Hypersonic Glide Vehicle]]  called the Common-Hypersonic Glide Body (C-HGB) in the nose cone. Once the booster reaches significant altitude and speed, it releases the C-HGB, which glides at hypersonic speeds towards its target.


On 29 April 2026, the US [[US Central Command|CENTCOM]] requested that the Dark Eagle be sent to the Middle East for potential deployment against Iran during the 2026 Iran war, marking the first time Washington would deploy the technology, according to Bloomberg.
