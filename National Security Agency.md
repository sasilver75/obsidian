A combat support agency employing mathematicians, linguists, engineers, and computer scientists focusing on [[Signals Intelligence|SIGINT]] and information assurance. NSA’s employees collect, process, analyze, and exploit adversaries’ communications to better understand adversary capabilities, actions, and intent. NSA maintains its HQ at Fort Meade, Maryland, and has four cryptologic centers, each with a regional focus.
