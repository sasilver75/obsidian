The centralized, 24/7 [[Command and Control]] hub where information from the battlefield or emergency theater is processed, analyzed, and coordinated to direct troop movements and manage ongoing operations.

Functions as the "brain" of military operations by integrating multiple branches, agencies, and specialties into one synchronized space.

Handles:
- Real-Time Monitoring
- Cross-Agency Collaboration
- Disaster and Crisis Response
- Information Ecosystem
